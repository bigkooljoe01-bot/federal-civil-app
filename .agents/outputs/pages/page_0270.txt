90. What document outlines the responsibilities of an Accounting Officer in a self-accounting
unit?
A. The organization's internal memo.
B. The Financial Regulations book, Chapter.
C. The annual budget document.
D. The HR policy manual.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Chapter 16 of the Financial Regulations book is dedicated to "Self Accounting
Status” and outlines these responsibilities.
91. What action is crucial for newly established revenue collecting offices regarding notification?
A. They must notify the local police.
B. They must notify the appropriate Sub Accounting Officer and the Auditor-General.
C. They must notify the general public.
D. They must notify their families.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 217 (from a related section but relevant to accounting officer
duties) states Accounting Officers will notify the appropriate Sub Accounting Officer and the
Auditor-General of the establishment of any new revenue collecting office
92. Who primarily bears the responsibility for any discrepancies in financial records within a
self-accounting unit?
A. The junior staff.
B. The Accountant-General.
C. The Accounting Officer.
D. The external auditor.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: The Accounting Officer is charged with ensuring strict observance of regulations
and proper accounting, implying ultimate responsibility for discrepancies
93. What is the frequency for producing accounts to the Accounting Officer to show cumulative
yearly totals of expenditure and revenue?
A. Weekly.
B. Monthly.
C. Annually.
D. Bi-annually.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1603(f) states that monthly accounts are produced to the
Accounting Officer to show cumulative yearly totals.
