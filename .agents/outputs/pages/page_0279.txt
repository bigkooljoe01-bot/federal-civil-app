26. Who must be present during the physical count and verification of cash, stamps, and other
security documents by the Board of Survey?
A. The Accountant-General.
B. The officer-in-charge of the items being surveyed.
C. An external auditor.
D. The Minister of Finance.
Correct Answer: B

or other security documents must be present throughout the physical count and verification.
27. What must the Board do with any surplus or shortage of cash discovered during the survey?
A. Keep the surplus and ignore the shortage.
B. Report the matter immediately to the Accounting Officer for prompt investigation.
C. Adjust the books to reflect the new balance.
D. Donate the surplus to charity.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1807 mandates that any surplus or shortage must be
reported immediately to the Accounting Officer for prompt investigation.

A. After the survey is completed.
