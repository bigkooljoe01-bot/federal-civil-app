37. Who has the authority to inspect the accounts of imprest Holders?
A. Only the Accounting Officer.
B. The Accountant-General and the Auditor-General.
C. The Head of Department only.
D. The Minister of Finance only.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1013 states: “The accounts of Imprest Holders are subject to
inspection by the Accountant-General and the Auditor-General."
38. What is the maximum reimbursable limit for a Standing Imprest held by a Permanent
Secretary or Director-General?
A. N100,000.
B. N200,000.
C. N300,000.
D. N60,000.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1014(i) specifies: “Permanent Secretary and
Director-General. N 200,00."
39. What is the maximum reimbursable limit for a Standing Imprest held by a Head of Formation
in each state and any other Imprest Holder?
A. N100,000.
B. N200,000.
C. N300,000.
D. N60,000.
Correct Answer: D.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1014(i) specifies: "Head of Formation in each state and any
Other Imprest Holder.. N 60,000."
