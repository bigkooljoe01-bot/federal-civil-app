Part 4: Financial Regulation: Self Accounting Status
71. What does Financial Regulation 1601 primarily define?
A, The responsibilities of an Auditor-General.
B. The role of an Accounting Officer in a self-accounting unit.
C. Procedures for revenue collection.
D Guidelines for capital expenditure.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1601 explicitly defines the "Role in a self-accounting unit" for
an Accounting Officer
72. Who approves the grant of self-accounting status to a Ministry, Extra-Ministerial Office, or
other arms of government?
A. The Auditor-General.
B. The Accountant-General.
C. The Head of Department.
D. The Minister of Finance.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1602 states that the Accountant-General's approval is
required for self-accounting status
73. What is one of the key responsibilities of the Accounting Officer in a self-accounting unit, as
per Financial Regulation 1603(a)?
A. To personally conduct all internal audits.
B. To ensure strict observance of existing financial rules and regulations.
C. To manage all personnel issues.
D. To approve all procurement contracts.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1603(a) mandates that the Accounting Officer shall ensure
strict observance of existing financial rules and regulations governing the receipt and
disbursement of public funds.
74. According to Financial Regulation 1603(b), who must the Accounting Officer issue
accounting instructions to?
