66. How is a memo different from a letter?
A. A memo is used for external communication, while a letter is for internal communication.
B. A memo is for internal communication, while a letter is for external communication.
C. A memo is always longer than a letter.
D. A memo is always shorter than a letter.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: A memorandum is a type of correspondence used to communicate internally
between officers within the same department or Ministry, while an official letter is used to
communicate with other Ministries and the public.
67. What is the first thing a schedule officer should do when preparing to write a
communication?
A. Call a meeting.
B. Look up the facts, figures, and data.
C. Write the draft immediately.
D. Ask a junior officer to do it.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: The officer must be careful with the facts, figures, and data in his writing. This
implies the need to verify them before drafting.
68. What is the purpose of “fairs in the context of government business?
A. To correct errors in a draft.
B. To provide a clean, final version of a document.
C. To file a document.
D. To send a document to another department.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Fairing refers to the process of creating a clean, final version of a document after
a draft has been prepared and approved.
