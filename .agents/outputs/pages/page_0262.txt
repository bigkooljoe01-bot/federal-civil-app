59. What is the purpose of the completed personal emolument form according to FR 15212
A. To determine an officer's promotion.
B. To serve as the basis for opening the group registers, which must be audited before an officer
is included in the payroll.
C. To calculate the officer's pension.
D. To justify the officer's annual leave.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1521(ii) states that the completed personal emolument form
shall be the basis for opening the group registers, which must be audited before any officer is
included in the payroll.
60. What must the salary section be informed of in writing at the earliest possible time?
A. The officer's personal interests.
B. Any variation that may affect the salary of any officer.
C. The officer's choice of bank.
D. The officer's travel plans.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1522(i) requires that the salary section must be informed in
writing of any variation that may affect an officer's salary at the earliest possible time.
61. Who should not be the officer directly in charge of preparation of the payroll, according to FR
1523(ii)?
A. The Head of Accounts.
B. An officer not below the rank of Senior Accountant.
C. The officer directly in charge of variation control.
D. The Accountant-General.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1523(ii) specifies that the officer directly in charge of
variation control must not be the officer directly in charge of preparation of the payroll and must
work independently.
