91. What is the rule regarding drawing unallocated stores for use?
A. They can be drawn at any time.
B. They should not be drawn before they are required to utilize provision in the Estimates which
would otherwise lapse.
C. They must be drawn by year-end.
D. They can be drawn to meet any unforeseen need.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 414 states: "Un allocated stores may not be drawn for use
before they are required in order to utilise provision in the Estimates which would otherwise
lapse at the end of the financial year."
92. Who is responsible for providing the Internal checking section, intemal audit unit, and
Paying Officer with specimen signatures of officers authorized to sign payment vouchers?
A. The Accountant-General.
B. The Minister of Finance.
C. The Accounting Officer.
D. The Head of Finance and Accounts.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 411 states: "The Accounting Officer shall furnish the Internal
checking section, intemal audit unit and the Paying Officer with the specimen signatures along
with the list of relevant votes chargeable.”
93. If a discrepancy is found when examining schedules of payments and adjustments from the
Accountant-General, what should be done?
A. It should be ignored if minor.
B. It should be immediately brought to the notice of the Accountant-General.
C. It should be corrected by the Accounting Officer.
D. It should be reported to the Auditor-General only.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 408 states: "Any discrepancy which such examinations may
disclose should immediately be brought to the notice of the Accountant-General."
