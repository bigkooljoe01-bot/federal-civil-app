111. What is the period of notice for relinquishing an appointment during a probationary period?
A. One month
B. Three months
C. A month's notice or payment of one month's salary in lieu of notice.
D. Two weeks
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Rule 020902 states that an officer may relinquish their appointment by giving "one
month's notice, or by paying one month's salary in lieu of notice."
112. An officer may be dismissed from service for what reason?
A. Resignation
B. Termination
C. Gross misconduct
D. Leaving the service
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Rule 020903 defines “termination” but does not explicitly mention “dismissal” in the
provided text. However, Rule 100407 defines dismissal and its effects. This question is a
knowledge gap in the provided text. Rule 020212 mentions that an act of serious misconduct
may include dismissal from the service. Rule 100401 defines gross misconduct. Therefore, the
most appropriate answer is "Gross misconduct.”
113. What is the procedure for an officer who wishes to resign from the service?
A. They must submit their resignation letter to the Head of the Civil Service of the Federation.
B. They must apply for a leave of absence.
C. They must give one month's notice of their intention to resign.
D. They must give three months’ notice.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Rule 020904 states that an officer who wishes to resign must give "a month's
notice.”
