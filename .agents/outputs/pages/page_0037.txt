115. What is the mandatory retirement age for all grades in the Service?
A. 50 years
B. 60 years or 35 years of pensionable service, whichever is earlier
C. 65 years.
D. 70 years
Correct Answer: B

Service is "60 years or 35 years of pensionable service, whichever is earlier.”
116. Which of the following is a ground for leaving the service?
A. Resignation
B. Abolition of Office
C. Failing a compulsory examination
D. All of the above
Correct Answer: D
Explanation: The rules in Section 9 cover all these topics as reasons for leaving the service®.
117. What is the tenure policy for a director or its equivalent?
A. They shall hold office for a term of eight years.
B. They shall retire at 60 years.
C. They shall retire after 35 years of service.
D. They have an indefinite tenure.
Correct Answer: A
Explanation: Rule 020909 states that a director or its equivalent shall hold office for “a term of
eight years and renewable for a further term of four years."
