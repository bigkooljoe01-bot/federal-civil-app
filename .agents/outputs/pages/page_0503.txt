40. What happens to papers routed upwards through official channels after a decision has been
reached?
A. They are discarded.
B. They are routed back in the same way to all intermediate officers.
C. They are kept only by the higher authority.
D. They are sent to a different department.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Papers routed upwards through official channels are routed back in the same
way, and all intermediate officers are, therefore, acquainted with decisions reached by the
higher authority.
41. What should intermediate officers do with papers routed through them for information only?
A. They should delay them unnecessarily.
B. They should not delay them unnecessarily.
C. They should take a copy for themselves.
D. They should file them immediately.
Correct Answer:B.
Explanation: Intermediate officers should not delay unnecessarily papers which are routed
through them for information only.
42. What is the purpose of the “Duplicate Note-Book System"?
A. To keep track of personal notes.
B. To record the file number, date, and destination whenever an officer sends out a file.
C. To create duplicate files.
D. To write meeting minutes.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Some officers still use the duplicate note-book system for recording transmission
of files whereby whenever an officer sends out a file, his Clerk records its number, date and
destination in a duplicate note-book.
43. What should a Clerk do with the top copy of their list from the duplicate note-book system at
the end of each day?
A. Keep it for themselves.
B. Give it to the officer in charge of the most appropriate Registry.
C. Discard it.
D. Send it to the Chairman.
Correct Answer: B.
