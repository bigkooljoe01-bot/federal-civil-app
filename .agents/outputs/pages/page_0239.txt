67. What is required for the destruction of condemned safes?
A. Casual disposal.
B. Destruction in the presence of a Board of Survey, with a certificate issued.
C. Recycling.
D. Selling them as scrap metal.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1126 states, "Condemned safes shall be destroyed in the
presence of a Board of Survey, and a certificate of destruction shall be issued.”
68. What is the rule regarding the custody of main stocks of postage stamps?
A. They can be kept anywhere.
B. They must be kept in the strong-room or reserve cash safe, and a register maintained.
C. They can be distributed freely.
D. They are not considered security items.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1127 states, "Main stocks of postage stamps shall be kept in
the strong-room or reserve cash safe, and a register shall be maintained for all such stamps
deposited.”
69. How should registered and unregistered mail containing public funds or security documents
be delivered?
A. By regular post.
B. By locked bag, with a register maintained.
C. By hand, without any special precautions.
D. By email.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1128 states, "Registered and unregistered mail containing
public funds or security documents shall be delivered by locked bag, and a register shall be
maintained for all such mail."
70. What is the responsibility of Sub-Accounting Officers regarding receipt and licence books?
A. To leave them unsecured.
B. To ensure their safe custody and proper use.
C. To dispose of them after use.
D. To print their own.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1130 states, "Sub-Accounting Officers shall be responsible
for the safe custody and proper use of all receipt and licence books issued to them.”
