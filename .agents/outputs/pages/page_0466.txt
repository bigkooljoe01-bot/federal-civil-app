86. What is the purpose of a "bring-up" pad?
A. To store files that are not needed.
B. To keep a list of all meetings.
C. To store a file that requires action at a future date.
D. To keep a list of all staff.
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: When an officer has finished with a file, he should ensure that it is returned to the
registry. If a file is required at a later date, it should be put on a "bring-up" pad.
87. What is the purpose of classifying documents?
A. To make them difficult to access.
B. To sensitize officers about the degree of care required for each document.
C. To hide their contents from other Ministries.
D. To prevent the public from ever seeing them.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Classified documents are duly classified so as to sensitize officers about the
degree of care required for each.
88. What is the lowest level of classification for an official document?
A. RESTRICTED.
B. CONFIDENTIAL.
C. SECRET.
D. TOP SECRET.
Correct Answer: A.
Explanation: The various classification ratings in descending order of security are TOP
SECRET, SECRET, CONFIDENTIAL, and RESTRICTED.
89. What is the best way to destroy classified documents that are no longer needed?
A. Tear them up and throw them in the waste bin.
B. Shred them or bum them.
C. Leave them on a desk for others to see.
D. Put them in the Open Registry.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Unwanted records must be physically destroyed either in a paper shredding
machine or burnt.
