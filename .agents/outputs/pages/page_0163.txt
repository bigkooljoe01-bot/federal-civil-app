75. What is the rule regarding deductions from revenue collections to adjust a previous
over-credit?
A. Itis allowed with the Accounting Officer's approval.
B. Itis strictly forbidden.
C. It can be done if the amount is small.
D. Itis allowed as long as the Head of Accounts is notified.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 223 states that no deductions shall be made from any
revenue collections to adjust a previous over-credit. The gross amount must always be
accounted for in full.
76. What is the rule for Accounting Officers regarding the return of arrears of revenue?
A. They must include only the arrears in respect of the headquarters.
B. They must include all arrears for which they are responsible to collect.
C. They should only return arrears that are substantial.
D. They are not responsible for retuming arrears.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 230(i) states that arrears of revenue returns must include all
arrears for which the Accounting Officer is responsible.
77. What is required for the abandonment of claims in the case of irrecoverable arrears of
revenue?
A. The approval of the Accounting Officer.
B. The approval of the Head of Finance and Accounts.
C. The authority of the Minister of Finance.
D. The authority of the Auditor-General.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 231i) states that the authority of the Minister of Finance is
required for any abandonment of claims, except where other authority is established by law.
