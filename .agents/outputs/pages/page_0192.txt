87. What happens if an officer neglects to press for claims from private parties, causing
prejudice to the private party?
A. The private party is solely responsible.
B. The officer who neglected to press for claims shall be held responsible.
C. The Accountant-General wil intervene.
D. The claim will be written off.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 412(i) states: "Neglect to do this will not prejudice the private
party but the Officer who has neglected to press for claims shall be held responsible.”
88. What is the purpose of stamping vouchers "ENTERED IN VOTE BOOK"?
A. To indicate approval for payment.
B. To confirm that the expenditure has been recorded as a commitment or payment in the vote
book.
C. To show that the voucher has been audited.
D. To mark it for archiving.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 407 implies this purpose, ensuring that financial
commitments and payments are properly tracked against the allocated budget.
89. If a payment is made on account for a contract, what is the money claimed limited to?
A. The full contract sum.
B. The cost of the work certified to have been performed.
C. An arbitrary percentage of the contract.
D. The amount available in the vote.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 412 states: “In the case o. payment on account, no money
will be claimed other than the cost of the work certified to have been performed.”
