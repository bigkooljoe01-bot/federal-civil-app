44. When cash has to be transported from one place to another (e.g., for salary payments),
what must the responsible officer be provided with?
A. A personal briefcase.
B. An approved cash tank or box, government vehicle, and police escort.
C. A public transport ticket.
D. A receipt for the cash.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1104 states that the officer "must be provided with an
approved cash tank or box, government vehicle and police escort.”
45. What periodic examination is required for strong-rooms?
A. To check for dust accumulation.
B. To ensure no vents or openings exist through which contents could be extracted.
C. To verify the aesthetic appeal.
D. To count the number of items stored.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1105 requires strong-rooms to be "examined periodically to
ensure that no vents or openings exist through which any of the contents could be extracted
either by hand or by other means."
46. Who is responsible for ensuring that departmental officers who hold public money, stamps,
elc., are provided with proper safe custody facilities?
A. The Accountant-General.
B. The Auditor-General.
C. Accounting Officers.
D. The Head of Department.
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1106(i) states, "It is the duty of Accounting Officers to ensure
that their departmental officers who are required to hold public money, stamps, etc., are
provided with proper safe custody facilities.”
47. Before procuring safes, strong-rooms, cash tanks, and boxes, whose specification must first
be obtained?
A. The Ministry of Finance.
B. The Accountant-General.
C. The Head of Civil Service.
D. The Auditor-General.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1106(ii) requires that “their specification must first be
obtained from the Accountant-General before procurement.”
