27. What should a civil servant do with a circular that requires action to be taken?
A. Discard it.
B. Place a copy in the relevant file and pass it to the appropriate officer for action
C. Read it and do nothing.
D. Rewrite the circular.
Correct Answer:B.
Explanation: The file copy of each such circular should be placed in the file relevant to the
subject matter and should be passed to the appropriate officer for action to be taken.
28. What is the purpose of the "Official Secret Act"?
A. To encourage civil servants to share all government information.
B. To prevent the leaking of government secrets to unauthorized persons.
C. To protect civil servants from prosecution.
D. To allow for the free flow of information to the public.
Correct Answer:B.
Explanation: The Official Secret Act is to prevent the leaking of goverment secrets to
unauthorized persons.
29. What is considered a very serious offense for a Civil Servant?
A. Taking a day off.
B. Unauthorized disclosure of any information acquired in the course of their duties.
C. Arriving late to a meeting.
D. Talking to a colleague.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Unauthorized disclosure of any information acquired in the course of their duties
is a very serious offence.
30. All Civil Servants are required to take what?
A. An Oath of Office.
B. An Oath of Secrecy.
C. A pledge of allegiance.
D. A vow of poverty.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: All Civil Servants are required to take an Oath of Secrecy.
