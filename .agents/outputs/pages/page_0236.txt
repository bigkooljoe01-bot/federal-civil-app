56. How often should annual returns of all Government strong-rooms and safes be rendered,
and to whom?
A. Quarterly, to the Minister of Finance.
B. Annually on March 31st, to the Accountant-General.

Explanation: Financial Regulation 1115 requires "Annual retums in duplicate, of all Government
strong-rooms and safes will be rendered on the 31st March by all ministries and extra-ministerial
offices and other arms of government to the Accountant-General.”

58. When locks to a strong-room or safe are changed, or a safe is condemned and destroyed,

A. Keep the old keys as souvenirs.
