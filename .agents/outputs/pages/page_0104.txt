63. What is the rule regarding the prohibition of private practice?

A. Officers are encouraged to engage in private practice.

B. No Officer shall engage in private practice, except as specified.

C. Officers can engage in private practice if it does not interfere with official duties.

D. Officers can engage in private practice if they have prior approval.

64. What is the rule regarding material discoveries?

A. Officers are encouraged to keep discoveries secret.

B. Officers are prohibited from concealing any valuable discoveries.

C. Officers can sell their discoveries for personal gain.

D. Officers can patent their discoveries without informing the Government.

65. What is the rule regarding seeking influence from prominent persons?

A. Officers are encouraged to seek influence for promotion.

B. No Officer shall seek the influence of prominent persons or any other person for ensuring due

consideration of his claims in matters connected with discipline or with a view to obtaining

consideration for appointment, transfer, promotion or postings.

C. Officers can seek influence if it is for official purposes.

D. Officers can seek influence if it benefits the Government.

66. What is the rule regarding posting and residence?

A. Officers can choose their posting location.

B. Every Officer shall discharge any duties assigned to him by the Government and accept the

liability of being stationed wherever his presence is considered to be most useful.

C. Officers can refuse posting if it is inconvenient.

D. Officers can request specific posting locations.

67. What is the rule regarding paid appointments during leave?

A. Officers can take up any paid appointment during leave.

B. An Officer on leave of absence shall not accept any paid employment without previously

obtaining the express approval of the Office of the Head of the Civil Service of the Federation

(OHCSF).

C. Officers can take up paid employment if it is part-time.

D. Officers can take up paid employment if it is in another country.

68. What is the rule regarding private agency?

A. Officers can undertake any private agency.

B. No Officer shall undertake any private agency in any matter connected with the exercise of

his public duties.

C. Officers can undertake private agency with prior approval.

D. Officers can undertake private agency if it is for charity.
