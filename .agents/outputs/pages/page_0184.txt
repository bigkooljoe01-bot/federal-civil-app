55. What must a departmental vote book clearly show in respect of each sub-head?
A. The amount sanctioned in the Estimates.
B. Any additional provision by supplementary warrants.
C. Any reduction of the provision by virement.
D. All of the above.
Correct Answer: D
Explanation: Financial Regulation 402(i)(a) lists all these as details to be shown in the vote
book.
56. How should any reduction of provision in a vote book by virement be indicated?
A. In black ink.
B. In red ink.
C. With a special stamp.
D. By an asterisk.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 402(i)(a)(iii) specifies: “Any such reduction should be shown
in red ink."
57. What should be entered in the vote book in addition to expenditure incurred?
A. Only liabilities.
B. Only commitments.
C. Both liabilities and commitments as soon as they are known or incurred.
D. Only payments made.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 403 states: "Similarly all liabilities and commitments will be
entered as-soon as they are known or incurred.”
