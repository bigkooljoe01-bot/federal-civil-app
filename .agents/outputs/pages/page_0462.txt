69. What is the danger of a document being destroyed, especially with older methods of record
management?
A. It can be easily recreated.
B. It can never be recalled and has to be recreated.
C. It will automatically reappear in a "Recycle Bin."
D. It can be recalled with a simple command.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Pre-computer, unwanted records must be physically destroyed either in a paper
shredding machine or bunt, and once a document is destroyed, it can never be recalled. It can
only be recreated again.
70. According to the handbook, what is a key asset for a schedule officer?
A. A large office.
B. Accurate information based on facts and figures.
C. A high salary.
D. A friendly boss.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: An important asset to most scheduled officers is accurate information based on
facts and figures.
71. What is the danger of using obsolete and incorrect information?
A. It can lead to a promotion.
B. It can help an officer make sound decisions.
C. It can undermine an officer's maturity and sound judgment.
D. It has no effect on the decision-making process.
Correct Answer:C.
Explanation: Obsolete and incorrect information do not help officers to make sound decisions
and can easily undermine factors such as maturity and sound judgment.

this?
A. ltis a sign of laziness.
B. It can get lost or misplaced.
C. It can be read by unauthorized persons.
D.BothBand C.
Correct Answer: D.
Explanation: Documents and files must not be left lying on desks where they could get lost or
