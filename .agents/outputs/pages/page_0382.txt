46. Who heads the Judiciary at the Federal level?
A. The President of the Senate
B. The Speaker of the House of Representatives
C. The Chief Justice of Nigeria
D. The President, Commander-in-Chief
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: The Judiciary is headed by the Chief Justice of Nigeria
47. What is the primary duty of the Executive arm of Goverment regarding policies and
programs?
A. Adjudication
B. Law making
C. Conceives, formulates, executes and monitors
D. Representation
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: The Executive is responsible for the day-to-day management of Government. In
this context, it conceives, formulates, executes and monitors the policies, programmes and
projects of the Government.
48. Which list in the Constitution contains matters where both Federal and State Governments
share responsibilities?
A. Exclusive List
B. Residual Powers
C. Concurrent List
D. Shared List
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: These areas which appear in the Concurrent List in the Constitution, include
Health, Education, Roads etc
