48. Who is responsible for keeping all duplicate keys to strong-rooms, safes, or departmental
cash tanks and boxes?
A. The cashier.
B. The Accounting Officer.
C. The Head of Department.
D. The Minister of Finance.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1107(i) states, "All duplicate keys shall be kept by the
Accounting Officer.”
49. If a safe is contained within a strong-room, what is the rule regarding key-holding?
A. One officer can hold all keys.
B. One officer should not hold the keys of both strong-room and safe.
C. The cashier must hold both keys.
D. Keys can be left unattended.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1108 stipulates, "Where a safe is contained in a
strong-room, one officer should not hold the keys of both strong-room and safe.”
50. What action must be taken if duplicate keys of safes are not available or untraceable?
A. Ignore the issue.
B. Report to the Accounting Officer and request that the locks be altered and new keys
provided.
C. Use a master key.
D. Replace the safe immediately without reporting.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1109 requires reporting to the Accounting Officer and
requesting “that the locks be altered and new keys provided."
51. Who is personally responsible for the safe custody of original keys of strong-rooms, safes,
cash tanks, or cash boxes, and collectively responsible for their contents?
A. Only the cashier.
B. Officers holding the keys.
C. The Accounting Officer only.
D. The security guard.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1110 states, "Officers holding the keys of strong-rooms,
safes, cash tanks or cash boxes are personally responsible for their safe custody and
collectively responsible for the contents.”
