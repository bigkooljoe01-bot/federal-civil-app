51. What two documents are required to be issued when an officer is transferred from one
ministry to another?
A. A Last Pay Certificate and a certified true copy of the Personal Emolument Records.
B. A letter of recommendation and a transfer letter.
C. An updated curriculum vitae and a new contract.
D. A bank statement and a new Treasury Form 14.
Correct Answer: A
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1514(i) requires that a Last Pay Certificate along with a
certified true copy of the Personal Emolument Records shall be issued when an officer is
transferred.
52. What information must the Last Pay Certificate and Personal Emolument Record show?
A. The officer's next of kin.
B. The officer's work history.
C. The last pay date, details of deductions, and the balance recoverable.
D. The officer's new salary.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: According to Financial Regulation 1514(ii), the last pay certificate and personal
emolument record must show the last pay date, full details of deductions, and the balance
recoverable.
53. What must a paying officer do with the total of each type of deduction from emoluments?
A. Transfer it to a suspense account.
B. Enter it as a receipt in his cash book.
C. Ignore it for the final payment calculation.
D. Retum it to the officer.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1515 states that the paying officer shall enter the total of
each type of deduction from emoluments as a receipt in his cash book.
