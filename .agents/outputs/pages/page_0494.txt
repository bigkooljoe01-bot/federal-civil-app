1. What is considered a key ingredient of good govemance, according to Chapter 13?
A. Financial stability.
B. Communication to the various stakeholders as the occasion demands.
C. Political power.
D. A large bureaucracy.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: A key ingredient of good govemance is communication to the various
stakeholders as the occasion demands.
2. Government communication is described as having its own what?
A. Language, culture, and practices.
B. Culture, methods, and practices.
C. Rules, regulations, and laws.
D. Staff, policies, and procedures.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Government is a very large organization with its own culture, methods and
practices.
3. Who should an officer not communicate with on official business, outside of the proper
channel?
A. A junior officer.
B. A more senior officer.
C. The Head of Department.
D. A non-official or non-Govemment person.
Correct Answer: D.
Explanation: The golden rule is that an officer must not communicate on official business with a
non-official or non-Government person unless the officer is specifically directed to do so by his
superior officer.
4. What is the purpose of a "handing over note"?
A. To record the personal assets of an officer.
B. To provide a detailed and comprehensive document to a successor, outlining the duties and
responsibilities of the post.
C. To provide a list of all official meetings.
D. To document all the money spent by an officer.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: It is essential that an officer who is about to proceed on leave, transfer, or retire
should prepare a detailed and comprehensive handing over note for the guidance of his
successor.
