1. What is the most fundamental demand on an officer chairing a meeting?
A. To ensure everyone is paid for their attendance.
B. To understand the purpose of the gathering.
C. To be the most senior officer in the room.
D. To take notes and prepare the minutes.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: The most fundamental demand on the Chairman of a meeting is to understand
the purpose of the gathering.
2. According to the handbook, when the purpose of a meeting is complex, what should be
done?
A. The chairman should make a unilateral decision.
B. The purpose should be ignored to save time.
C. The purpose must be spelled out in formal Terms of Reference.
D. The chairman should ask each member individually what they think the purpose is.
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: The purpose of a forum may be so complex that it has to be spelled out in formal
Terms of Reference.
3. If the chairman and other members cannot agree on the purpose of a meeting, what is the
chairman's responsibility?
A. To move on with the meeting anyway.
B. To dismiss the meeting entirely.
C. To revert to the convening authority to seek clarification.
D. To force his own interpretation of the purpose on the members.
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: Where the chairman and the other members cannot achieve such an
understanding or agreement on what the purpose is, it is the chairman's duty to revert to the
convening authority to seek and secure clarification.
4. What is a key element for a meeting's efficiency and effectiveness, besides a good chairman?
A. Alarge conference room.
B. A good Secretariat.
C. Alarge budget.
D. A friendly atmosphere.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Just as every meeting needs a Chairman to achieve its purpose, it equally needs
a good Secretariat for efficiency and effectiveness.
