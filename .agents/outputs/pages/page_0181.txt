44. What is the primary responsibility of the Political Head of a Ministry regarding its activities?
A. To manage day-to-day financial transactions.
B. To approve all contracts.

45. What is the definition of a "Revenue Collector"?
A. An officer entrusted with receipt, custody, and disbursement of public money.
B. An officer entrusted with an official receipt, licence, or ticket booklet for regular revenue
collection and keeps a cash book.
C. An officer who approves revenue expenditure.
D. A Sub-Accounting Officer.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 119 defines a "Revenue Collector” as “an officer, other than
a Sub- Accounting officer, who is entrusted with an official receipt, licence or ticket booklet for
the regular collection of some particular forms of revenue and who is required to keep a cash
book.”
46. What is the definition of an "Imprest-holder"?
A. An officer who manages the ministry's main bank account.
B. An officer entrusted with the disbursement of public money for which vouchers cannot be
immediately presented to a Sub-Accounting Officer, and who keeps a cash book.
C. An officer who approves all capital expenditure.
D. An officer responsible for auditing revenue collections.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 120 defines "Imprest-holder” as "an officer other than a
Sub-Accounting officer who is entrusted with the disbursement of public money, for which
vouchers cannot be presented immediately to a Sub-Accounting Officer and who is required to
keep a cash book."
