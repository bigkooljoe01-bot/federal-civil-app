64. What should a Schedule Officer have, regarding their writing?
A. A complex style.
B. A good style.
C. A very informal style.
D. A poetic style.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: A Schedule Officer should have a good style.
65. What is a "draft" in government business?
A. Afinal, approved document.
B. A proposed reply, circular, or any other document put up by an officer to a higher authority.
C. A personal note.
D. A confidential report that is not meant for circulation.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: A draft is a proposed reply, circular or any other document put up by an officer to
a higher authority.
66. When is a draft considered good and approved?
A. Ifitis lengthy.
B. If it serves the prescribed purpose.
C. If it uses complex vocabulary.
D. If it is written quickly.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: If the proposed reply or suggested circular serves the prescribed purpose, it is
considered good and approved.
67. How might a draft be amended?
A. By completely rewriting it.
B. By filling in gaps or incorporating additional facts by a senior officer.
C. Only by the original writer.
D. By discarding it and starting anew.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: A draft may be amended by filling in the gaps in the efforts of the officer or by the
incorporation of additional facts by the senior officer.
