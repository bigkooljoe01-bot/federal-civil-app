Part 8: Externally Assisted Courses
56. Where training facilities under a foreign Government's technical assistance scheme are
required, all application forms for the nominations of candidates will be forwarded to the
National Planning Commission for:
A. Approval
B. Processing
C. Review
D. Selection
57. Officers attending courses of instruction under a technical assistance scheme will continue
to receive their emoluments while all other Conditions of Service will be decided in accordance
with the existing arrangements between the donor government and the:
A. Federal Civil Service Commission
B. Federal Government
C. Office of the Head of the Civil Service of the Federation
D. National Planning Commission
58. Where an Officer is permitted on the recommendation of his Permanent Secretary/Head of
Extra-Ministerial Office to take a course of instruction abroad on Government's own request,
special conditions may be imposed. These conditions will be decided by the Office of the Head
of the Civil Service of the Federation and may include the grant of leave without pay and the
withdrawal of all or any of the allowances, or other privileges as prescribed in this Chapter. Such
cases are to be informed in writing, before his departure, of the conditions which have been:
A. Approved
B. Decided
C. Negotiated
D. Reviewed
59. For post-graduate courses, in exceptional cases and where the course of study is not
offered by any Institution in Nigeria, Officers may be granted leave with pay subject to a
maximum of:
A. One year
B. Two years
C. Three years
D. Four years
60. Officers engaged in private studies for higher degrees shall not be hindered provided their
studies do not interfere with:
A. Their social life
B. Their personal interests
C. Their family commitments
D. Their official duties.
