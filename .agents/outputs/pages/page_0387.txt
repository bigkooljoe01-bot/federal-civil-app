77. What is the essential objective of the Public Procurement Act 2007?
A. To privatize all govemment-owned entities
B. To establish the National Council on Public Procurement and the Bureau of Public
Procurement as regulatory authorities
C. To increase the government's budget deficit
D. To remove all existing procurement policies and practices
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: The essential objective of the Public Procurement Act 2007 is the "Establishment
of National Council on Public Procurement and the Bureau of Public Procurement as the
regulatory authorities responsible for the monitoring and oversight of public procurement".
68. The Public Procurement Act 2007 applies to entities that derive at least what percentage of
funds for any procurement from the Federation Share of Consolidated Revenue Fund?
A. 10%
B. 25%
C. 35%
D. 50%
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: The Act applies to entities that derive at least 35% of funds appropriated for any
procurement from the Federation Share of Consolidated Revenue Fund.
