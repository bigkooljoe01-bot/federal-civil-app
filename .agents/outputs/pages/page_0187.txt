67. If a serious irregularity in a voucher is established or suspected, what is the first action the
officer controlling the vote should take?
A. Report to the Minister of Finance.
B. Immediately investigate and report the circumstances.
C. Disallow the payment immediately.
D. Transfer the funds to a suspense account.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 409 states: "Where a serious irregularity in a voucher is
established or suspected the officer controlling the vote shall immediately investigate and report
the circumstances..."
68. In the event of an unauthorized payment due to an incorrect certificate or entry on a
voucher, who shall be held responsible and surcharged?
A. Only the Sub-Accounting Officer.
B. The certifying officer or the Sub-Accounting Officer.
C. The Accounting Officer.
D. The Auditor-General.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 409 states: "the certifying officer or the sub-accounting
officer as provided under Regulation 420 shall be held responsible and surcharged for the
amount involved.”
69. The signature of the officer controlling a vote on a voucher certifies the accuracy of what?
A. Only the amount.
B. Only the payee's name.
C. Every detail on the voucher.
D. Only the contract reference.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 410 states: “The signature of the officer controlling a vote, or
an officer signing for him, certifies to the accuracy of every detail on the voucher.”
