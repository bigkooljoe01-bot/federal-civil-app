95. For receipts and licences with fixed fees, what additional information should be printed on
them?
A. The date of issue.
B. The amount of the fixed fee.
C. The name of the payer.
D. The signature of the issuing officer.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1220 requires that “the amount of the fixed fee should be
printed on the receipt or licence.”
96. How often must monthly returns of all receipt and licence books issued, used, and unused
be rendered, and to whom?
A. Annually, to the Minister of Finance.
B. Monthly, to the Accountant-General.
C. Quarterly, to the Auditor-General.
D. Bi-annually, to the Head of Department.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1221 mandates "monthly returns of all receipt and licence
books issued, used and unused... to the Accountant-General."
97. What is the procedure for the disposal of unused stock of receipt and licence books?
A. They can be kept indefinitely.
B. They must be returned to the Accountant-General for destruction by a Board of Survey.
C. They can be destroyed by the issuing officer.
D. They can be given to other departments.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1223 requires returning them to the Accountant-General for
destruction by a Board of Survey.
98. What action is required if a receipt or licence book is discovered to be missing?
A. No action is needed if it's an old book.
B. The circumstances must be reported immediately to the Accountant-General and the
Auditor-General.
C. A new book should be ordered without reporting.
D. Only the Head of Department needs to be informed.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1225 states, "The circumstances must be reported
immediately to the Accountant-General and the Auditor-General."
