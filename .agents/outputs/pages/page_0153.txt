36. The Head of Finance and Accounts is responsible for ensuring prompt rendition of which of
the following returns?
A. Consolidated Accounts (monthly transcripts).
B. Bank reconciliation statements.
C. Revenue and Expenditure returns.
D. All of the above.
Correct Answer: D
Explanation: Financial Regulation 114(e) lists all these as retums for which the Head of
Finance and Accounts is responsible for prompt rendition.
37. Question: What does the term "Sub-Accounting Officer” mean?
A. An officer who prepares financial reports for the Accountant-General.
B. An officer entrusted with receipt, custody, and disbursement of public money, required to
keep a cash book.
C. An officer who audits the accounts of a ministry.
D. The Head of Finance and Accounts.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 115(1) defines "Sub-Accounting Officer” as "an officer who is
entrusted with the receipt, custody, and disbursement of public money and who is required to
keep one of the recognised Cash books."
38. Which of the following is NOT included in the definition of a "Sub-Accounting Officer"?
A. Sub Treasurer of the Federation.
B. Federal Pay Officer.
C. Minister of a Ministry.
D. Pensions Pay Officer.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 115(ii) lists various officers included, but the "Minister" is the
Political Head (FR 116), not a Sub-Accounting Officer.
