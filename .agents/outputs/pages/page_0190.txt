80. What happens if expenditure is incorrectly charged to a vote?
A. It will be adjusted in the next financial year.
B. It will be disallowed.
C. It will be transferred to a general fund.
D. It will be reported to the Minister of Finance for approval.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 417 states: "Expenditure incorrectly charged to a vote shall
be disallowed.”
