Section F
Part 1: Financial Regulation: Internal Audit
1. What is the overall policy of intemal audit according to Financial Regulation 1701?
A. To replace external auditors.
B To provide a complete and continuous audit of accounts and records of revenue, expenditure,
plant, and stores.
C. To focus only on revenue generation.
D. To handle all financial transactions.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1701 states the policy is to provide a complete and
continuous audit of the accounts and records of revenue, expenditure, plant, and stores.
2. Who is responsible for the appointment of the Internal Auditor?
A. The Auditor-General.
B. The Head of Department.
C. The Accountant-General.
D. The Minister of Finance.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1702 specifies that the Accountant-General shall be
responsible for the appointment and deployment of Internal Auditors.
3. What is a key duty of the Intemal Auditor as per Financial Regulation 1703?
A. To approve all payments.
B. To conduct an annual audit of the entire Ministry.
C. To audit and certify all payment vouchers.
D. To manage the department's budget.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1703(d) lists one of the duties as auditing and certifying all
payment vouchers.
4. According to Financial Regulation 1704, what must the Head of the Internal Audit Unit
prepare annually?
A) The department's annual budget.
B) An audit program.
C) A list of staff to be promoted.
D) A report on staff performance.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1704 requires the Head of the Internal Audit Unit to prepare
an annual audit program.
