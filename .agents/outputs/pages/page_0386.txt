62. The concept of "Due Process” applies to which of the following aspects of government
business?
A. Procurement and contract awards only
B. Budgetary operations only
C. All aspects of Government business, including negotiation, recruitment, and promotion
D. Financial operations only
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: A common error is associating "due process" only with procurement, but it applies
to all other aspects of government business, such as negotiation, recruitment, and promotion.
63. What is one of the main objectives of the Due Process policy?
A. To increase government spending on all projects
B. To strictly enforce due process principles of transparency, competition, and efficiency
C. To promote extra-budgetary spending in Ministries, Departments, and Agencies (MDAs)
D. To centralize all procurement decisions at the federal level
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: A main objective is to strictly enforce due process principles of transparency,
competition, efficiency, and value for money in the procurement of public goods, works, and
services.
