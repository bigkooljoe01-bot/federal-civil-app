9. The Auditor-General's report on the accounts of the Federation is submitted to which body?

A. The President

B. The National Assembly

C. The Head of the Civil Service of the Federation

D. The Minister of Finance

10. What is the purpose of a Treasury Circular in financial management?

A. To provide guidelines for international trade

B. To give instructions on accounting procedures, financial regulations, and rules

C. To set government salaries.

D. To approve new government projects

Correct Answer: B.

Explanation: Treasury Circulars are directives that provide clear instructions and guidelines on

accounting procedures, financial regulations, and rules to all government agencies.

Part 2: Management of Relations with Parastatals

11. What is the primary role of a Parastatal?

A. To assist the Ministry in its policy formulation.

B. To manage all human resource issues for the Ministry.

C. To implement specific policies and programmes of the government as assigned.

D. To act as a legislative body for the Ministry.

Correct Answer: C.

Explanation: Parastatals are autonomous organizations, which exist to implement specific

policies and programmes assigned to them by the goverment.

12. Who is the Accounting Officer of a Parastatal?

A. The Permanent Secretary of the supervising Ministry.

B. The Chairman of the Board of the Parastatal.

C. The Chief Executive/Director-General of the Parastatal.

D. The Minister of the supervising Ministry.

Correct Answer: C.

Explanation: The Chief Executive or Director-General of a parastatal is the Accounting Officer

for all expenditures and revenue.
