61. What is the maximum duration of a contract appointment?
A. One year
B. Two years
C. Four years
D. Five years
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Rule 020406(vi) specifies that the duration of a contract appointment must be one
or two years and renewable but not more than four years in total*%.
62. When processing contract appointments, who is responsible for identifying the required
skills?
A. The Federal Civil Service Commission
B. The Office of the Head of the Civil Service of the Federation (OHCSF)
C. The Ministry/Extra-Ministerial Department
D. The National Youth Service Corps
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Rule 020405(i) states that Ministries, extra-ministerial departments, and Agencies
(MDAs) are to "undertake identification of required skills and defend the skills at the OHCSF,
whose responsibility shall be to work out and approve a waiver for the desired skills."
63. An officer on contract appointment is responsible for mentoring serving officers as a
condition for the offer of the contract appointment. Which rule specifies this?
A. 020405
B. 020406
C. 020407
D. 020408
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Rule 020406(i) states that the contract officer “must, as part of his duties, be
responsible for mentoring serving officers as a condition for the offer of the contract
appointment.”
64. What is the process for terminating a contract appointment?
A. A letter of termination is issued directly by the Ministry.
B. A recommendation from the Federal Civil Service Commission is required.
C. The contract is terminated at the discretion of the Permanent Secretary.
D. A simple verbal notification is sufficient.
Correct Answer: B
