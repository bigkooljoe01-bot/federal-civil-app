15. Who is responsible for establishing and supervising Federal Pay Offices in each state capital
of the federation?
A. The Minister of Finance.
B. The Auditor-General.
C. The Accountant-General.
D. The Head of Service.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 107(g) states the Accountant-General shall "establish and
supervise Federal Pay Offices in each state capital of the federation.”
16. What is one of the Accountant-General's duties regarding compliance with rules and
regulations?
A. To issue warnings to defaulting officers.
B. To conduct routine and in-depth inspection of books of accounts to ensure compliance.
C. To approve all government contracts.
D. To manage all federal government investments.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 107(h) states the Accountant-General shall "conduct routine
and in-depth inspection of the books of accounts of federal ministries... to ensure compliance
with rules, regulations, and policy decisions."
17. Who is responsible for approving and ensuring compliance with accounting codes, internal
audit guides, and stock verification manuals?
A. The Minister of Finance.
B. The Auditor-General.
C. The Accountant-General.
D. Accounting Officers.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 107(i) states the Accountant-General shall “approve and
ensure compliance with accounting codes, internal audit guides and stock verification manuals.”
