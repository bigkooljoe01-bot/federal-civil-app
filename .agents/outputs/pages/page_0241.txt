75. To whom should requests for Treasury Receipts Books be submitted?
A. The Minister of Finance.
B. The Accountant-General.
C. The Auditor-General.
D. The Head of Department.

Explanation: Financial Regulation 1205 states that requests for Treasury Receipts Books
should be submitted to the Accountant-General.
76. What must accompany every issue of a receipt book or books?
A. A delivery note.
B. A Receipt Book Issue Note.
C. A payment voucher.
D. A memo.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1206(i) specifies that every issue must be accompanied by a
Receipt Book Issue Note.
77. How many copies of the Receipt Book Issue Note are printed, and what is their distribution?
A. Two copies; original to officer, duplicate retained
B. Three copies; original to officer, duplicate to Auditor-General, triplicate retained.
C. Four copies; original and duplicate to officer (duplicate receipted and returned), triplicate to
Auditor-General, quadruplicate retained.
D. Five copies; all retained by the issuing officer.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1206(i) details the four-copy distribution: original and
duplicate to the officer (duplicate returned receipt), triplicate to the Auditor-General, and
quadruplicate retained.

A. The Accountant-General.

D. Heads of Department.
