91. How should unpresented cheques that become stale after 6 months be handled?
A. Re-issued immediately.
B. Written back in the Cash Book after written confirmation of non-payment from the bank.
C. Reported as a loss of funds.
D. Forwarded to the Accountant-General.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 807(a) states: "Unpresented cheques which become stale
after 6 months should be written back in the Cash Book after written confirmation of
non-payment from the bank."
92. How often should Sub-Accounting Officers submit their accounts?
A. By the seventh day of the following month.

D. Quarterly.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 808(i) states: "Sub-Accounting Officers shall submit their
accounts by the fourteenth day of the following month or within such other period as may be
prescribed by the Accountant-General."
