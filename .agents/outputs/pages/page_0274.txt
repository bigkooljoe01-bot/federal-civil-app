5. What type of reports must be submitted to the Accounting Officer, according to Financial
Regulation 1706?
A. Daily reports.
B. Periodic audit reports.
C. Staff performance reports.
D. Budget forecast reports.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1706 requires the Intemal Auditor to submit periodic audit
reports to the Accounting Officer.
6. What is the Internal Auditor's guide for auditing procedures known as, according to FR 1708?
A. The Auditor-General's Manual.
B. An Audit Guide.
C. A Financial Statement.
D. The General Warrant.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1708 states that the Head of Internal Audit Unit shall prepare
and issue an "Audit Guide" for the use of audit staff.
7. Which of the following is a restriction on the duties of audit staff, as per FR 1709?
A. They cannot make suggestions for improvements.
B. They are not allowed to handle cash or stores.
C. They must not report their findings.
D. They are not allowed to communicate with other staff.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1709(a) prohibits audit staff from handling cash, receipts,
stamps, or stores.
8. What must an Internal Auditor do when he discovers an apparent abuse of Financial
Regulations?
A. Ignore it.
B. Report it immediately to the Accountant-General.
C. Call the attention of the appropriate officer in writing.
D. Take disciplinary action against the officer.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1703(a) states the Internal Auditor shall call the attention of
the appropriate officer in writing where there is any apparent abuse of Financial Regulations.
