46. To what classification code should both the Treasury Receipt Voucher and Payment Voucher
for pension deductions be classified?
A. Revenue Head and Sub-Head.
B. Cash transfer code 2000 series.
C. Capital expenditure code.
D. General administrative expenses.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1907 specifies that both the Treasury Receipt Voucher and
Payment Voucher should be classified to cash transfer code 2000 series.
47. What is the overall goal of the pension scheme outlined in Chapter 19?
A. To centralize all government funds
B. To ensure proper administration and accounting of pension contributions.
C. To reduce government expenditure.
D. To provide short-term loans to employees.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The chapter details the administration, responsibilities of various entities, and
accounting procedures for the pension scheme, indicating its goal is proper management of
contributions.
48. What document provides the legal framework for the investment of pension funds by the
Central Bank of Nigeria?
A. The Financial Regulations book.
B. The Pension Reform Act.
C. The Annual Appropriation Act.
D. The Ministry of Finance circulars.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1904(b) states that investments are made "as provided in
the Pension Reform Act.”
49. What is a "Below-the-Line Classification code" used for in the context of pension
contributions?
A. To categorize revenue.
B. To record monthly pension contributions.
C. To track outstanding debts.
D. To manage capital projects.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1906 states that this code is used "for the recording of
monthly pension contributions.”
