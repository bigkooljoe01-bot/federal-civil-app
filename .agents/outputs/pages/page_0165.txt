83. What must Accounting Officers do if there is a considerable increase in arrears of revenue
without adequate explanation?
A. Assume it is a normal occurrence.
B. Inform the Minister of Finance.
C. Investigate the possibility of fraud.
D. Write off the arrears as irrecoverable.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 230(ii) wams officers to not overlook the possibility of fraud
and to take steps to test this possibility.
84. Who must be notified of any amounts collectable by a department's officers?
A. The Minister of Finance
B. The Auditor-General
C. The Heads of Department/Divisions
D. The Accountant-General
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 230(i) states that Heads of Department/Divisions should be
notified of any amounts collectable by their department officers.
85. What is the role of the cashier in the process of handling cheques and other negotiable
instruments received by post?
A. The cashier opens the mail.
B. The cashier maintains the Paper Money Register.
C. The cashier enters the details in the Paper Money Register after it is opened by the Head of
Accounts.
D. The cashier is not involved in this process.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 233(ii) mentions the cashier making entries in the Paper
Money Register, and Financial Regulation 233(iv) states that the Paper Money Register shall
not be maintained, or incoming mail opened, by the cashier.
