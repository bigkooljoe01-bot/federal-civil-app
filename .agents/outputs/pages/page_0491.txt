92. Who should sign the original and copies of the Handing Over Notes?
A. Only the Officer Handing Over.
B. Only the Officer Taking Over.
C. Both the Officer Handing Over and the Officer Taking Over.
D. The Head of Department only.
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: The original and the two copies of the Notes should be signed by both the Officer
Handing Over and the Officer Taking Over.
93. What is the purpose of passing the filed original of the Handing Over Notes to the

A. To keep it confidential.
B. To ensure that the duties of that post have been properly taken over.
C. To allow them to rewrite it.
D. To discard it.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: The filed original should then be passed to the Supervisor and/or a superior
officer who should know that the duties of that post have been properly taken over.
