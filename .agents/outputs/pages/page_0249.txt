5. What is required to support a withdrawal from a deposit?
A. A photocopy of the original receipt
B. A handwritten note from the depositor
C. The original receipt issued when the money was placed on deposit
D. A verbal confirmation from the Accounting Officer
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1306 states that withdrawals from deposit must be
supported by the original receipt issued when the money was placed on deposit.
6. What is the procedure for an original receipt after a withdrawal is made from a deposit?
A. Itis returned to the depositor
B. Itis filed for seven years
C. Itis marked "repaid" and attached to the withdrawal voucher
D. Itis sent to the Accountant-General
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1306 specifies that when a withdrawal is made, the original
receipt will be marked “repaid” and attached to the withdrawal voucher.
7. When should a refund of a deposit be made?
A. At the end of the financial year
B. When there is sufficient balance in the deposit account
C. As soon as itis due, provided that all necessary checks have been made
D. Only with the explicit approval of the Minister of Finance
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1307 states that refunds of deposits will be made as soon as
they are due, provided that all necessary checks and reconciliations have been completed.
8. What must a Sub-Accountant do with unclaimed deposits at the end of each financial year?
A. Transfer them to a suspense account
B. Send a list to the Accounting Officer
C. Write them off as irrecoverable
D. Use them for administrative expenses
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: According to Financial Regulation 1310, a list of all unclaimed deposits shall be
forwarded to the Accounting Officer at the end of each financial year.
