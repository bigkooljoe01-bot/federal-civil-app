42. Which of the following is NOT a type of appointment recognized in the Federal Public

Service?

A. Trainee/Pupil

B. Non-pensionable contract

C. Casual appointment

D. Acting appointment

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Rule 020202 lists the categories of appointments, and "casual appointment" is not

among them.

43. What is the quota reserved for recruitment of persons with disabilities?

A.2%

B.5%

C. 10%

D. 15%

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Rule 020202(b) reserves five percent (5%) of all recruitments for persons with

disabilities.

44. What is the minimum age requirement for eligibility for appointment into the Federal Public

Service?

A. Not less than 21 years

B. Not less than 16 years

C. Not less than 18 years

D. Not less than 25 years

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Rule 020206(a) states that to be eligible for appointment, every applicant must be

“not less than 18 years and not more than 50 years of age.”

45. An applicant for a public service position must possess a testimonial of good conduct from

which of the following?

A. His/her last employer

B. The last school or college attended

C.BothAand B

D. A local government chairman

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Rule 020206(d) requires an applicant to "possess a testimonial of good conduct

from the last employer or, if not previously employed, from the last school or college attended.”
