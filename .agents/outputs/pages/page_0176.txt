24. Who is personally responsible for ensuring that expenditure against a sub-head from which
provision was transferred by a virement warrant does not exceed the reduced balance?
A. The Accountant-General.
B. The Minister of Finance.
C. Officers authorised to incur expenditure (officers controlling votes).
D. The Head of Finance and Accounts.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 317 states: "Officers controlling votes are personally
responsible for ensuring compliance with this regulation.”
25. From which fund is Capital Expenditure paid?
A. Consolidated Revenue Fund.
B. Contingencies Fund.
C. Development Fund.
D. Federation Account.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 318 states: "Capital Expenditure is paid from the
Development Fund..."
26. What is the maximum period a Provisional Development Fund General Warrant can
authorize withdrawal of money?
A. Three months.
B. Six months.
C. Nine months.
D. Twelve months.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 319 states: “for a period not exceeding six months.”
27. Who authorizes the withdrawal of money from the Development Fund through a Provisional
Development Fund General Warrant?
A. The Minister of Finance.
B. The Accountant-General.
C. The President.
D. The National Assembly.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 319 states: "the President shall authorise the withdrawal of
money from the Development fund as may be necessary through the issuance of a Provisional
Development Fund General Warrant..."
