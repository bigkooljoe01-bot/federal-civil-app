39. What should intermediate officers do with papers that are routed through them for
information only?
A. They should delay them unnecessarily.
B. They should not delay them unnecessarily.
C. They should take a copy for themselves.
D. They should file them immediately.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Intermediate officers should not delay unnecessarily papers which are routed
through them for information only.
40. All Civil Servants are required to take what?
A. An Oath of Office.
B. An Oath of Secrecy118.
C. A pledge of allegiance.
D. A vow of poverty.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: All Civil Servants are required to take an Oath of Secrecy.
41. What is considered a very serious offense for a Civil Servant?
A. Taking a day off.
B. Unauthorized disclosure of any information acquired in the course of their duties121.
C. Arriving late to a meeting.
D. Talking to a colleague.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Unauthorized disclosure of any information acquired in the course of their duties
is a very serious offence.
42. What is the purpose of having a “confidential” classification for a document?
A. To make it difficult to access.
B. To protect information that could be harmful if disclosed.
C. To hide its contents from other Ministries.
