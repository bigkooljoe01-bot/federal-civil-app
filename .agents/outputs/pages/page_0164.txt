79. What is the purpose of the Paper Money Register?
A. To record all incoming and outgoing letters.
B. To record the receipt of cheques and other negotiable instruments received through the post.
C. To record the daily transactions of the Sub-Accounting Officer.
D. To track the movement of staff.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The context of Financial Regulations 232 and 233 indicates that the Paper Money
Register is for recording cheques, bank drafts, and other negotiable instruments received
through the post.
80. Who is responsible for ensuring that provisions for Value Added Tax (VAT) and Withholding
Tax (WHT) are made and remitted?
A. The Minister of Finance
B. The Accounting Officers
C. The Accountant-General
D. The Head of Internal Audit
Correct Answer: B

full compliance with the dual roles of making provision for and remitting VAT and WHT.
