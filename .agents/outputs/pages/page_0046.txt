150. What is the consequence of failing to pass the compulsory confirmation examinations for
clerical grade officers?
A. The officer will be retired.
B. The officer will have their probationary period extended.
C. The officer may have their appointment terminated at the end of the probationary period.
D. The officer will be demoted.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Rule 020303 specifies that an officer on probation is required to pass the
prescribed examination, and failure to do so may lead to the termination of the appointment.
Prescribed Examination for Confirmation
Part 15: General
151. According to the Public Service Rules, who is responsible for the conduct and supervision
of compulsory confirmation examinations?
A. The Head of the Civil Service of the Federation
B. The Permanent Secretary/Head of Extra-Ministerial Department
C. The Federal Civil Service Commission
D, The Examination Board
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Rule 030102 states that the Permanent Secretary or Head of an Extra-Ministerial
Department is responsible for the conduct and supervision of these examinations.
152. What is the consequence for an officer who fails to pass the compulsory confirmation
examination within the stipulated period?
A. The officer is granted an extension to retake the exam.
B. The officer's appointment is terminated.
C. The officer is demoted to a lower grade level.
D. The officer is suspended without pay.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Rule 030104 clearly states that failure to pass the examination within the
specified time shall result in the termination of the officer's appointment.
153. For a probationary officer, how often should the compulsory confirmation examinations be
held?
A. Once every quarter
B. Once every six months
C. Once a year
D. As stipulated by the Examination Board
Correct Answer: B
