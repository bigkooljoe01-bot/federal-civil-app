52. How should a Civil Servant handle a file that has been left on their desk and has been
forgotten?
A. Throw it away.
B. Report it to their superior officer and return it to the registry.
C. Keep it in their desk.
D. Read the contents to see if it is important.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Should a file be left on a desk and has been forgotten, it should be reported to the
superior officer and returned to the registry.
53. The handbook advises Civil Servants to avoid leaving what on their desks at the end of the
day?
A. Their personal belongings.
B. Official files and documents.
C. Their lunch box.
D. Their phone.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Documents and files must not be left lying on desks where they could get lost or
misplaced, or read by unauthorized persons.
54. What is the purpose of having a "confidential" classification for a document?
A. To make it difficult to access.
B. To protect information that could be harmful if disclosed.
C. To hide its contents from other Ministries.
D. To make it a permanent record.
Correct Answer: B.
The “confidential” classification is used to protect information that could be harmful if disclosed.

A. To protect information that could cause grave damage to the government if disclosed.
B. To protect information that is not important.
C. To make it difficult to access.
D. To hide its contents from other Ministries.
Correct Answer: A.
