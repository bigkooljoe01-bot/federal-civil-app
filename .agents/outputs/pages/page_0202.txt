22. Can a cheque be raised or cash paid for services for which a voucher has not been raised?
A. Yes, in urgent cases.
B. Yes, with special approval.
C. No, under no circumstances.
D. Only if the amount is small.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 601 states: "Under no circumstances shall a cheque be
raised or cash paid for services for which a voucher has not been raised.”
23. How should vouchers be raised for different sub-heads or different services?
A. A single voucher can cover multiple sub-heads and services.
B. Separate vouchers shall be raised for separate sub-heads and different services.
C. Vouchers can be combined if the total amount is small.
D. Itis at the discretion of the Accounting Officer.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 602 states: "Separate vouchers shall be raised for separate
sub-heads. Separate vouchers shall also be raised for the payment of different services."
24. What kind of particulars should all vouchers contain?
A. Only the amount and payee name.
B. Full particulars such as dates, numbers, quantities, distances, and rates.
C. A brief description of the service.
D. Reference to the budget only.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 603(i) states: "All vouchers shall contain full particulars of
each service, such as dates, numbers, quantities, distances and rates, so as to enable them to
be checked without reference to any other documents and will invariably be supported by
relevant documents such as local purchase orders, invoices, special letters of authority, time
sheets, etc.”
