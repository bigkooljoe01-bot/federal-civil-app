63. What must accompany the monthly returns of expenditure when an A LE. is issued between
ministries?
A. Original A.|.E. document.
B. Copies of paid vouchers.
C. Bank statements.
D. A list of all staff.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 406 states: "The monthly returns must be supported with
copies of paid vouchers.”
64. What stamp must be affixed to all vouchers, indents, local purchase orders, job orders, and
agreements when entered in the vote book?
A. "APPROVED BY ACCOUNTANT-GENERAL".
B. "ENTERED IN VOTE BOOK™.
C. "PAID IN FULL".
D. "AUDITED AND PASSED".
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 407 states: "...all vouchers, indents, local purchase orders,
job orders, agreements shall be stamped ‘ENTERED IN VOTE BOOK' and initialed by the
officer responsible for maintaining the vote book."
65. Who is responsible for ensuring that the requisite vote book entries are made before
payments are authorized?
A. Only the Accountant-General.
B. Officers empowered to incur expenditure.
C. The Head of Finance and Accounts.
D. The Sub-Accounting Officer.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 407 states: "Officers empowered to incur expenditure must
ensure by adding their initials against the. relevant entry that the requisite vote book entries are
made before the payments are authorised by their signatures to the payment vouchers.”
66. How often does the Accountant-General furnish Accounting Officers with schedules of
payments and adjustments?
A. Annually.
B. Quarterly.
C. As soon as possible after the end of each month.
D. Upon request.
Correct Answer: C
