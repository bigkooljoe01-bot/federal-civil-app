30. Question: What is the rule regarding signing multiple certificates on a payment voucher?
A. Signatures can be written across multiple certificates.
B. Each certificate on a payment voucher shall be signed separately.
C. Only the first certificate needs a full signature.
D. A single signature at the bottom of the voucher is sufficient.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 607 states: "Each certificate on a payment voucher shall be
signed separately. Signatures shall not be written across one or more certificates.”
31. How should duplicate and triplicate copies of vouchers be marked?
A. They do not need any special marking.
B. They should be clearly marked "Duplicate" and "Triplicate".
C. They should be stamped "Copy".
D. They should be marked "For Information Only".
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 608 states: "Duplicate and triplicate copies shall be clearly
marked "Duplicate", "Triplicate" and copies shall not be accepted as payment vouchers.”
32. What is the maximum time a payment voucher should be held in the checking section?
A. 24 hours.
B. 48 hours.
C. 72 hours.
D. 5 working days.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 609(iii) states: "Payment vouchers received in the checking
section must be promptly dealt with and under no circumstances shall a voucher be held in that
unit for more than forty-eight (48) hours."
33. What is the procedure if an alteration to the amount of a voucher is necessary?
A. The alteration can be initiated by the certifying officer.
B. A new voucher must be prepared.
C. The alteration can be made and stamped "Corrected".
D. The Head of Finance and Accounts can approve the alteration.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 610 states: "Alterations to the amount of a voucher whether
in words or figure are not allowed. A new voucher must be prepared when necessary.”
