4. What is the primary responsibility of the Executive arm of Government?
A. Adjudication of disputes.
B. Law making and confirmation of appointments.
C. Day-to-day management of Government, including policy formulation and execution.
D. Monitoring revenue accruals only.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: The Executive is responsible for the day-to-day management of Government, and
conceives, formulates, executes and monitors policies, programmes and projects.
5. Who heads the Federal Government in Nigeria's Presidential system?
A. The Chief Justice of Nigeria
B. The President of the Senate
C. The President, Commander-in-Chief of the Armed Forces
D. The Speaker of the House of Representatives
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Nigeria practices the Presidential system of Government under which the Federal
Government is headed by the President, Commander-in-Chief of the Armed Forces
6. At the Federal level, who heads the Legislature?
A. The Speaker of the House of Representatives
B. The Chief Justice of Nigeria
C. The President of the Senate
D. The Head of the Civil Service of the Federation
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: At the Federal level, the President of the Senate heads the Legislature,
complemented by the Speaker who heads the House of Representatives.
