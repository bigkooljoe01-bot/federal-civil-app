18. What is the correct way to address a minute or a file submission through official channels?
A. The most junior officer's title first, followed by other officers in descending order of status.
B. The most senior officer's title first, followed by other officers in descending order of status
prefixed by the word “through.”
C. The Permanent Secretary's title first, then everyone else.
D. There is no specific format; any order is acceptable.
Correct Answer:B.
Explanation: The style of address in general use for the submission of minutes through official
channels is by placing the most senior officer's title first followed by other officers in descending
order of status prefixed by the word "through".
19. What is the purpose of routing papers back through official channels after a decision has
been reached by a higher authority?
A. To delay the implementation of the decision.
B. To allow all intermediate officers to be acquainted with the decisions reached.
C. To give junior officers more work to do.
D. To provide an opportunity for the decision to be reversed.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Papers routed upwards through official channels are routed back in the same
way, and all intermediate officers are, therefore, acquainted with decisions reached by the
higher authority.
20. What is the proper procedure for a Civil Servant who has been transferred to a new office?
A. They should take all the files with them to their new office.
B. They should submit a formal handing-over note.
C. They should leave immediately without any handover.
D. They should ask their new supervisor to get the files.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: An officer being transferred to a new office must prepare a formal handing-over
note to their successor.
