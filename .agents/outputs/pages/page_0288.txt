62. What specific action must a driver take at the end of each journey, according to FR 2006?
A. Clean the vehicle.
B. Park the vehicle in the garage.
C. Record details in the logbook and sign against the entries.
D. Report to the Accounting Officer.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 2006 requires the driver to record details of the journey in
the logbook and sign against the entries.
63. Who is responsible for examining the logbook regularly and signing against the entries?
A. The Accounting Officer.
B. The Permanent Secretary or Head of Extra-Ministerial Office.
C. The Transport Officer.
D. The driver's supervisor.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 2007 states that the Permanent Secretary or Head of
Extra-Ministerial Office shall examine the logbook regularly and sign against the entries.
64. What is the frequency for the Mechanical Engineer to verify and certify the fuel and
lubricants consumption?
A. Daily.
B. Weekly.
C. Monthly.
D. Annually.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 2008 requires the Mechanical Engineer to verify and certify
fuel and lubricants consumption monthly.
65. What action must be taken for all journeys outside office hours or during weekends and
public holidays?
A. They are strictly prohibited.
B. They require the written permission of the Accounting Officer or his delegated representative.
C. They are permitted for personal use.
D. They must be reported to the police.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 2009 states that journeys outside office hours, weekends,
and public holidays require the written permission of the Accounting Officer or his delegated
representative.
