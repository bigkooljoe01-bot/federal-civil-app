95. What is the main danger of an organization's records not being kept properly?
A. Civil servants will not be paid on time.
B. It could lead to the loss of government revenue.
C. It could lead to policy reversals and conflicts.
D. The public will lose trust in the government.
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: If records are not kept properly, it could lead to policy reversals, conflicts within
and between agencies, and chaos in the public sector.
96. Who, according to the document, is responsible for the proper keeping of records in the Civil
Service?
A. Only the Permanent Secretary.
B. Only the staff of the Records/Registry Unit.
C. All Civil Servants, regardless of their position.
D. Only the Head of the Civil Service of the Federation.
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: The proper keeping of records is the responsibility of every Civil Servant,
irrespective of their position.
97. What is the term for a document created in response to an instruction or a matter referred to
an officer?
A.A letter.
B. A memorandum.
C. A minute.
D. A note.
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: A minute is a note or document that is created in response to an instruction or a
matter referred to an officer for his attention, advice, or action.

B. The officer's recommendations and conclusions.
