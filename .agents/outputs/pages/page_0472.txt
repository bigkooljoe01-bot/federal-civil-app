10. What is the purpose of the chairman arriving promptly at the venue and starting the meeting
on time?
A. To finish the meeting as quickly as possible.
B. To show he is more important than the members.
C. To encourage members to take the forum and its objectives seriously28.
D. To rush the members into making decisions.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: The chairman should make it a point of duty to arrive promptly at the venue, with
the full intention of starting the meeting on time, as this will encourage the members to take the
forum and its objectives seriously.
11. How should a chairman conduct a meeting?
A. In a dictatorial manner, enforcing his will on the members.
B. In a fair, orderly and effective manner, using the adopted Agenda31.
C. By allowing the most senior members to dominate the discussions.
D. By allowing the most talkative members to speak as much as they want.
Correct Answer:B.
Explanation: A chairman should conduct the meeting in a fair, orderly and effective manner,
using as much as possible the adopted Agenda.
12. A chairman should consciously give room for opposing views and protect which members
from being intimidated?
A. The most senior members.
B. The most talkative members.
C. The weak or junior members34.
D. The chairman's personal friends.
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: He should consciously give room for opposing views and protect the weak or
junior members from being intimidated by their superiors.
