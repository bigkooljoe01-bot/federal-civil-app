98. Who brings to account all transactions conducted by Sub-Accounting Officers in his main
and subsidiary ledgers for a self-accounting unit?
A. The Accountant-General.
B. The Director of Finance and Accounts.
C. The Head of Accounts of a self-accounting unit.
D. The Auditor-General.
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 812 states: “The Head of Accounts of a self accounting unit
will bring to account in his main and subsidiary ledgers all transactions conducted by his
Sub-Accounting Officers and shall submit the consolidated account of the unit to the Accountant
General as in Financial Regulation 808."
99. What is the purpose of reconciling outstanding items in the Bank Reconciliation Statement
expeditiously?
A. To delay payments.
B. To ensure the effectiveness of the statement.
C. To reduce bank charges.
D. To identify new revenue sources.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 807(c) states: "Prompt action should always be taken to
obtain bank advice to confirm the various entries in the bank statement as the effectiveness of
the Bank Reconciliation Statement lies in the prompt clearance of outstanding items."
100. What kind of payments are Revenue and Expenditure vouchers submitted in duplicate to
Sub-Accounting Officers?
A. Only for large amounts.
B. All types of payments unless otherwise directed by the Accountant-General.
C. Only for inter-ministerial transfers.
D. Only for capital projects.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 803 states: "Revenue Expenditure, and Below-the-line
vouchers will be submitted to Sub-Accounting Officers in duplicate, except as May be otherwise
directed from time to time by the Accountant- General."
