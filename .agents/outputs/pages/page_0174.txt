17. Virement from a Recurrent Expenditure Head of Account to a Capital Expenditure Head of

Account is:

A. Allowed with Minister of Finance approval.

B. Allowed with National Assembly approval.

C. Not allowed.

D. Allowed only in the last quarter of the financial year.

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Financial Regulation 316(iii) states: "Virement from one Head of Account in the

Recurrent Expenditure estimates to another Head of account in Capital Expenditure estimates

shall not be allowed and vice versa.”

18. Who must approve any variation in the Establishment or rates of allowances authorized for

the Public Service?

A. Only the Minister of Finance.

B. Only the Office of the Head of Civil Service.

C. The Minister of Finance and the Office of the Head of Civil Service.

D. The Accounting Officer.

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Financial Regulation 311 states: "Any variation in the Establishment or rates of

allowances... must receive the approval of the Minister of Finance and the Office of the Head of

Civil Service..."

19. What is the purpose of a Supplementary (Statutory) Expenditure Warrant?

A. To provide funds for new capital projects.

B. To authorize additional expenditure for votes chargeable against the Consolidated Revenue

Fund by virtue of the Constitution.

C. To replenish the Contingencies Fund.

D. To facilitate virement between sub-heads.

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Financial Regulation 312 states it is for "additional expenditure over and above

that included in the General Warrant or in a Supplementary General Warrant, in respect of votes

which are chargeable against the Consolidated Revenue Fund by virtue of the Constitution..."
