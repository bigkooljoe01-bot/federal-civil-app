23. What is the purpose of an "official letter” in the Civil Service?
A. To communicate with other Ministries and the public.
B. To communicate internally.
C. To document a personal conversation.
D. To provide a confidential report.
Correct Answer: A.
Explanation: An official letter is used to communicate with other Ministries, Parastatals, and the
general public.
24. What should letters always have?
A. A personal salutation.
B. A heading briefly descriptive of the subject matter, a reference number, and a date.
C. A humorous opening.
D. Alist of all previous correspondence.
Correct Answer B.
Explanation: Letters should always have a heading briefly descriptive of the subject matter and
must bear a reference number and date.
25. What is the purpose of an “official stamp"?
A. To indicate who created the document.
B. To indicate that a document is authentic and has been received by an office.
C. To make a document look nice.
D. To indicate the date the document was created.
Correct Answer:B.
Explanation: An official stamp is used to indicate that a document is authentic and has been
received by an office.
26. What is the purpose of a *circular™?
A. To communicate with the public.
B. To communicate an official announcement, directive, or information to all staff in a Ministry or
the Service at large.
C. To document a personal conversation.
D. To provide a confidential report.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: A circular is used to communicate an official announcement, directive, or
information to all staff in a Ministry or the Service at large.
