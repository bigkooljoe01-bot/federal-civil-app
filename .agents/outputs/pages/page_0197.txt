5. What documents are federal ministries and other arms of government required to use for their
daily financial transactions?
A. Only the Annual Estimates.
B. The Financial Regulations, Treasury Accounting Manual, and Treasury Circulars issued by
the Office of the Accountant General.
C. Internal audit reports only.
D. Specific departmental guidelines only.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 503 states: "For this purpose, federal ministries/extra
ministerial offices and other arms of government shall use the Financial Regulations, Treasury
Accounting Manual and Treasury Circulars issued by the Office of the Accountant General in
accounting for their daily financial transactions.”
6. Who must approve any material variation of an existing accounting procedure or a proposed
new accounting procedure?
A. The Minister of Finance only.
B. The Accounting Officer only.
C. The Accountant-General after consultation with the Auditor-General.
D. The Head of Finance and Accounts.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 504 states: "Any material variation of an existing accounting
procedure, or a proposed accounting procedure to be introduced into a new office, must be
approved by the Accountant-General after consultation with the Auditor-General."
