18. What is a key function of the Head of the Internal Audit Unit concerning the audit program?
A. To delegate all tasks to junior staff.
B. To amend the program as he deems fit, without approval.
C. To ensure the audit program is effectively carried out.
D. To present the program to the general public.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1704 mandates that the Head of the Internal Audit Unit shall
“ensure that the programme is effectively carried out.”
19. What does the "management letter" from the Internal Auditor primarily address?
A. An officer's promotion.
B. Irregularities and failures of intemal control.
C. The department's budget performance.
D. Recommendations for new hires.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The management letter is used to communicate discoveries of negligent disregard
of financial regulations or abuse of internal controls.
