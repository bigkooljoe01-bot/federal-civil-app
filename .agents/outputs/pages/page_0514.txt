87. Who is the Chairman of the National Council on Establishments?
A. The Head of the Civil Service of the Federation.
B. The President of the Senate.
C. The Minister of Finance.
D. The Head of each state's Civil Service.
Correct Answer: A.
Explanation: The council is composed of the Heads of Service of the States, and the Head of
the Civil Service of the Federation serves as the Chairman.
88. How often does the National Council on Establishments meet?
A. Every quarter.
B. At least once a year.
C. As needed, when a new President takes office.
D. Only when there is a major policy change.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: The National Council on Establishments meets at least once a year to deliberate
on establishment matters.
89. What is one of the functions of the National Council on Establishments?
A. To manage the budget for all government ministries.
B To serve as a consultative body on matters of salary structures, gradings, and conditions of
serviced.
C. To oversee the day-to-day work of civil servants.
D. To hire and fire civil servants.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: The Council acts as a consultative body for the governments of the Federation on
matters such as staff establishments, salary structures, gradings, and conditions of service to
achieve uniformity in the Nigerian Civil Services.
90. What is the condition regarding transfers or lateral conversions into the Foreign Service?
A. Itis encouraged for experienced staff from other ministries.
B. Itis not allowed above GL 10 due to the uniqueness of the Foreign Service.
C. Itis a common practice for promotions.
D. Itis permissible with special approval from the President.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Due to the unique nature of the Foreign Service, lateral conversions or transfers.
into it are not permitted for officers above Grade Level 10.
