38. What should be presented to the Sub-Accounting Officer for inspection when payments are
to be made to legal representatives?
A. A simple letter of request.
B. Authorities such as Powers of Attorney, Letters of Administration, etc.
C. A copy of the deceased's will only.
D. A police report.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 615 states: "When payments are to be made to legal
representatives, authorities such as Powers of Attorney, Letters of Administration, etc. shall be
presented to the Sub Accounting Officer for inspection...”
39. How should payments to firms be made?
A. By cash only.
B. By cheque crossed "Not Negotiable", "Account Payee only”, in favour of the firm.
C. By bank transfer directly to the firm's account.
D. By postal order.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 616 states: "Payments to firms, shall be made only by
cheques crossed "Not Negotiable", "Account Payee only", in favour of the firm."
40. What must be given on the face of the voucher, signed by the payee, when it is
impracticable to obtain receipts for petty disbursements?
A. A detailed explanation.
B. A certificate to that effect.
C. An affidavit.
D. A promissory note.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 617 states: "When it is impracticable to obtain receipts for
petty disbursements, a certificate to that effect must be given on the face of the voucher signed
by the payee.”

government against double payment?
A. A new invoice.
B. A certificate of indemnity.
C. A sworn affidavit.
