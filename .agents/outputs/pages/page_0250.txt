9. What is the minimum period of time a deposit must remain unclaimed before it is transferred
to revenue?
A. Three years
B. Two years
C. One year
D. Six months
Correct Answer: A
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1311 states that if the money has remained on deposit for
more than three years, it will be transferred to revenue.
10. What must be done with bank guarantees received to cover possible default or negligence
of a contractor or public officer?
A. They can be kept in a private office safe.
B. They must be lodged with the Accountant-General.
C. They should be submitted to the Auditor-General for safekeeping.
D. They must be retained in the custody of the Accounting Officer.
Correct Answer: D
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1314 stipulates that bank guarantees must be retained in the
custody of the Accounting Officer to be produced on request by the Accountant-General or the
Auditor-General.
11. Who has the authority to approve special instructions for the operation of deposit accounts
peculiar to a ministry?
A The Minister of Finance
B. The Accountant-General in consultation with the Auditor-General
C. The Head of Finance and Accounts
D. The Accounting Officer
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1303 requires that special instructions for the operation of
deposit accounts shall be subject to the approval of the Accountant-General in consultation with
the Auditor-General.

the prescribed period?
A. A Treasury Voucher
B. An official receipt voucher
C. A journal voucher
D. A bank teller
