40. If a directive from a Political Head results in unauthorized expenditure, who is responsible
unless a report was made?
A. The Political Head.
B. The Accounting Officer.
C. The Head of Service.
D. The Minister of Finance.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 117(ii) states, “the Accounting Officer shall be responsible for
such an unauthorized expenditure unless a report had been made by the Accounting Officer to
the Head of Service... or to the Minister."
41. What is the responsibility of the Political Head regarding his ministry, agency, and/or
parastatal?
A. To supervise and control its activities.
B. To manage its day-to-day financial transactions.
C. To personally approve all procurements.
D. To conduct internal audits.
Correct Answer: A
Explanation: Financial Regulation 118(i) states, “It shall be the responsibility of the Political
Head to supervise and control the activities of his ministry, agency, and/or parastatal.”
42. "Revenue Collector” is defined as an officer who:
A. Is a Sub-Accounting Officer.
B. Is entrusted with an official receipt, licence, or ticket booklet for regular revenue collection
and keeps a cash book.
C. Only deals with large sums of money.
D. Approves revenue expenditure.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 119 defines a "Revenue Collector” as "an officer, other than
a Sub- Accounting officer, who is entrusted with an official receipt, license, or ticket booklet for
the regular collection of some particular forms of revenue and who is required to keep a cash
book.”
