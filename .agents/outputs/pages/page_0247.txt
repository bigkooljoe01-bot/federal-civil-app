99. Question: What must be obtained when receipt and licence books are handed over from one
officer to another?
A. A verbal confirmation.
B. A handing-over certificate.
C. A simple memo.
D. A new set of books.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1226 states, "When receipt and licence books are handed
over from one officer to another, a handing-over certificate must be obtained.”
100. What is the procedure for the destruction of used receipt and licence books?
A. They can be burned without supervision.
B. They must be destroyed by buming in the presence of a Board of Survey, and a certificate of
destruction issued.
C. They can be shredded and recycled.
D. They can be buried.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1227 and 1228 specify destruction by burning in the
presence of a Board of Survey, with a certificate issued.
