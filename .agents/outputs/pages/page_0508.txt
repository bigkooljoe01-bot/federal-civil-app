62. What is the "Head of Department's” role regarding copies of his own letters?
A. He circulates them to all his subordinates.
B. He does not circulate them to his subordinates.
C. He gives them to the most junior officers.
D. He files them in the confidential registry.
Correct Answer: B..
Explanation: It is not customary for copies of the Head of Department's own letters to be
circulated to his subordinates.
63. When a Head of Department wants a subordinate to see a copy of his letter, what does he
do?
A. He sends an email.
B. He minutes the relevant file to the person concerned.
C. He tells them about it verbally.
D. He makes a public announcement.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: When a Head of Department considers it necessary, he merely minutes the
relevant file to the person concerned — who then sees the copy of the letter.
64. Why is it important to include file references on the file copy of a letter?
A. To make the file longer.
B. To avoid unnecessary research work later on.
C. To show that the letter is important.
D. To confuse other officers.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: If this is done when the original letter is being written, unnecessary research work
could be avoided later on.
65. What does a "Book File" contain?
A. Alist of all government policies.
B. A collection of letters and minutes arranged chronologically.
C. Alist of all govemment employees.
D. A personal diary of an officer.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: The Book File system is the method of filing whereby letters and minutes are all
enclosed on the left-hand side-face to cover in chronological order and are numbered in the
same fashion as a book.
