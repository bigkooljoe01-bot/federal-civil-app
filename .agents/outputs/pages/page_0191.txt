84. Who can effect an alteration to the classification of a voucher?
A. Any checking officer.
B. Any Sub-Accounting Officer.
C. The officer controlling expenditure.
D. The Head of Finance and Accounts.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 421 states: "An alteration to the classification of a voucher
shall only be effected by the officer controlling expenditure who will amend his Vote Book
accordingly."
85. What is the responsibility of officers controlling votes regarding payment for services
rendered?
A. To defer payments to the next financial year for budgetary reasons.
B. To ensure payments are settled within the financial year in which they were rendered.
C. To only pay for services after all claims have been perfectly validated.
D. To transfer all outstanding claims to a suspense account.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 412(i) states: "Officers controlling votes are responsible for"
ensuring that, as far as possible, payment for services rendered are settled within the financial
year in which they were rendered.”
