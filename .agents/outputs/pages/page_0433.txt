51. What is the purpose of classifying documents in the Civil Service?

A. To make them difficult to access.

B. To prevent new staff from seeing them.

C. To sensitize officers about the degree of care required for each document.

D. To hide their contents from other Ministries.

Correct Answer: C.

Explanation: Classified documents are duly classified so as to sensitize officers about the

degree of care required for each.

52. In a Secret Registry, what is typically kept?

A. All files, regardless of classification.

B. Only personal files of staff.

C. Only highly classified subject files.

D. Only public records.

Correct Answer: C.

Explanation: Where the registry is split into two, only highly classified subject files are kept in

the Secret Registry whilst the Confidential Registry keeps the confidential personal files of the

staff.

53. What is the potential risk of a document being destroyed, especially with older methods of

record management?

A. It can be easily recreated.

B. It can never be recalled and has to be recreated.

C. It will automatically reappear in a "Recycle Bin."

D. It can be recalled with a simple command.

Correct Answer: B.

Explanation: Pre-computer, unwanted records must be physically destroyed either in a paper

shredding machine or burt, and once a document is destroyed, it can never be recalled. It can

only be recreated again.
