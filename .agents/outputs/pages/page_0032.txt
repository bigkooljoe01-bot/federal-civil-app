95. What is the responsibility of ministries regarding acting appointments?
A. They must inform the Federal Civil Service Commission about the acting appointment.
B. They must obtain approval from the Head of the Civil Service of the Federation.
C. They must conduct a fresh promotion exercise.
D. They must give a written test to the officer.
Correct Answer: A
Explanation: Rule 020707 specifies that ministries "shall be required to notify the Federal Civil
Service Commission when officers revert from acting appointments.”
96. What happens if an officer on an acting appointment proceeds on casual or special leave?
A. The acting appointment ceases.
B. The acting appointment is suspended.
C. The officer will not be regarded as relinquishing the duties and responsibilities of the acting
appointment.
D. The acting appointment is extended.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Rule 020708 states that an acting officer "will not be regarded as relinquishing the
duties and responsibilities of the acting appointment” when proceeding on a casual or special
leave.
97. Who is responsible for making acting appointments for a duty post below SGL 14?
A. The Federal Civil Service Commission
B. The Permanent Secretary/Head of Extra-Ministerial Office
C. The Head of the Civil Service of the Federation
D. This is not explicitly stated in the provided text.
Correct Answer: D
Explanation: Rule 020701 only specifies that acting appointments are made for a duty post not
lower than SGL 14. It does not provide information for posts below this grade level.
98. When does a cessation of an acting appointment not happen?
A. When the substantive holder of a duty post resumes.
B. When the acting officer takes up another post.
C. When the acting officer proceeds on casual or special leave.
D. When the acting officer is promoted.
Correct Answer: C
