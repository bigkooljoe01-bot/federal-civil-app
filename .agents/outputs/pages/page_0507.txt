57. When an officer receives a circular that does not require action, what should they do?
A. Discard it.
B. Keep it on their desk.
C. Minute the file to others for information and disposal.
D. Reply to the circular.
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: If he does not require actually to initiate any action he will either merely minute the
file to others for information and disposal or he will file it.
58. What does the term "Minute/Memo" mean?
A. A summary of a meeting.
B. A written submission to present a view or position on an issue.
C. A short report for a supervisor.
D. A personal note.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Minute/Memo:- Written submission to present a view or position on an issue.
59. What does the term "Brief" mean?
A. A written submission on an extant position on a subject matter.
B. A personal note.
C. Along report.
D. A list of all government policies.
Correct Answer: A.
Explanation: Brief:- Written submission on an extant position on a subject matter.
60. What is the purpose of the phrase "For the attention of*?
A. To address a specific person within a department.
B. To address a specific department.
C. To address the public.
D. To address the Chairman.
Correct Answer: A.
Explanation: The phrase “For the attention of" is used when a letter is addressed to a
department or organization but is intended for a specific person within that organization.
61. Who is the "Officer Taking Over" in the context of a handing-over note?
A. The officer who is leaving the post.
B. The officer who is taking up the post.
C. The head of the department.
D. The Permanent Secretary.
Correct Answer: B.
