7. Warrants issued by the Minister of Finance authorize the Accountant-General of the
Federation to disburse funds from which accounts?
A. Only the Consolidated Revenue Fund.
B. Only the Development Fund.
C. Consolidated Revenue Fund and Development Fund.
D. Contingencies Fund only.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 103 mentions "Warrants issued by the Minister from time to
time authorizing the Accountant-General of the Federation to disburse funds from the
Consolidated Revenue Fund and Development Fund.”
8. According to Financial Regulation 103, no payments shall be made except on the authority of
a warrant duly issued and signed by whom?
A. The President.
B. The Accountant-General.
C. The Minister of Finance.
D. The National Assembly.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 103 explicitly states, "no payments shall be made except on
the authority of a warrant duly issued and signed by the Minister."
9. Question: Which of the following is NOT listed as a financial legislation enacted by the
National Assembly?
A. The Finance (Control and Management) Act, Cap 144.
B. The Annual Appropriation Act.
C. The Public Service Rules.
D. The Revenue Mobilisation and Fiscal Allocation Commission Act, Cap 16.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 104 lists the Finance (Control and Management) Act, Annual
Appropriation Act, Supplementary Appropriation Act, and Revenue Mobilisation and Fiscal
Allocation Commission Act. The Public Service Rules are regulations, not an Act listed here.
