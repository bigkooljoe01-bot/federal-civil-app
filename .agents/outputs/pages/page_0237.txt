60. What is prohibited regarding private money or personal effects in an official safe?
A. They can be kept if space allows.
B. Officers are prohibited from keeping private money or personal effects in an official safe, and
any found will be credited to revenue.
C. They can be kept with permission.
D. Only personal effects are allowed.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1119 explicitly states, "Officers are prohibited front keeping
private money or personal effects in an official safe, etc. Any private money found in an official
safe shall be credited to revenue.”
61. Who is personally responsible for ensuring that the balance of cash with the cashier is
locked in a strong-room or safe at the close of business daily?
A. The cashier only.
B. Sub-Accounting Officers.
C. The Head of Department.
D. The Internal Auditor.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1120 assigns this responsibility to "Sub-Accounting Officers.”
62. What must be kept in each strong-room or reserve cash safe for all cash, stamps,
receipt/licence books, and other articles deposited?
A. A simple list.
B. Registers.
C. A daily log.
D. A memo.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1121 requires that "Registers shall be kept in each
strong-room or reserve cash safe of all cash, stamps, receipt or licence books and other articles
deposited.”
63. What is the rule regarding the monthly check of contents in strong-rooms or safes?
