61. What should a Civil Servant do if they come across a classified document that they are not
authorized to view?
A. Read it anyway to be informed.
B. Passiit to a colleague.
C. Report it to their superior officer.
D. Ignore it.
Correct Answer: C..
Explanation: An officer who comes across a classified document that they are not authorized to
view should report it to their superior officer.
62. What is the purpose of a “despatch book"?
A. To record all incoming mail.
B. To record all outgoing mail
C. To record all official meetings.
D. To record all financial transactions.
Correct Answer:B.
Explanation: A despatch book is used to record all outgoing mail from a department or Ministry.
63. What is the purpose of a "receipt book"?
A. To record all incoming mail.
B. To record all outgoing mail.
C. To record all official meetings
D. To acknowledge the receipt of all official mail.
Correct Answer: D.
Explanation: A receipt book is used to acknowledge the receipt of all official mail.

A. To list all the files in a Ministry and their unique reference numbers.
B. To list all the staff in a Ministry.
C. To record all official meetings.
D. To record all financial transactions.
Correct Answer: A.
