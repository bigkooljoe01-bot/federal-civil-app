54. What characteristics of good govemance involve free availability and accessibility of
information?
A) Participation
B) Accountability
C) Transparency
D) Rule of Law
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Under Transparency, information is freely available and accessible to those who
will be affected by such decisions and their enforcement
55. Which characteristic of good governance implies that institutions and processes produce
results that meet societal needs while making the best use of resources?
A. Equity and Inclusiveness
B. Effectiveness and Efficiency
C. Responsiveness
D. Purposeful Leadership
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Effectiveness and Efficiency: processes and institutions produce results that
meet the needs of society while making the best use of resources at their disposal'”.
56. What is a key aspect of "Equity and Inclusiveness" in good governance?
A. Ensuring only certain groups benefit from development
B. Ensuring all members of society feel they have a stake and are not excluded
C. Prioritizing the needs of the majority over minorities
D Discriminating based on religion or ethnic group
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Equity and Inclusiveness: ensuring that all members of society feel that they
have a stake in it and do not feel excluded from the mainstream.
57. What does "Consensus Orientation” in good governance emphasize?
A. The need for strict adherence to a single viewpoint
B.The need for mediation of different interests to reach broad consensus for the whole
community's best interest
C. Short-term gains over long-term development
D. Unilateral decision-making by the government
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Consensus Orientation: need for mediation of the different interests in the
society to reach a broad consensus on what is in the best interest of the whole community and
how this can be achieved
