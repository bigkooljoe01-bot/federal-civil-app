7. A key principle of service delivery is to be responsive. What does this mean?
A. Only serving those who are powerful.
B. Serving all stakeholders within a reasonable timeframe.
C. Only serving those who ask for help.

Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: The principle of Responsiveness states that institutions and processes try to
serve all stakeholders within a reasonable timeframe.
8. Which of the following is NOT one of the fundamental and universal principles for the
provision of public services listed in the chapter?
A. Principle of equality of treatment.
B. Principle of nepotism
C. Principle of continuity.
D. Principle of legality.
Correct Answer:B.
Explanation: The principles listed include equality of treatment, neutrality, legality, continuity,
proximity and accessibility of services, participation, quality, evaluation, transparency, speed and
responsiveness, accountability, motivation, and integrity. Nepotism is a prohibited act under the
Principle of Integrity.
