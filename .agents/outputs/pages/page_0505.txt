49. What is the consequence of a Minister making a policy pronouncement without Executive
Council approval?
A. The Minister will be praised.
B. The Minister may cause massive discomfort for their principals or even lose their job.
C. The Minister will be promoted.
D. The Minister will be ignored.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: The chapter cautions an officer not to allow a Minister to make a policy
pronouncement unless such a Council approval has been secured. Otherwise, you may cause
your principals massive discomfort or even their jobs.
50. What kind of language should be used in government communication?
A. Complex and academic.
B. Simple, smooth, and free-flowing.
C. Technical jargon only.
D. Colloquial and informal.
Correct Answer: : B.
Explanation: Your style and choice of words should be simple, smooth and free- flowing..
51. What is the purpose of including references to enclosures in the letter itself?
A. To make the letter longer.
B. To show that enclosures exist.
C. To make it easy to find them.
D. To ensure the enclosures do not become detached from the letter.
Correct Answer: D.
Explanation: The abbreviations Enc. or Encs. should be included at the foot of the letter as a
reminder that the enclosures are there, and that they are not detached from the letter.
