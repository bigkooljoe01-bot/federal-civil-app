33. What is the primary purpose of a Development Fund Virement Warrant?
A. To fund new capital projects.
B. To permit the issue of additional funds for the completion of a capital project where earlier
funds were insufficient.
C. To transfer funds from the Consolidated Revenue Fund.
D. To authorize expenditure from the Contingencies Fund.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 324 states: "The purpose of a Development Fund Virement
Warrant is to permit the issue of additional funds required for the completion of a capital project,
for which the funds earlier provided... was insufficient or inadequate..."
34. Which of the following is a limitation for a Development Fund Virement Warrant?
A. Re-allocation can be made between any Heads of Expenditure.
B. Funds can be provided for a new project.
C. Re-allocation may be made only within the same Head of Expenditure in the Capital
Estimates.
D. It can increase provision to raise doubts about National Assembly intentions.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 324(a) states: “re-allocation may be made only within the
same Head of Expenditure in the Capital Estimates.”
35. Who issues a Development Fund Virement Warrant?
A. The Accountant-General.
B. The Auditor-General
C. The Minister of Finance after approval by the National Assembly.
D. The President.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 324 states: "A Development Fund Virement Warrant shall be
issued by the Minister of Finance after approval by the National Assembly."
