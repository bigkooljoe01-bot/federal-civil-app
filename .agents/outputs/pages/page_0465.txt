82. How should a Civil Servant handle a file that has been on their desk for a long time without
any action?
A. Keep it in their desk.
B. Report it to their superior officer and return it to the registry.
C. Throw it away.
D. Pass it to a colleague.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Should a file be left on a desk for a long time without any action, it should be
reported to the superior officer and retumed to the registry.
83. The handbook advises against leaving files unattended on a desk. What is the reason for
this?
A. Itis a sign of laziness.
B. It can get lost or misplaced.
C. It can be read by unauthorized persons.
D. Both Band C.
Correct Answer: D.
Explanation: Documents and files must not be left lying on desks where they could get lost or
misplaced, or read by unauthorized persons.
84. What is the purpose of the "Official Secret Act"?
A. To encourage civil servants to share all government information.
B. To prevent the leaking of government secrets to unauthorized persons.
C. To protect civil servants from prosecution.
D. To allow for the free flow of information to the public.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: The Official Secret Act is to prevent the leaking of government secrets to
unauthorized persons.
85. What is the purpose of a "handing-over note"?
A. To record the personal assets of an officer.
B. To detail the projects, files, and responsibilities of a position for a successor.
C. To provide a list of all official meetings.
D. To document all the money spent by an officer.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: The handing-over note is a detailed document that summarizes all the projects,
files, and responsibilities that an officer is handing over to their successor.
