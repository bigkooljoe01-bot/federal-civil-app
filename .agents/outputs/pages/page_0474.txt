19. What is the purpose of preparing an "Agenda"?
A. To make the meeting longer.
B. To make the meeting more complex.

20. How should the agenda be structured?
A. In no particular order.
B. By placing the most controversial items first.
C. Logically, starting with opening formalities and ending with closing formalities.
D. By placing the most junior members’ items first.
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: The Agenda should be structured logically, starting with opening formalities,
moving to the main business, and ending with closing formalities.
