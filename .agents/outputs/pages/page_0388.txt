70. Which of the following is NOT a function of the Bureau of Public Procurement?

A. Publicizing and explaining the provisions of the Act

B. Certifying federal procurement prior to contract awards

C. Directly implementing government projects

D. Publishing the details of major contracts in the procurement journal

Correct Answer: C.

Explanation: The functions of the Bureau include publicizing the Act, certifying procurement,

and publishing contract details, but not directly implementing projects.

71. The Bureau of Public Procurement is responsible for maintaining a national database of

what?

A. Standard process prices

B. All government employees

C. All foreign contractors

D. Intemational trade agreements

Correct Answer: A.

Explanation: The Bureau's functions include monitoring the prices of tendered items and

keeping a national database of standard process prices.
