Part 2: Financial Regulations: Custody of Public Moneys, Stamps, Security Books, and
Documents
41. What is the primary instruction regarding the use of banking facilities for public funds?
A. All cash must be kept in the office safe.
B. The fullest possible use shall be made of banking facilities, and all cash in excess of daily
requirements shall be paid into the bank account immediately.
C. Banking facilties are only for large transactions.
D. Cash should be held for at least a week before banking.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1101 states, "The fullest possible use shall be made of
banking facilities and all cash in excess of daily requirements shall be paid into the bank
account immediately.”
42. For the custody of cash, stamps, or receipt and licence books beyond immediate
requirements, what security measure is required for strong-rooms or reserve cash safes?
A. Asingle lock.
B. Not less than two locks, with keys held by different officers.
C. A simple padlock.
D. No locks are necessary if the room is secure.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1102 mandates that strong-rooms or reserve cash safes
should be "secured if possible by not less than two locks, the key to one being held by the
cashier and the key to the other by a more senior official than the cashier.”
43. What type of safe is required for the safe custody of the daily working balance of cash,
stamps, and receipt/licence books during office hours?
A. A portable safe.
B. A safe that is built into the wall of the building or securely attached to its structure.
C. Any safe available.
D. A fireproof cabinet.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1103 specifies that such safes "should be built in to the wall
of the building or otherwise securely attached to the structure of the building."
