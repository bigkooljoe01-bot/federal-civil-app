20. What are the two main types of Imprests?
A. Daily and Monthly Imprests.
B. Cash and Cheque Imprests.
C. Standing and Special Imprests.
D. Personal and Official Imprests.
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1004(i) identifies the two types as *(a) Standing Imprests"
and *(b) Special imprests.”
21. Which type of imprest can be replenished from time to time during a financial year?
A. Special Imprest.
B. Standing Imprest.
C. Emergency Imprest.
D. Capital Imprest.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1004(i)(a) describes Standing Imprests as those "which may
be replenished from time to time during a financial year by the submission of paid vouchers to
‘Sub-Accounting Officers for reimbursement.”
22. When must a Special Imprest be retired in full?
A. At the end of the financial year.
B. Monthly, regardless of purpose.
C. Immediately the purpose for which it was granted has been achieved.
D. When the imprest holder is transferred.
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1004(i)(b) states that Special Imprests "must be retired in full
when the purpose has been achieved.”
23. What is explicitly prohibited regarding the use of personal advances in disguise of Special
Imprest?
A. Using them for official travel.
B. Using them in the procurement of stores.
C. Using them for minor office repairs.
D. Using them for staff welfare.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1004(ii) explicitly states: "On no account shall personal
advance be used in disguise of Special Imprest, in the procurement of stores which is governed
by the provision of chapter 23 of these Regulations."
