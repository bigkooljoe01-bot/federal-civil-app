87. The Principle of Quality, Effectiveness, and Efficiency requires the public service to make
optimal use of what?
A. Political connections.
B. The resources at its disposal.
C. Personal favors.
D. Obsolete technology.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: The public service shall ensure that the highest quality and the most effective and
efficient services are provided by making optimal use of the resources at its disposal.
88. What should adjustments to the way services are organized and operated lead to?
A. Fewer services for the public.
B. More bureaucracy.
C. Better service delivery.
D. Increased costs.
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: Adjustments to the way in which services are organized and operated must lead
to better service delivery and must be informed by best practices in the application of the
information and communication technologies.
89. How should the evaluation of services be based?
A. On personal opinions.
B. On objectives and programmes of activities defined beforehand, accompanied by
performance indicators and criteria18.
C. On the number of complaints received.
D. On the amount of money spent.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: The evaluation of the effectiveness and productivity of services shall be based on
objectives and programmes of activities defined beforehand, accompanied by performance
indicators and criteria.
