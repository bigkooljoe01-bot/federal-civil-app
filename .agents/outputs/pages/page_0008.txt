4. Who is encouraged to study and imbibe these rules and their associated and complementary
instructions, guidelines, and extant circulars?
A. Only Chief Executive Officers (CEOs).
B. All Public Servants.

extant Circulars..."
5. How often shall the Public Service Rules (PSR) be reviewed?
A. Every two years.
B. Every three years.
