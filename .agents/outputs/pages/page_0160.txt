63. What is the maximum interval for a Revenue Collector's cash book to be produced for
examination under normal circumstances?
A. Two weeks
B. One month
C. Three months
D. Six months
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 211 states that unless there are exceptional circumstances,
the interval for examination shall not be greater than one month.
64. What kind of checks should the Accountant-General or Accounting Officer use to
supplement routine checks on Revenue Collectors’ cash books?
A. Written correspondence
B. Surprise inspections at the offices concerned
C. Telephone calls
D. Email reminders
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 211 states that the Accountant-General or Accounting Officer
shall supplement routine checks with surprise inspections at the offices.
