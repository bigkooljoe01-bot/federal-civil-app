73. What is the maximum period an officer can be seconded to another government or
recognized international organization?
A. Two years in the first instance, with extensions up to four years.
B. One year in the first instance, with extensions up to two years.
C. Three years with no extension.
D. Four years with no extension.
Correct Answer: A
Explanation: Rule 020503(v) states that secondment can be for a maximum period of two
years in the first instance and, with extensions, not to exceed four years“.
74. During a period of secondment, what happens to the officer's substantive post and
promotion prospects?
A. The officer forfeits their substantive post and promotion.
B. The officer's substantive post is held in abeyance, and they are not entitled to promotion.
C. The officer's substantive post is held, and they are entitled to be treated as having been
posted on special duty, with their promotion prospects maintained.
D. The officer is permanently transferred to the new organization.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Rule 020503(h) states that during secondment, the officer's substantive post
“shall continue to be held, and he/she will be entitled to increment and promotion and will be
treated as having been posted on special duty.”

D. The officer is placed on a probationary period in the new grade.

transfer-on-promotion to any MDA, except in response to an advertised post‘.
