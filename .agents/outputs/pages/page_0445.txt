98. A key part of the Principle of Motivation is that employees must be provided with what?
A. A pleasant and favorable working environment35.
B. An opportunity to make their own rules.
C. More power than their superiors.
D. A chance to work from home exclusively.
Correct Answer: A.
Explanation: The administration shall ensure that employees are provided with a pleasant and
favorable working environment so that they can realize their full potential.
99. The Principle of Integrity states that public officers must be known for what?
A. Their ability to make a lot of money.
B. Their political connections.
C. Their high standards of morality and integrity37.
D. Their willingness to accept bribes.
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: The Principle of Integrity states that public officers must be known for their high
standards of morality and integrity in the conduct of official business.
100. What is prohibited under the Principle of Integrity?
A. Working hard.
B. Exercising leadership.
C. Nepotism, corruption, influence peddling, and illicit enrichment39.
D. Being polite to the public.
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: It prohibits the use of public office for personal gain, nepotism, corruption,
influence peddling and illicit enrichment.
