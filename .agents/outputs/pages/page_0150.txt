23. What is the Accountant-General's role conceming the accounting policy of the federal
government?
A. To implement policies set by the Minister of Finance.
B. To formulate the accounting policy.
C. To audit the accounting policy.
D. To advise on the accounting policy.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 107(0) states the Accountant-General shall "formulate the
accounting policy of the federal government.”
24. Who is the officer responsible under the Constitution for the audit and report on the public
accounts of the federation?
A. The Accountant-General for the Federation.
B. The Minister of Finance.
C. The Auditor-General for the Federation.
D. The Public Accounts Committee.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 108 identifies "the Auditor-General for the Federation” as this
officer.
25. The Auditor-General's examination of accounts aims to ascertain whether:
A. Accounts have been properly kept.
B. Public monies have been fully accounted for.
C. Monies have been expended for appropriated purposes.
D. All of the above.
Correct Answer: D
Explanation: Financial Regulation 108 lists all these as aspects the Auditor-General shall
ascertain.
26. Which of the following is a statutory function of the Auditor-General?
A. Financial Audit.
B. Appropriation Audit.
C. Financial Control Audit.
D. All of the above.
Correct Answer: D
Explanation: Financial Regulation 109(j) lists all these as statutory functions of the
