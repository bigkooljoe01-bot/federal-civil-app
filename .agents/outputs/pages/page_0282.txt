39. What is one of the responsibilities of the National Pension Commission regarding
accounting records?
A. To destroy accounting records after five years.
B. To maintain accounting records for contributions and ensure proper periodic reconciliation.
C. To delegate all accounting responsibilities to ministries.
D. To only report to the Auditor-General.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1904(c) states that the Commission shall maintain
accounting records for the contributions and ensure proper periodic reconciliation of the
accounts.
40. How often should the National Pension Commission generate and distribute Retirement
Savings Account (RSA) statements to contributing employees?
A. Annually.
B. Bi-annually.
C. Quarterly.
D. Monthly.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1904(d) mandates that on a quarterly basis, the commission
shall generate and distribute Retirement Savings Account (RSA) statements to each
contributing employee.

B. Senior Accountant.
C. Director. 11
