147. What is the timing for an officer on a non-pensionable appointment to express their desire
for further employment?
A. One month before the expiration of their agreement.
B. Four months before the date of expiration of their agreement.
C. Three months before the date of expiration of their agreement.
D. Six months before the date of expiration of their agreement.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Rule 021212 states that the person engaged shall give a written notice to the
government stating their desire to remain in employment "four months before the date of
expiration of agreement.
148. What is one of the conditions for a non-pensionable officer to be granted leave?
A. The rules do not provide for leave.
B. They are entitled to annual leave as per the rules.
C. They are entitled to leave in accordance with the provisions of Chapter Twelve of the Rules.
D. The leave is at the discretion of the Permanent Secretary.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Rule 021214 states that the person engaged "shall be entitled to leave in
accordance with the provisions of Chapter Twelve.
149. What is the legal requirement for a non-pensionable officer to make good any pecuniary
damage arising from their negligence or failure to comply with an order?
A. The officer is not liable.
B. The officer is liable to have a deduction from their salary to make good the damage.
C. The officer must pay the full amount upfront.
D. The officer must be terminated.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Rule 021211 states that in the event of any pecuniary damage arising from the
person engaged disregarding or failing to comply with an order, "he may be liable to a deduction
from his salary to make good the damage or any part thereof.
