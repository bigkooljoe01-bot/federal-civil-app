8. What document must be submitted to the Accountant-General when applying for a variation
of an existing accounting procedure or introducing a new one?
A. A budget proposal.
B. A detailed flow chart.
C. An audit report.
D. A list of personnel.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 506 states: "A detailed flow chart shall be submitted to the
Accountant General when application is being made for a variation of an existing procedure or
introduction of a procedure to a new office.”
9. To whom should the approved Accounting Code, Internal Audit Guide, and Treasury Circulars
be circulated within a ministry or self-accounting unit?
A. Only to the Accounting Officer.
B. Only to the Head of Finance and Accounts.
C. To all officers within the ministry, extra-ministerial office and other arms of government for
their guidance and compliance.
D. Only to the internal audit unit
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 507 states: "The approved Accounting Code and Internal
Audit Guide of ministries/extra-ministerial offices and other arms of government or self
accounting unit as well as Treasury circulars issued by the Accountant-General shall be
circulated to all officers within the ministry, extra-ministerial office and other arms of government
for their guidance and compliance.”
10. What is the relationship between an approved accounting code and the Financial
Regulations if there is a conflict of detail?
A. The Financial Regulations always prevail.
B. The accounting code shall prevail.
C. The Minister of Finance decides.
D. The Auditor-General decides.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 201 (which sets the precedent for accounting codes) states:
"Where the approved code is at variance with these regulations, the code will prevail.” This
principle is implied for all accounting codes.
