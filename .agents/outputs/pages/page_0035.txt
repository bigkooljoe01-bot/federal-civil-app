107. What is the minimum number of years of service required for an officer to be eligible for the
post of Permanent Secretary?
A. 15 years
B. 17 years
C. 20 years
D. The document does not explicitly state a number of years.
Correct Answer: D
Explanation: Rule 020811 outlines the eligibility criteria for Permanent Secretaries but does not
specify a minimum number of years of service.
108. What is one of the eligibility criteria for the post of Permanent Secretary?
A. Must be a Director on Grade Level (GL) 17.
B. Must be on GL. 16.
C. Must have a master's degree.
D. Must have been confirmed in their appointment.
Correct Answer: A
Explanation: Rule 020811(a) states that a candidate must be a "Director on Grade Level (GL)
17 and in the mainstream of the Federal Civil Service."
109. Who serves as resource persons during the selection process for permanent secretaries?
A. The Head of the Civil Service of the Federation.
B. The Permanent Secretary.
C. The Civil/Public Service and Private institutions.
D. The Federal Civil Service Commission.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Rule 020813 states that "Resource persons from the Civil/Public Service and
Private Institutions may be involved in the selection process."
