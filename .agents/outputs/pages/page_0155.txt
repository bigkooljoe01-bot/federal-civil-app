44. Who is an "Officer Controlling Expenditure"?
A. The Accountant-General of the Federation.
B. The officer to whom the control of a subhead or specified amount has been delegated by the
appropriate Accounting Officer.
C. The Minister of Finance.
D. Any officer who signs payment vouchers.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 121 defines an "Officer Controlling Expenditure” as “the
officer to whom the control of a sub-head, or specified amount of a sub-head, has been
delegated by the appropriate Accounting Officer.”
45. When a Ministry or Department is constituted as a Self Accounting Unit, who is charged with
the installation and maintenance of a proper system of accounts for that unit?
A. The Accountant-General.
B. The Auditor-General.
C. The Accounting Officer.
D. The Head of Finance and Accounts.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 122 states that “the Accounting Officer as defined in
Financial Regulation 111 is charged, in relation to that unit, with the installation and maintenance
of a proper system of accounts.”
46. Which of the following is a general duty of public officers with monetary or financial
responsibilities?
A. To ensure prompt collection of public revenue.
B. To promptly bring to account all moneys under proper Heads and Sub-heads.
C. To ensure proper provision for safekeeping of public moneys and documents.
D. All of the above.
Correct Answer: D
Explanation: Financial Regulation 123(j) lists all these as general duties.
