91. What is the mandatory training period for newly appointed Foreign Service Officers at the
Foreign Service Academy?
A. Three months.
B. Six months.
C. One year.
D. Two years.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: All new Foreign Service Officers must attend a mandatory one-year training
course in diplomatic practice at the Foreign Service Academy.
92. What is the consequence for an officer who fails the Foreign Service Academy training
course after two attempts?
A. They are redeployed to a home ministry.
B. They are given a final third attempt.
C. They are relieved of their appointment.
D. They are placed on a longer probation period.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: If an officer fails to pass the training course at the Foreign Service Academy after
two attempts, they will be relieved of their appointment.
93. What is the rule regarding married Foreign Service Officers and their spouses?
A. Their spouses must also be employed in the Civil Service.
B. The officer must get a government-issued marriage certificate.
C. They are expected to provide full particulars of their spouses for registration.
D. Spouses are required to undergo a separate training program.
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: All married officers in the Foreign Service are required to provide full details of
their spouses for registration purposes.
94. What is required if a Foreign Service Officer intends to marry a foreigner?
A. They must get a promotion first.
B. They must obtain the prior permission of the Government10.
C. The officer must resign from the service.
D. The officer's spouse must become a Nigerian citizen.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: An officer who wants to marry a foreigner must first seek the government's
permission.
