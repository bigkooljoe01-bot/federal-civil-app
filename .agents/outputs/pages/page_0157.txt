Part 2: Financial Regulations: “Revenue Collection and Accounting,”
51. Who is responsible for ensuring the full collection of all government revenues and for
promptly bringing them to account?
A. The Minister of Finance
B. The Accountant-General
C. The Auditor-General
D. The Accounting Officer
Correct Answer: D
Explanation: Financial Regulation 201 explicitly states that Accounting Officers are responsible
for the full collection of all revenues due to the government and for their prompt lodgment and
accounting.
52. According to Financial Regulation 202, what must an officer receiving public money issue for
every sum of money paid to the goverment?
A. A verbal confirmation
B. A signed declaration
C. An official receipt
D. A bank deposit slip
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 202 mandates that an official receipt shall be issued by the
officer receiving public money for every sum paid to the government.
53. What information should an official receipt contain, as per Financial Regulation 204?
A. Only the amount of money received
B. The date, amount, and the name of the officer receiving it
C. The date, the name of the person paying, and a brief description of the transaction
D. The date, amount, and a brief description of the transaction
Correct Answer: D
Explanation: Financial Regulation 204 requires that official receipts must show the date, the
amount received, and a brief description of the transaction for which payment is being made.
