Part 2: Financial Regulations: Expenditure Classification and Control
51. Who is primarily responsible for making disbursements of public funds?
A. Only the Accountant-General
B. The Accountant-General and Accounting Officers.
C. Only Accounting Officers.
D. The Minister of Finance.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 401(i) states: "All disbursements of public funds shall be
made by the Accountant General on the authority of the appropriate Warrant issued to him and
also by Accounting Officers entrusted with the expenditure of public fund falling within the votes
of charge of their ministries or extra ministerial offices and other arms. of government.”
52. Who retains the ultimate responsibility for the control of votes to the Federal Executive
Council?
A. The Minister of Finance.
B. The Accountant-General.
C. Accounting Officers.
D. Officers controlling votes.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 401(ii) states: “the ultimate responsibility to the Federal
Executive Council for the control of votes rests with the Account log Officers at all times."
53. What is the personal consequence for any officer making, allowing, or directing any
disbursement without proper authority?
A. They will receive a warning.
B. They will be personally responsible for the amount involved
C. Their subordinates will be held responsible.
D. The expenditure will be absorbed by the Contingencies Fund
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 401(ii) states: "any officer making, allowing or directing any
disbursement without proper authority shall be personally responsible for the amount involved."
