5. When should the handing over note be written?
A. After the successor has taken over.
B. Before the officer proceeds on leave, transfer or retirement.
C. During the officer's leave.
D. Only when requested by a superior officer.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: It is essential that an officer who is about to proceed on leave, transfer or retire
should prepare a detailed and comprehensive handing over note.
6. Who should sign the original and copies of the Handing Over Notes?
A. Only the Officer Handing Over.
B. Only the Officer Taking Over.
C. Both the Officer Handing Over and the Officer Taking Over.
D. The Head of Department.
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: The original and the two copies of the Notes should be signed by both the Officer
Handing Over and the Officer Taking Over.
7. What is the purpose of passing the filed original of the Handing Over Notes to a superior
officer?
A. To keep it confidential.
B. To ensure that the duties of that post have been properly taken over.
C. To allow them to rewrite it.
D. To discard it.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: The filed original should then be passed to the Supervisor and/or a superior officer
who should know that the duties of that post have been properly taken over.
8. What should be the first entry in a handing over note?
A. A summary of the officer's personal life.
B. The names of the most important people the officer knows.
C. Details regarding the contents of the schedule of duties of the post.
D. A list of all files in the office.
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: Handing Over Notes may include the following: (i) Details regarding the contents
of the schedule of duties of the post.
