13. Can expenditure incurred under the Contingency Fund procedure be charged directly to the
Contingency Fund?
A. Yes, always.
B. Yes, with special approval
C. No, never.
D. Only for minor expenses.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 307 explicitly states: “In no circumstances will expenditure
incurred under this Contingency Fund procedure be charged, or the relevant vouchers classified
directly, to' the Contingency Fund."
14. What is the authority for the Accountant-General to transfer sums appropriated from the
Consolidated Revenue Fund to the Contingency Fund for replenishment?
A. Provisional General Warrant.
B. Supplementary General Warrant.
C. Virement Warrant.
D. Development Fund General Warrant.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 308 states: "A Supplementary General Warrant shall be the
authority for the Accountant-General to transfer any sum appropriated from the Consolidated
Revenue Fund to the Contingency Fund by way of replenishment.”
15. When is a Virement Warrant issued?
A. When funds are required for a new project.
B. When additional provision is required under a sub-head and equivalent savings can be made
under another sub-head of the same Head.
C. To authorize expenditure from the Contingencies Fund.
D. At the beginning of each financial year.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 309 defines a Virement Warrant as being issued "when...
additional provision is required under a particular sub-head (or a new sub-head is required)
while, at the same time, equivalent savings can be made under another sub- head of the same
Head."
