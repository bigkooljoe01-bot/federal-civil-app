86. What is the purpose of the Bureau's review of the procurement and award of contract

procedures of every entity to which the Act applies?

A. To reduce the number of entities

B. To increase the cost of contracts

C. To ensure compliance with the Act

D. To eliminate all contracts

Correct Answer: C.

Explanation: The Bureau's function is to review the procurement and award of contract

procedures of every entity to which the Act applies.

87. According to the document, what is the role of the Bureau of Public Procurement in the

process of contract awards?

A. It awards all contracts directly.

B. It certifies federal procurement prior to the award of contracts.

C. It sets the final price for all contracts.

D. It only monitors projects after they have been awarded.

Correct Answer: B.

Explanation: A main function of the Bureau is to "subject to thresholds as may be set by the

Council, certify Federal procurement prior to the award of contract".

88. What is a key function of the Bureau regarding major contracts?

A. To keep them secret from the public

B. To publish their details in the procurement journal

C. To award them to the highest bidder

D. To allow Ministries to handle them independently

Correct Answer: B.

Explanation: A function of the Bureau is to publish the details of major contracts in the

procurement journal.
