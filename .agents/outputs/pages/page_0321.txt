COMPUTER-BASED TEST (CBT) PROMOTION EXAMINATION FOR OFFICERS IN
GRADES 15 TO 17 IN THE FEDERAL CIVIL SERVICE OF THE FEDERATION ON
EINANCIAL REGULATION.
Instruction: This examination consists of 100 multiple-choice questions designed to assess your
readiness for promotion to the next level in the Federal Civil Service. You have exactly one
hour (60 minutes) to complete all questions; manage your time wisely to ensure you attempt
every question. Read each question carefully before selecting the best answer from the options.
1. Who is the "Chief Accounting Officer of the Federation"?
A. The Auditor-General of the Federation
B. The Minister of Finance
C. The Accountant-General of the Federation
D. The Permanent Secretary, Federal Ministry of Finance
2. According to Financial Regulation 107, who is responsible for the overall supervision of the
accounts of the Federal Government?
A. The Auditor-General of the Federation
B. The Minister of Finance
C. The Accountant-General of the Federation
D. The National Assembly
3. What is the primary responsibility of a Permanent Secretary or Head of Extra-Ministerial
Office, as an Accounting Officer?
A. To personally audit all accounts.
B. To manage all personnel issues.
C. To ensure the regularity and propriety in the expenditure of public funds.
D. To approve all foreign loans.
4. What must be provided for every sum of money paid to the government?
A. A verbal acknowledgment
B. A handwritten note
C. An official, numbered receipt
D. A copy of the bank statement
5. What is the rule regarding the use of Revenue Collector's Cash Books?
A. Only the Revenue Collector is allowed to handle it.
B. It can be used by anyone in the office.
C. It must be signed by the Accounting Officer daily.
D. It must be kept in a safe place.
