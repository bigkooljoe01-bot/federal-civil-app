76. What is the condition for a transfer to be approved?

A. The applicant must have a clean record.

B. The applicant must have served for a minimum of two years.

C. The applicant must be confirmed in their present appointment.

D. All of the above.

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Rule 020507(i) states that the applicant must be confirmed in their present

appointment”.

77. What is the requirement for Permanent Secretaries/Heads of Extra-Ministerial Offices

regarding transfers on junior posts?

A. They must notify the Federal Civil Service Commission immediately.

B. They must not accept any staff seconded without the endorsement of the Federal Civil

Service Commission/Board.

C. They must obtain approval from the Head of the Civil Service of the Federation.

D. They must conduct an interview for the staff

Correct Answer: B.

Explanation: Rule 020507(j) states that "No Ministry shall accept any staff seconded to it

without the endorsement of the Federal Civil Service Commission/Board."

78. Who is responsible for paying the salary of an officer on secondment?

A. The parent MDA that released the officer.

B. The MDA where the officer is seconded to.

C. The Office of the Head of Civil Service of the Federation.

D. Both MDAs share the cost.

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Rule 020504 states that the MDA with the higher salary shall be responsible for

the payment of the salary and non-salary allowances should be paid by the receiving MDA".
