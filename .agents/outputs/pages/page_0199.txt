12. Who is enjoined to acquaint themselves with these Regulations, the Finance (Control and
Management) Act, and relevant Sections of the Constitution dealing with public finance?
A. Only Ministers.
B. Only the Accountant-General.
C. Public Officers.
D. Only External Auditors.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: The Preface states: "Public Officers. are enjoined to acquaint themselves with
these Regulations, the Finance (Control and Management) Act, Cap.144 Laws of the federation
of Nigeria 1990, as well as the relevant Sections of the Constitution dealing with public finance.
for proper guidance.”
13. What does the Finance (Control and Management) Act Cap 144 Laws of the Federation,
1990, regulate?
A. Only the procurement of goods.
B. Certain financial matters of the government.
C. Only the salaries of public officers.
D. International trade agreements.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 104 states: "Certain financial matters of government are
regulated by the laws of the Federation enacted by the National Assembly. These include the
Finance (Control and Management) Act CAP 144 Laws of the Federation of Nigeria, 1990..."
