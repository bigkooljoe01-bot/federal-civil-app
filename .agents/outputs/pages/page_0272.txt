99. What is the implication of the Accounting Officer ensuring payments are made when due
and that over-payments are not made?
A. They are only concerned with timely payments.
B. They are responsible for the accuracy and timeliness of payments.
C. They delegate all payment responsibilities.
D. They only focus on preventing fraud.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: This responsibility encompasses both the punctuality and correctness of
payments, indicating a dual focus on timeliness and accuracy.
100. What type of reporting must the Accounting Officer ensure is accurate for management and
control purposes?
A. Informal verbal reports.
B. Comprehensive financial reports including budget performance.
C. Only external audit reports.
D. Quarterly reports only.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The Accounting Officer is responsible for budget proposals, defense, and
monthly/quarterly budget performance statements, all indicating comprehensive financial
reporting.
