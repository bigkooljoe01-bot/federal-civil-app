22. What is the purpose of an "Absent File card" or a "card slip"?

23. What should a Civil Servant do with a file when they are finished with it?
A. Keep it in their desk for future reference.
B. Putit on a "bring-up" pad if action is required at a later date.
C. Throw it away if it is not needed anymore.
D. Pass it to a colleague without informing them.
Correct Answer:B.
Explanation: When an officer has finished with a file, he should ensure that it is returned to the
registry. If a file is required at a later date, it should be put on a "bring-up” pad.
