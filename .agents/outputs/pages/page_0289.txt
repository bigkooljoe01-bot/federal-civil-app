67. What action should be taken by the Head of Division or Unit when a government vehicle is
involved in an accident?
A. Ignore it if there is no major damage.
B. Report it immediately to the Accounting Officer.
C. Take the vehicle for repairs without reporting.
D. Attempt to resolve the matter privately.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 2011 requires the Head of Division or Unit to report vehicle
accidents immediately to the Accounting Officer.
68. What document must the Accounting Officer ensure is filled out immediately after a
government vehicle accident?
A. An accident claim form from the insurance company.
B. A Report of Loss of Government Funds or Stores (Treasury Form 146).
C/ A police incident report.
D. A vehicle repair estimate.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 2012 requires the Accounting Officer to ensure that a Report
of Loss of Government Funds or Stores (Treasury Form 146) is filled out immediately.
69. What is the role of the Mechanical Engineer after a government vehicle accident?
A. To determine fault.
B. To inspect the vehicle and prepare an estimate of the cost of repairs.
C. To contact the insurance company.
D. To dispose of the damaged vehicle.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 2013 states that the Mechanical Engineer shall inspect the
vehicle and prepare an estimate of the cost of repairs.
