Chapter 2: Public Service Rules (PSR)
This chapter is your guide to the fundamental principles and regulations that govern the conduct
of all civil servants. It dissects the Public Service Rules (PSR), covering everything from
appointment and discipline to promotions and retirement. A solid understanding of these rules is
non-negotiable for a director, as you will be responsible for enforcing them. The questions in this
chapter are designed to test your comprehension and application of the PSR in various
scenarios. This chapter contains sections A to F. Section A which has part 1 to 20 covers the
first three chapters of the Public Service Rule which is core to the service and contains
questions, answers, and explanations to the selected answers from the option A to D in the
question. While section B to F is designed to help students practice objective questions that will
be featured in computer-based test (CBT) examination.
Section A:
Part 1: Introduction and Application
1. What is the primary purpose of the Public Service Rules (PSR) as stated in the foreword?
A. To define the employment relationship between employees in the Public Service and the
Government.
B. To serve as a guide for private sector employees.
C. To outline international trade regulations.
D. To provide a historical overview of governance.
Correct Answer: A
Explanation: The Foreword explicitly states that "Public Service Rules (PSR) define the
employment relationship between employees in the Public Service and the Government."
2. The 2021 Edition of the Public Service Rules is a review of which previous edition?
A. 2005 Edition
B. 2008 Edition
C. 2010 Edition
D. 2015 Edition
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The foreword mentions, “This edition of the Public Service Rules is therefore a
review of the 2008 edition.”
3. What emerging issues are reflected in the 2021 edition of the Public Service Rules?
A. Only financial management systems.
B. Performance Management System, Talent Sourcing, Volunteerism, Virtual
Meetings/Engagements.
C. Only disciplinary procedures.
