COMPUTER-BASED TEST (CBT) PROMOTION EXAMINATION FOR OFFICERS IN
GRADES 15 TO 17 IN THE FEDERAL CIVIL SERVICE OF THE FEDERATION ON
EINANCIAL REGULATION
Instruction: This examination consists of 100 multiple-choice questions designed to assess your
readiness for promotion to the next level in the Federal Civil Service. You have exactly one
hour (60 minutes) to complete all questions; manage your time wisely to ensure you attempt
every question. Read each question carefully before selecting the best answer from the options.
1. Who is ultimately responsible for the efficient and economical use of government vehicles
within a Ministry, Extra-Ministerial Office, or other arm of government?
A. The Head Driver
B. The Accounting Officer
C. The Transport Officer
D. The Mechanical Engineer
2. What is the primary duty of an Internal Auditor, as per Financial Regulation 1703(d)?
A. To manage the department's budget.
B. To approve all payments.
C. To audit and certify all payment vouchers.
D. To handle all personnel issues.
3. What is the minimum number of members on a Board of Survey for Cash and Stamps,
according to FR 1802?
A. Two
B. Three
C. Four
D. Five
4. What is the purpose of a "Subsidiary Account Pension Unit" as stated in Financial Regulation
1905?
A. To handle staff welfare issues.
B. To function independently of the main accounts section to manage pension contributions.
C. To manage all government investments.
D. To provide loans to employees.
5. What is the main principle regarding the use of government vehicles as per FR 2001?
A. They can be used for personal errands with permission
B. They are provided for the official duties of the government.
C. They are for the exclusive use of senior officials.
D. They can be rented out to the public for a fee.
