42. What must all Accounting Officers ensure is created in their ministries/extra-ministerial
offices regarding pensions?
A. A main accounts section.
B. A Subsidiary Account Pension Unit.
C. An external audit department.
D. A human resources department.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1905 states that all Accounting Officers shall ensure that a
Subsidiary Account Pension Unit is created.
43. How should the Subsidiary Account Pension Unit function in relation to the main subsidiary
accounts section of ministries?
A. It should be integrated with the main section.
B. It should function independently. 15
C. It should report directly to the Accountant-General.
D. It should be supervised by the main accounts section.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1905 states that the section shall function independently of
the main subsidiary accounts section.
44. Who allocates a Below-the-Line Classification code for the recording of monthly pension
contributions?
A. The Budget Office.
B. The Director of Finance and Accounts.
C. The Accountant-General. 17
D. The National Pension Commission.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1906 states that the Accountant-General shall allocate a
Below-the-Line Classification code.
45. On receipt of monthly personnel cost warrants, what should the Director of Finance and
Accounts ensure is issued?
A. A payment advice to the Central Bank of Nigeria.
B. A Treasury Receipt Book 6 in respect of the gross amount on the warrant. 19
C. A bank statement.
D. An audit report.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1907 states that on receipt of monthly personnel cost
warrants, the Director of Finance and Accounts shall ensure the issuance of Treasury Receipt
Book 6.
