Part 5: Rules for Appointment on Probation
51. What is the typical period of probation for an officer newly appointed to the service?
A. Two years
B. Not less than six months and not more than two years.
C. Three years
D. One year
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Rule 020301 specifies that officers on probation are required to serve for two
years, but this period can be reduced to not less than six months or extended up to two years.
52. What happens to an officer's appointment if they do not pass their prescribed examination
within the probationary period?
A. The probationary period is extended.
B. Their appointment will be confirmed.
C. Their appointment may be terminated.
D. They will be demoted.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Rule 020303 states that an officer on probation is required to pass the prescribed
examination, and if they fail to do so, their appointment may be terminated at the end of the
probationary period.
53. Which body is responsible for making recommendations regarding the confirmation of
officers holding senior posts?
A. The Ministry/Extra-Ministerial Office
B. The Federal Civil Service Commission
C. The Permanent Secretary/Head of Extra-Ministerial Office
D. The Head of the Civil Service of the Federation
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: Rule 020304 states that recommendations for the confirmation of officers holding
senior posts shall be made by the Permanent Secretary/Head of Extra-Ministerial Office.
