87. Question: If a cashier or officer temporarily leaves receipt books with another officer, who is
held responsible for any misuse?
A. The temporary officer.
B. The cashier or officer who left the receipts.
C. The Head of Accounts.
D. The Accounting Officer.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1212(iii) clearly states, "Such cashiers or officers who leave
the receipts so issued to them with another officer on a temporary basis shall be held
responsible for any misuse."
88. Can receipt and licence books be transferred between Sub-Accounting Officers?
A. Yes, at any time.
B. Yes, with the consent of the Accounting Officer, or in special emergency cases with reporting.
C. No, never.
D. Only if the books are unused.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1213 allows transfers “with the consent of the Accounting
Officer. except in special cases of emergency when the circumstances will be reported to the
issuing officer.”
89. Can used or unused receipt or licence books be transferred between one Revenue Collector
and another?
A. Yes, always.
B. Only if one Revenue Collector is officially relieved by another, or with the approval of the
Accountant-General.
C. No, under no circumstances.
D. Only if the books are unused.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1214 permits this "Except when one Revenue collector is
officially relieved by another. no used or unused receipt or licence books shall be transferred
between one Revenue Collector and another except with the approval of the Accountant
General."
90. What action must be taken when an incorrect entry is made on a receipt or licence?
A. The incorrect entry should be crossed out and corrected.
B. The receipt or licence shall be cancelled and a new one completed.
C. The receipt or licence should be filed as is.
D. The issuing officer should be surcharged.
Correct Answer: B.
