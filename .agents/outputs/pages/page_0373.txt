8. What is the role of the Legislature regarding laws made by the government?
A. Itimplements the laws.
B. It makes the laws but does not implement them.
C. It adjudicates on the laws.
D. It only approves the budget.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Though the Legislature approves the Budget and makes the laws, it does not
implement such laws. That duty belongs to the Executive.
9. Which of these is an exclusive responsibility of the Federal Goverment, according to the
handbook?
A. Education
B. Health
C. Foreign Affairs
D. Roads
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: The Federal Government has exclusive responsibility for specified matters such
as Foreign Affairs.
10. Matters on which both the Federal and State Governments share responsibilities are listed
in which part of the Constitution?
A. Exclusive List
B. Residual Powers
C. Concurrent List
D. Legislative List
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: These areas which appear in the Concurrent List in the Constitution, include
Health, Education, Roads etc..
