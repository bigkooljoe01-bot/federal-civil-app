13. Who, according to the document, is responsible for the proper keeping of records in the Civil
Service?
A. Only the Permanent Secretary.
B. Only the staff of the Records/Registry Unit.
C. All Civil Servants, regardless of their position.
D. Only the Head of the Civil Service of the Federation.
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: The proper keeping of records is the responsibility of every Civil Servant,
irrespective of their position.
14. What is the term for a document created in response to an instruction or a matter referred to
an officer?
A. A letter.
B. A memorandum.
C. A minute.
D. A note.
Correct Answer:C.
Explanation: A minute is a note or document that is created in response to an instruction or a
matter referred to an officer for his attention, advice, or action.
15. A minute should contain a summary of the facts of the case, as well as what?
A. The officer's personal opinion.
B. The officer's recommendations and conclusions.
C. Alist of all staff involved.
D. A detailed financial analysis.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: A minute should contain a summary of the facts of the case, the officer's
recommendations, and his conclusions.
16. Which of the following is a key characteristic of a good minute?
A. It should be verbose and include all personal thoughts.
B. It should be written in a conversational tone.
C. It should be brief, factual, and free from grammatical errors.
D. It should only be written by senior officers.
Correct Answer:C.
Explanation: A good minute should be brief, factual, well-reasoned, and free from grammatical
errors, spelling mistakes, and ambiguity.
