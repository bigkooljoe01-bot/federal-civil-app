31. What is the purpose of a "handing-over note"?
A. To record the personal assets of an officer.
B. To detail the projects, files, and responsibilities of a position for a successor.
C. To provide a list of all official meetings.
D. To document all the money spent by an officer.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: A handing-over note is a detailed document that summarizes all the projects, files,
and responsibilities that an officer is handing over to their successor.
32. Who should sign a handing-over note?
A. Only the officer handing over.
B. Only the officer taking over.
C. Both the officer handing over and the officer taking over.
D. The Head of Department.
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: The original and the two copies of the notes should be signed by both the Officer
Handing Over and the Officer Taking Over.

A. To communicate with other Ministries and the public97.

D. To provide a confidential report.
