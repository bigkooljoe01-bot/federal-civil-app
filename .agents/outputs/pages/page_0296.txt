95. What is the role of the Storekeeper regarding the Store Ledger?
A. The Storekeeper is the only person allowed to make entries.
B. The Storekeeper is responsible for the proper keeping of the Store Ledger and other stores
records.
C. The Storekeeper is not allowed to touch the Store Ledger.
D. The Storekeeper only approves the entries made by others.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 2107(a) assigns the responsibility for the proper keeping of
the Store Ledger and other stores records to the Storekeeper.
96. What is the rule regarding the use of store forms, as per FR 21087
A. They can be adapted to suit personal preferences.
B. The official store forms issued by the Accountant-General must be used without modification.
C. Any form can be used as long as it has a signature.
D. Electronic forms are preferred, and paper forms are obsolete.
Correct Answer: B

Accountant-General must be used without modification.
97. Who is responsible for ensuring that all stores are physically secure?
A. The Internal Auditor.
B. The Permanent Secretary or Head of Extra-Ministerial Office.
C. The Accountant-General.
D. The Storekeeper.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 2128(b) places the responsibility for the physical security of
all government stores on the Permanent Secretary or Head of Extra-Ministerial Office.
