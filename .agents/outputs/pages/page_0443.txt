91. What must the administration do when it takes a decision concerning a person?
A. Ignore the person's concerns.
B. Inform the person concemed, indicating the reasons for such decisions and stating legal
remedies where necessary.
C. Keep the decision confidential
D. Announce the decision to the public at large.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: The administration shall inform the person concerned of any decision taken
concerning him/her, indicating the reasons for such decisions and stating, where necessary, the
legal remedies open to him/her, should he/she decide to challenge the decision.
92. The public service must establish or strengthen reception and information units for users in
order to assist them in what?
A. Gaining access to services and in recording their views, suggestions or complaints24.
B. Filing lawsuits against the government.
C. Complaining about the service.
D. Making political contributions.
Correct Answer: A.
Explanation: The administration shall establish or strengthen reception and information units
for users in order to assist them in gaining access to services and in recording their views,
suggestions or complaints.
93. The Principle of Speed and Responsiveness states that the public service shall determine
and respect what?
A. The number of staff available.
B. The number of hours in a workday.
C. Deadlines in the delivery of its services.
D. The budget of the department.
