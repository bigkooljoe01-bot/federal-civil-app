COMPUTER-BASED TEST (CBT) PROMOTION EXAMINATION FOR OFFICERS IN
GRADES 15 TO 17 IN THE FEDERAL CIVIL SERVICE OF THE FEDERATION ON
CIVIL SERVICE HANDBOOK.
Instruction: This examination consists of 100 multiple-choice questions designed to assess your
readiness for promotion to the next level in the Federal Civil Service. You have exactly one
hour (60 minutes) to complete all questions; manage your time wisely to ensure you attempt
every question. Read each question carefully before selecting the best answer from the options.
1. What is the highest-ranking career officer in the Civil Service of the Federation?
A. The Minister
B. The Head of the Civil Service of the Federation
C. The Permanent Secretary
D. The Secretary to the Government of the Federation
2. What is the official term for the administrative head and accounting officer of a Federal
Ministry?
A. Minister
B Director-General
C. Permanent Secretary
D. Chairman
3. According to the handbook, who is responsible for the overall management of the Civil
Service and for advising the President on civil service matters?
A. The Federal Civil Service Commission
B. The Secretary to the Government of the Federation
C. The Head of the Civil Service of the Federation
D. The Minister
4. Which government body is responsible for making sure that rules and regulations relating to
the Civil Service are not contravened?
A. The Ministry of Finance
B. The Federal Civil Service Commission
C. The National Assembly
D. The Office of the Head of Civil Service of the Federation
5. What is the name of the administrative division within a Ministry that is responsible for all
matters relating to staff?
A. The General Administration Division
B. The Personnel Division
