56. What is the purpose of an “official letter” in the Civil Service?
A. To communicate with other Ministries and the public.
B. To communicate internally.
C. To document a personal conversation.
D. To provide a confidential report.
Correct Answer: A.
Explanation: Letters to other Ministries are always addressed to the Honourable Minister. An
official letter is used to communicate with other Ministries, Parastatals, and the general public
57. What is considered a very serious offense for a Civil Servant?
A. Taking a day off.
B. Unauthorized disclosure of any information acquired in the course of their duties 162.
C. Arriving late to a meeting.
D. Talking to a colleague.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Unauthorized disclosure of any information acquired in the course of their duties
is a very serious offence.
58. What is the purpose of the "Official Secret Act"?
A. To encourage civil servants to share all government information.
B. To prevent the leaking of government secrets to unauthorized persons.
C. To protect civil servants from prosecution.
D. To allow for the free flow of information to the public.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: The Official Secret Act is to prevent the leaking of government secrets to
unauthorized persons.
