42. Any payment due to or receipted by any government officer in his official capacity shall be
paid to whom?
A. Their personal bank account.
B. The Head of Finance and Accounts.
C. The Treasury.
D. The Accountant-General directly.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 619 states: "Any payment due to or receipted by any
government officer in his official capacity shall be paid to the Treasury for which official receipt
shall be obtained.”
43. What is the minimum amount for a receipt given for payment for goods supplied or services
rendered to be liable to N50.00 Stamp Duty?
A. N50.00.
B. N500.00.
C. N1,000.00.
D. N5,000.00.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 620(a)(i) states: "Receipts given for payment for goods
supplied or services rendered. If the amount is N1,000 or over a N50.00 stamp is required in
respect of each signature acknowledging an amount."
44. Which of the following payments is NOT exempt from Stamp Duty under all conditions?
A. Advances of salary.
B. Wages.
C. Payments for goods supplied or services rendered if the amount is N1,500.00.
D. Pensions, Gratuities, Compassionate Allowances.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 620(b)(i) states that payments for goods supplied or services
rendered are exempt if the amount is under N1,000.00. Therefore, N1,500.00 would be liable.
