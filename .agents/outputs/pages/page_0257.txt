39. What happens if an officer fails to ensure the “full repayment" of an advance, as per
Financial Regulation 14197
A. The advance is automatically converted to a grant
B. Disciplinary action may be taken
C. The outstanding balance is transferred to another department
D. The officer is granted an extension
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: While not explicitly stated in the provided snippet for FR 1419, failure to comply
with financial regulations generally leads to disciplinary action.
40. Which of these is NOT directly addressed under "Advances" in Chapter 14?
A Correspondence advance
B. Outstanding balance
C. Board of Survey
D. Recovery of advances
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: While the document mentions "Boards of Survey” in other chapters (e.g., Chapter
18 for Cash and Stamps), it is not listed as a sub-topic under "Advances" in Chapter 14
Part 3: Financial Regulation: Salaries
41. According to Financial Regulation 1501, what is the definition of "Salary"?
A. A bonus paid for good performance.
B. The personal emolument paid to an employee, usually monthly, for services rendered at a
predetermined rate.
C. A one-time payment for a special project.
D. An allowance for housing and transportation.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1501 defines salary as the personal emolument paid to an
employee, usually monthly, for services rendered at a predetermined rate of pay

A. They must be permanent staff.
B. They must be in the employment of the public service.
C. They must have worked for at least one year.
D. They must be on a management level.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1502 states that all officers in the employment of the public
service are eligible for the payment of salaries
