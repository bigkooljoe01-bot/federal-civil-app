68. When should an officer draft a reply to an item of correspondence?
A. Only when specifically instructed.
B. Where an item of correspondence requires a reply.
C. Only for complex issues.
D. Never, as it is the Secretariat's job.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Where an item of correspondence requires a reply, it is the duty of the Schedule
Officer to whom it has been allocated to prepare a draft reply.
69. What should typed drafts of minutes, briefs, and letters have?
A. Random numbers.
B. Headings which give an idea of the matter in the drafts.
C. Only the date.
D. The name of the typist.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Typed drafts of minutes, briefs, letters, etc. should have headings which give an
idea of the matter in the drafts.
70. Why is reasonable spacing used in typed drafts?
A. To make them longer.
B. So that there is ample space for amendments.
C. To save paper.
D. To make them look more professional.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Reasonable spacing is used in typed drafts so that there is ample space for
amendments.
71. What should never be used for drafting, especially if it contains classified information?
A. Brand new paper.
B. Discarded papers containing classified information.
C. Recycled paper.
D. Plain notebooks.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Discarded papers containing classified information should never be used for
drafting.
