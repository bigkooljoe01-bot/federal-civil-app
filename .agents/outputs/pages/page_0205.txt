34. A Sub-Accounting Officer may not make payment against a voucher unless it is not more
than how many months since it was signed by the officer controlling expenditure?
A. One month.
B. Two months.
C. Three months.
D. Six months.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 611(c) states: "It is not more than three months since the
voucher was signed by the officer controlling expenditure.”
35. When a voucher is submitted for payment at another station, how should it be sent after
being checked and stamped?
A. By regular mail.
B. By email.
C. Under the security schedule of Transfer of Payment Vouchers through the Sub-Treasurer.
D. Hand-carried by the payee.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 612 states: "When a voucher is submitted to a Sub-Treasury
or Cash Office for payment at another station, it will, after being checked and stamped in
accordance with Regulation 609, be sent under security schedule of Transfer of Payment
Vouchers through the Sub- Treasurer (See TF 193)."
36. Who is responsible for furnishing proof of identity if required when a payment is being
claimed?
A. The Paying Officer.
B. The payee.
C. The ministry, extra-ministerial office, or other arms of Government authorising payment.
D. The Accountant-General.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 613 states: "...it is the duty of the ministry, extra-ministerial
office and other arms of Government authorising payment to furnish proof of identity if required.”
37. When a payee is illiterate, whose mark must be witnessed by a literate official other than the
paying officer?
A. The paying officer's mark.
B. The illiterate payee's mark.
C. The literate official's mark.
D. No mark is required.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 614 states: "Where a payee is an illiterate, his or her mark
must be witnessed by a literate official other than the paying officer.”
