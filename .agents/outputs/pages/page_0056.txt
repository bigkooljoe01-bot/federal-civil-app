189. If an officer successfully passes the compulsory confirmation examination, what is the
next step towards confirmation?
A. They are automatically confirmed.
B. The Permanent Secretary reviews their performance and recommends confirmation.
C. They are promoted to the next grade level.
D. They are given a special bonus.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Rule 020303 states that an officer is confirmed at the end of their probationary
period upon passing the prescribed examination and a recommendation from the Permanent
Secretary
190. Can a contract officer on a non-pensionable appointment be required to sit for the
compulsory confirmation examination?
A. Yes, it is mandatory for all officers.
B. No, as the examination is for officers on pensionable appointments.
C. Yes, but only if they desire to convert to a pensionable appointment.
D. Yes, if specified in their contract.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The rule (030501) specifies that the examination is for officers on a
“pensionable appointment who are on probation,” thus excluding non-pensionable contract
staff.
