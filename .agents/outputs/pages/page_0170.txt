Section B
Part 1: Financial Regulations: Expenditure Authorities for Recurrent and Capital
Expenditure
1. What are the primary instruments used to limit and arrange the disbursement of Federal
Government funds?
A. Treasury Circulars and Accounting Manuals.
B. Annual Estimates and Appropriation Act.
C. Financial Regulations and Accounting Codes.
D. Ministerial Directives and Executive Orders.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 301 states: “The Annual Estimates and Appropriation Act are
instruments used to limit and arrange the disbursement of the funds of the Federal
Government.”
2. Who is responsible for any expenditure incurred without proper authority, as stated in
Financial Regulation 301?
A. The Minister of Finance.
B. The Accountant-General.
C. The officer controlling the vote.
D. The National Assembly.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 301 clearly states that "Any officer controls a vote, or part
thereof, who incurs expenditure without such authority does so on his sole responsibility and will
consequently be held peculiarly responsible for his actions.”
3. From which fund is Recurrent Expenditure primarily paid?
A. Development Fund.
B. Contingencies Fund.
C. Consolidated Revenue Fund.
D. Federation Account.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 302(i) states: "Recurrent expenditure is paid from the
Consolidated Revenue Fund..."
4. Which of the following is NOT a form of warrant by which the Minister of Finance conveys
authority for recurrent expenditure?
A. Provisional General Warrant.
B. Development Fund (Special) Warrant.
C. Recurrent Expenditure General Warrant.
D. Virement Warrant.
Correct Answer: B
