55. A newly recruited officer who has spent six months and above shall be allowed to sit for

compulsory examinations for confirmation in the service. Which rule specifies this?

A. 020302

B. 020305

C. 020306

D. 020307

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Rule 020306 specifies that a newly recruited officer who has spent six months

and above shall be allowed to sit for compulsory examinations for confirmation.

56. An officer wishing to transfer from one senior post to another within the Federal Public

Service must have served for a minimum period of how many months?

A. 3 months

B. 6 months

C. 12 months

D. 24 months

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Rule 020307 states that an officer must have served for a minimum period of six

months in his original post before seeking a transfer to another post.
