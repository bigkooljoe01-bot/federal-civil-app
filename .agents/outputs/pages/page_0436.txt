64. What is the purpose of the Code of Conduct Bureau?
A. To manage the finances of the government.
B. To receive a written declaration of assets and liabilities from public officers.
C. To handle all political appointments.
D. To set the salary structure for the Civil Service.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Every public officer shall submit to the Code of Conduct Bureau a written
declaration of all his properties, assets and liabilities and those of his spouse, or unmarried
children under the age of 18 years.
65. A public officer must submit a declaration of assets and liabilities at what frequency?
A. Annually.
B. At the beginning and end of their term of office and at the end of every four years.
C. Only at the beginning of their term.
D. Only when they are promoted.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Every public officer shall, within 3 months after the coming into force of this Code
of Conduct or immediately after taking office and thereafter at the end of every four years and at
the end of his term of office, submit a written declaration of all his properties, assets, and
liabilities to the Code of Conduct Bureau.
66. What is the consequence of a false statement in a public officer's declaration of assets?
A. Nothing, as it is a minor error.
B. It shall be deemed to be a breach of the Code of Conduct.
C. They will be given a warning.
D. They will be fined a small amount.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Any statement in such declaration that is found to be false... shall be deemed to
be a breach of this Code.
