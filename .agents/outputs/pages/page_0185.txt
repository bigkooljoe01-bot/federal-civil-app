59. Can an officer to whom expenditure control has been delegated sub-delegate such
responsibility?
A. Yes, freely.
B. Yes, with the approval of the Accounting Officer.
C. No, never.
D. Only for minor expenses.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 404 states: "He shall not sub-delegate such responsibility
wholly or partly, without the knowledge and approval of the officer controlling the vote."
60. What document is issued by an Accounting Officer to authorize other officers to incur
expenditure and sign vouchers on his behalf?
A. A Virement Warrant.
B. An Authority to Incur Expenditure (A.LE).
C. A Treasury Circular.
D. A General Warrant.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 405 states: "...he shall do so by issuing Authority to Incur
Expenditure (A.LE)..."
61. What must accompany an Authority to Incur Expenditure (A.LE) issued by an Accounting
Officer?
A. A copy of the Annual Estimates.
B. Appropriate cash backing.
C. Alist of all previous expenditures.
D. A letter of approval from the Minister of Finance.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 405 states: "...supported by appropriate cash backing..."
62. When an A LE. is issued to another ministry, what is the duty of the responsible officer in the
receiving ministry?
A. To immediately spend all the funds.
B. To maintain a Vote Book and forward monthly returns of expenditure to the issuing Ministry.
C. To return the ALLE. to the issuing ministry.
D. To report directly to the Accountant-General.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 406 states: “it will be the duty of the responsible officer in the
receiving Ministry or Department to maintain a Vote Book and forward a monthly returns of
expenditure to the issuing Ministry/ Department..."
