70. What additional register must Sub-Accounting Officers maintain besides a cash book?
A. A ledger for all transactions.
B. A cheque summary register.
C. A register of outstanding payments.
D. A register of bank statements.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 717 states: "Sub-Accounting Officers shall maintain a
cheque summary register in addition to a cash book."
71. Who should sign every request for official cheque books?
A. The Accounting Officer.
B. One of the authorized signatories to the account and countersigned by the Head of Accounts.
C. The Minister of Finance.
D. The Accountant-General.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 718 states: "Every request for official cheques books shall
be signed by one of the authorised signatories to the account and countersigned by the Head of
Accounts.”
72. When a cheque is received, how should it be made payable?
A. To the specific ministry/department.
B. To "Federal Government of Nigeria."
C. To the Accountant-General.
D. To the payee's name.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 719(i) states: "A cheque received should be made payable
to "Federal Government of Nigeria."
73. What is the maximum amount for a cheque that must be properly confirmed with designated
banks before acceptance?
A. N10,000.
B.N20,000.
C. N50,000.
D. N100,000.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 719(i)(h) states: "All cheques above N20, 000 must be
properly confirmed with the designated banks before acceptance.”
