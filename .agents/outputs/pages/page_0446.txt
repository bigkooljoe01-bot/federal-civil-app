2. According to the handbook, the Civil Service should discharge its catalytic role of facilitating
what?
A. Political campaigns.
B. Successful conception, planning, execution and monitoring of the policies, projects and
programmes of Government.
C. Private business ventures.
D. The affairs of private citizens.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: The Civil Service should discharge its catalytic role of facilitating successful
conception, planning, execution and monitoring of the policies, projects and programmes of
Government.
3. What is the purpose of preparing a "Fact Book" for the budget defence?
A. To serve as a personal diary for the Minister.
B. To provide a comprehensive resource of information to answer any questions from the
National Assembly.
C. To be a list of all staff in the MDA.
D. To track the progress of ongoing projects.

Explanation: The "Fact Book" is a key document that contains all relevant information to
address any queries from the National Assembly during the budget defence. This question is
from a previous chapter but is included as it is a common question within the quiz context.
