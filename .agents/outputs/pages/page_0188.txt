72. If a deduction is made from a contract payment for a penalty or fine, what happens to the
deduction?
A. Itis kept by the ministry.
B. Itis credited to the appropriate revenue head.
C. Itis transferred to a suspense account.
D. Itis returned to the contractor.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 412 states: “while the deduction shall be credited to the
appropriate revenue head.”
73. When does the authority for recurrent expenditure conveyed by warrants lapse?
A. At the end of the calendar year.
B. At the end of the financial year to which it relates.
C. After three months.
D. When the funds are fully expended.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 413(i) states: "The authority for recurrent expenditure
conveyed by any of the warrants contained in Chapter 3 lapses at the end of the financial year
to which it relates.”
