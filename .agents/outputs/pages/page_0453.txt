30. What is the purpose of having separate "Minutes" and "Correspondence" sides in a file?
A. To make the file thicker.
B. To ensure a clear distinction between the official notes of officers and the documents
received or sent out.
C. To hide the minutes from the public.
D. To make the file easier to read.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The minutes are separated from the correspondence to make a clear distinction
between the official notes of officers and the actual documents.
31. The handbook advises against removing documents from a file. What is the exception to this
rule?
A. When an officer is in a hurry.
B. When the document is no longer needed.
C. When it is done to correct a grave error and is approved by a senior officer.
D. When the document is personal.
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: No document shall be removed from a file unless it is to correct a grave error and
is approved by the Head of the Department or the Accounting Officer.
32. Why is the "Secret/Confidential Registry" maintained separately from the "Open Registry"?
A. To keep all personal files secret.
B. To protect classified documents and correspondences from unauthorized access.
C. To store documents that are not yet ready for public view.
D. To keep a record of all secret meetings.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: The Secret/Confidential Registry is maintained separately to protect classified
documents and correspondences from unauthorized access.
33. A supervisor in a registry is expected to enforce discipline with what?
A. Total authority and no explanation.
B. Justice and faimess at all times.
C. Political bias.
D. A focus on seniority.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: The supervisor should enforce discipline with justice and fairness at all times.
