79. In what order shall receipt and licence books be issued for use?
A. Random order.
B. Strict serial order.
C. Alphabetical order.
D. By request order.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1208 clearly states that they "shall be issued for use in strict
serial order.”
80. What is prohibited for Federal Revenue Collectors stationed in a State regarding state
receipts?
A. They can use them with permission.
B. They shall not under any circumstances request, stock, or issue state receipts for Federal
Govemment eamings.
C. They can issue them for small amounts.
D. They can use them if federal receipts are unavailable.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1209 explicitly prohibits Federal Revenue Collectors from
using state receipts for federal earnings.
81. What is the maximum number of receipt and licence forms that should be issued to
Revenue Collectors?
A. An unlimited number.
B. The number required for their daily use.
C. A month's supply.
D. A year's supply.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1210 states that issues "shall not exceed the number
required for their daily use.”
82. Who is responsible for checking every issue of receipt and licence books with the supporting
Receipt Books Issue Note upon receipt?
A. The issuing officer.
B. The receiving officer.
C. The Auditor-General.
D. The Accountant-General.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1211 places this responsibility on the receiving officer.
