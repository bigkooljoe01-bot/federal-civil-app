77. When is it advisable to type short drafts or minutes if one’s handwriting is not legible?
A. Always.
B. Never.
C. Unless you can write legibly.
D. Only for senior officers.
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: Unless you can write legibly, it is advisable to type short drafts or minutes.
78. What should be done if one's handwriting is not easily readable for minutes or drafts
covering more than half of an A4 page?
A. Write them by hand anyway.
B. They should be typewritten.
C. They should be discarded.
D. They should be sent back to the sender.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Unless one’s hand writing is easily readable, all minutes or drafts covering more
than half of an Ad page should be typewritten.
79. What should officers try to avoid, as mentioned in the "Irelevance" section?
A. Having a grasp of the subject at issue.
B. Drifting away from the question being considered.
C. Being too brief.
D. Being too factual
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Try to have a grasp of the subject at issue, and do not drift away from the
question being considered.
80. What should letters always have?
A. A personal salutation.
B. A heading briefly descriptive of the subject matter, a reference number, and a date.
C. A humorous opening.
D. A list of all previous correspondence.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Letters should always have a heading briefly descriptive of the subject matter and
must bear a reference number and date.
