123. What is the purpose of Rule 021004?
A. To provide a framework for pension contributions.
B. To outline the requirements for pension payments.
C. To enable officers who exit the Service to access their National Housing Funds (NHF)
contributions.
D. To process retirement.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Rule 021004 states that officers who exit the service "shall access their National
Housing Funds (NHF) contributions within one month of retirement.”
124. How are the requirements for pension payments processed?
A. The officer must personally submit the documents to PENCOM.
B. The Departmental Pension Officer forwards the required documents to PENCOM
C. The Head of the Civil Service of the Federation sends the documents.
D. The ministry/extra-ministerial office submits the documents.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Rule 021002 states that Departmental Pension Officers are required to forward to
the Pension Commission (PENCOM) an up-to-date record of service of these officers.
125. What is the process for an officer to access their National Housing Fund (NHF)
contributions?
A. The officer must apply directly to the National Housing Fund.
B. The officer must wait for a circular from the Head of Civil Service of the Federation.
C. The officer must provide a stamped death certificate.
D. The officer must exit the service and can access their contributions within one month of
retirement.
Correct Answer: D
Explanation: Rule 021004 states that officers who exit the Service "shall access their National
Housing Funds (NHF) contributions within one month of retirement.”
