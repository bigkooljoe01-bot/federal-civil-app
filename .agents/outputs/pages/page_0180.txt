40. What happens to any balance in the Central Capital Account (CCA) at the end of the
financial year that has not been cash-backed into the Capital Account of the spending
Ministry/Agency?
A. Itis carried forward indefinitely.
B. It lapses by March 31st of the following year.
C. Itis transferred back to the Consolidated Revenue Fund.
D. It is subject to a new appropriation.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 413(ii)(b) states: "any balance in the CCA at the end of the
Financial year which has not been cash-backed into the Capital Account of the spending
Ministry/Agency shall lapse by 31st March of the following year."
41. If an amount is cash-backed into the account of a spending Ministry/Agency for capital
expenditure, does it lapse?
A. Yes, at the end of the financial year.
B. Yes, by March 31st of the following year.
C. No, it shall be utilized for the purpose stated in the warrant.
D. Only if the project is completed.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 413(ii)(c) states: "Any amount cash-backed into the Account
of the spending Ministry/ Agency shall not lapse but shall be utilised for the purpose- stated in
the warrant.”
