Part 1: Civil Service Handbook: Relations with National Assembly and Its Public

Accounts Committees

1. What is the primary role of the Public Accounts Committees of the National Assembly?

A. To prepare the annual budget for Ministries, Departments, and Agencies (MDAs)

B. To manage and invest public funds

C. To scrutinize government expenditures based on the reports of the Auditor-General for the

Federation

D. To approve all new government contracts

Correct Answer: C.

Explanation: The Public Accounts Committees scrutinize the reports of the Auditor-General for

the Federation on the accounts of MDAs and call the accounting officers to explain any

discrepancies.

2. Who is the Accounting Officer for a Ministry?

A. The Minister

B. The Director of Finance and Accounts

C. The Permanent Secretary

D. The Head of the Civil Service of the Federation

Correct Answer: C.

Explanation: The Permanent Secretary of a Ministry is designated as the Accounting Officer

and is responsible for all expenditures and revenue.

3. In the context of a Public Accounts Committee hearing, what is the role of the

Accountant-General of the Federation?

A. To represent all MDAs and answer for them

B. To provide clarification on accounting procedures and financial regulations

C. To manage the committee's budget

D. To prepare the Auditor-General's report

Correct Answer: B.

Explanation: The Accountant-General of the Federation attends the committee's sittings and

provides clarification on accounting procedures, treasury circulars, and financial regulations

A. To approve new projects

C. To highlight any financial anomalies, unauthorized expenditures, or lack of proper accounting
