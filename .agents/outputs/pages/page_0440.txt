79. The Principle of Equality of Treatment states that persons in a comparable situation vis-a-vis
the administration shall be treated:
A. Based on their political convictions.
B. According to their ethnic group.
C. Equally without any distinctions whatsoever.
D. Based on their social status.
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: The Principle of equality of treatment states that persons in a comparable
situation vis-a-vis the administration shall be treated equally without any distinctions
whatsoever.
80. Which of the following is NOT a prohibited distinction under the Principle of Equality of
Treatment?
A. Race
B. Gender
C. Political convictions
D. Job performance
Correct Answer: D.
Explanation: The principle prohibits any discrimination based on the place of origin, race,
gender, religion, ethnic group, philosophical or political convictions or other personal
considerations.
81. The Principle of Neutrality means the public service as a whole remains neutral with respect
to whom?
A. The public.
B. The government of the day.
C. Its employees.
D. The opposition parties.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: The public service as a whole remains neutral in respect to the government of the
day, and this fundamental principle will be respected by all administrations.
82. What does the Principle of Legality require?
A. Public service shall be provided in strict compliance with the law.
B. Public service shall be provided continuously.
C. Public service shall be accessible to all.
D. Public service shall be transparent.
Correct Answer: A.
