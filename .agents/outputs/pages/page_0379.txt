33. Which of these is a source of revenue for the Consolidated Revenue Fund?
A. Proceeds of tax under pay-as-you-eam system from police personnel
B. Share from the Federation Account
C. Proceeds from domestic consumption of crude oil
D. External grants
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Sources of revenue for the Consolidated Revenue Fund include "Share from the
Federation Account”.
34. What type of expenditures are charged to the Consolidated Revenue Fund?
A. Capital projects
B. All Recurrent Expenditures
C. Unforeseen circumstances
D. External grants
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: All Recurrent Expenditures including the salaries of the Auditor-General, Chief
Justice, President etc are charged to it
35. The Development Fund is primarily meant for what type of projects?
A. Recurrent expenditures
B. Emergency response
C. Capital projects
D. Operational expenses
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: This Fund is meant for capital projects. It is established by the 1999 Constitution.
36. What is the purpose of the Contingency Fund?
A. To fund all government projects.
B. To take care of unforeseen circumstances such as national disasters.
C. To pay recurrent expenditures.
D. To distribute revenue among the tiers of government.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The Contingency Fund was established to take care of un-foreseen
circumstances such as national disasters, fire-outbreaks, serious flooding etc.”.
