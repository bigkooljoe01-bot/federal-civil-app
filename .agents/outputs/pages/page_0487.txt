74. What follows the text of a minute, concluding it?
A. The writer's signature or initials.
B. The date only.
C. The file number.
D. The Ministry's stamp.
Correct Answer: A.
Explanation: The text of the minute follows. It is concluded by the signature or initials of the
writer.
