35. What is the implication of the Board of Survey's findings for an officer-in-charge?
A. The findings have no consequences for the officer.
B. The officer is solely responsible for any discrepancies.
C. The officer is immediately dismissed if a shortage is found.
D. The officer's responsibility is discharged only after a satisfactory report is submitted.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The officer-in-charge must be present during the count and the Board's report is
submitted for investigation of discrepancies, which implies the officer is responsible for the items
under their charge.

36. What is the primary purpose of Chapter 19 of the Financial Regulations book?
A. To define the roles of Accounting Officers.
B. To outline the pension scheme in the Federal Public Service.
C. To detail procedures for internal audit.
D. To explain bank account management.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Chapter 19 is titled "Pension Scheme in the Federal Public Service.”
37. According to Financial Regulation 1904(a), who is responsible for opening Retirement
Savings Accounts (RSA) for each employee?
A. The Accountant-General.
B. The Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN).
C. The National Pension Commission (NPC).
D. The Ministry of Finance.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1904(a) states that the National Pension Commission (NPC)
will open Retirement Savings Accounts (RSA) for each employee.
38. What action is the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) required to take upon receipt of payments
and advice from the National Pension Commission?
A. To disburse funds directly to employees.
