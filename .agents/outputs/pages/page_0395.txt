98. What is the purpose of preparing a "Fact Book” for the budget defence?

A. To serve as a personal diary for the Minister

B. To provide a comprehensive resource of information to answer any questions from the

National Assembly

C. To be a list of all staff in the MDA

D. To track the progress of ongoing projects

Correct Answer: B.

Explanation: The "Fact Book" is a key document that contains all relevant information to

address any queries from the National Assembly during the budget defence.

99. Before presenting the budget to the National Assembly, an MDA must ensure that the

proposed budget is in conformity with what?

A. The MDA's internal policies only

B. The previous year's budget

C. The Federal Government's overall budget framework and policy thrust

D. The demands of international donors

Correct Answer C.

Explanation: The MDA's budget proposals must be in line with the Federal Government's

budget framework and policy thrust for the year.

100. A successful budget defence is dependent on the ability of the MDA's delegation to do

what?

A. Provide all information in a timely and effective manner

B. Avoid answering difficult questions

C. Promise to deliver on all projects, regardless of funding

D. Criticize other MDAs' budgets

Correct Answer: A.

Explanation: A successful budget defence hinges on the delegation's ability to provide all

information requested by the National Assembly in a timely, effective, and convincing manner.
