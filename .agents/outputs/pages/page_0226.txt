17. Who is authorized to issue Imprests?

A. Only the Minister of Finance.

B. The Accountant-General of the Federation and Accounting Officers of Self-Accounting

ministries/extra-ministerial offices.

C. The Auditor-General for the Federation.

D. The Head of Civil Service of the Federation.

Correct Answer: B.

Explanation: Financial Regulation 1002 states that “"Imprests are issued by the

Accountant-General of the Federation and the Accounting Officers of Self Accounting

ministries/extra-ministerial offices and other arms of government.”

18. What document conveys the authority for issuing Imprests?

A. The Annual Appropriation Act.

B. The General Imprest Warrant issued by the Minister of Finance to the Accountant-General.

C. The Monthly Consolidated Account.

D. The Material Expense Statement (M.E.S.).

Correct Answer: B.

Explanation: Financial Regulation 1002 specifies that "The authority for issuing Imprests is

conveyed in the Annual General imprest Warrant issued by the Minister of Finance to the

Accountant-General (see Appendix 5).

19. When is the General Imprest Warrant issued by the Minister of Finance?

A. At the beginning of each calendar year.

B. Immediately after the enactment of the Annual Appropriation Act.

C. Quarterly, upon request from ministries.

D. Only when a special imprest is required.

Correct Answer: B.

Explanation: Financial Regulation 1003(i) states: "The General Imprest Warrant shall be issued

by the Minister of Finance immediately after the enactment of the Annual Appropriation Act.”
