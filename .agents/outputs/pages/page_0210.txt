54. What is required for any bank account beyond the three specified types?
A. Approval from the Minister of Finance.
B. Express approval of the Accountant-General.
C. Notification to the Auditor-General.
D. A resolution from the National Assembly.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 701(ii) states: "No other bank account shall be allowed
without the express approval of the Accountant-General."
55. How often should every organization submit a return of their bank accounts to the
Accountant-General?
A. Monthly.
B. Quarterly.
C. Half-yearly.
D. Annually.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 701(v) states: "Every organisation shall submit a return of
their bank accounts half yearly to the Accountant-General..."
56. Where should all Capital Accounts be maintained?
A. Any commercial bank.
B. The Central Bank of Nigeria or any other designated banks by the government.
C. A foreign bank for international transactions.
D. At the discretion of the Accounting Officer.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 702 states: "All Capital Accounts shall be maintained at the
Central Bank of Nigeria or any other Banks as may be designated by the government of the
federation and authorised by the Accountant-General.”
