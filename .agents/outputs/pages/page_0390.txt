in which areas?
A. Private sector investments
B. The operations of non-govemmental organizations (NGOs)
C. Costing of projects and services
D. Foreign direct investments
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: The Due Process is aimed at ensuring fiscal transparency and strict compliance
with procedures; effectiveness, and efficiency in the costing of projects and serices2".
79. The Public Procurement Act 2007 institutionalized the operations of which unit?
A. The Federal Civil Service Commission
B. The Bureau for Monitoring and Price Intelligence Unit (BMPIU)
C. The Office of the Head of the Civil Service of the Federation
D. The National Assembly Budget and Research Office
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: The steps to institutionalize the operations of the Bureau for Monitoring and Price
Intelligence Unit (BMPIU) culminated in the signing into law of the Public Procurement Act 2007.
80. Which principle of public procurement is emphasized to prevent contract inflation?
A. To ensure value for money
B. To ensure cost reasonableness and accuracy
C. To prevent extra-budgetary spending
D. To guarantee elimination of waste
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: The objective to prevent contract inflation is achieved by ensuring cost
reasonableness, accuracy, and comparability of all Public contracts with national, regional, and
global costs.
81. What is the purpose of certifying projects for public funding under the Due Process policy?
A. To fast-track projects without proper scrutiny
B. To fund projects that have not been included in the budget
C. To certify only those projects and contracts that pass proper project implementation and
execution scrutiny
D. To give preferential treatment to certain contractors
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: The Due Process policy aims to certify for public funding only those projects and
contracts that pass proper project implementation and execution scrutiny.
