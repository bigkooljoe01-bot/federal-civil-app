Part 2: Notes on the Nigerian Foreign Service
71. Who is responsible for making appointments to the Nigerian Foreign Service?
A The President
B The Minister of Foreign Affairs
C The Federal Civil Service Commission 3
D The Head of the Civil Service of the Federation
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: Appointments to the Foreign Service are made by the Federal Civil Service
Commission. However, appointments of Principal Representatives like Ambassadors are made
by the President
72 Who appoints the Principal Representatives of Nigeria, such as Ambassadors and High
Commissioners?
A. The Federal Civil Service Commission
B. The President
C. The National Assembly
D. The Head of the Civil Service of the Federation
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: The President is responsible for appointing Principal Representatives of Nigeria,
including Ambassadors, High Commissioners, and Heads of Mission.
73 What is the expected priority consideration for appointment as Principal Representatives of
Nigeria?
A. Non-career diplomats
B. Career politicians
C. Career diplomats 5
D. Civil servants from other ministries
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Priority consideration is expected to be given to career diplomats over non-career
diplomats for appointments as Principal Representatives of Nigeria.
74. What happens to non-career Ambassadors when the President who appointed them ceases
to hold office?
A. They are automatically re-appointed by the new President.
