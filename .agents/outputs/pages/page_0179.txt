37. The authority to incur expenditure from Public Funds other than the Consolidated Revenue
Fund and Development Fund is what?
A. A general directive from the President.
B. The appropriate warrant signed by the Minister of Finance, addressed to the
Accountant-General.
C. Approval from the National Assembly.
D. A resolution from the Federal Executive Council.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 326 states: "The authority to incur expenditure from any of
the other Public Funds of the Government is the appropriate warrant signed by the Minister of
Finance, addressed to the Accountant-General..."
38. What happens to an unspent balance on a recurrent expenditure vote at the end of the
financial year?
A. Itis carried forward to the next year.
B. It lapses.
C. Itis transferred to the Development Fund.
D. Itis returned to the Federation Account.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 314 states, "An unspent balance on a recurrent. expenditure
vote cannot be revoted.” and FR 413(i) states “The authority for recurrent expenditure... lapses
at the end of the financial year..."
