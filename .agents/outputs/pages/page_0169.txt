99. What is the name of the register used for incoming cheques and other negotiable
instruments received by post?
A. The Treasury Cash Book
B. The General Ledger
C. The Paper Money Register
D. The Revenue Collector's Chart
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 232(i) requires the use of a "Paper Money Register” for this
purpose.
100. What is the purpose of the audit stamps that a Revenue Collector may be required to
collect at the end of each duty?
A. To track the number of receipts issued.
B. To confirm the collection of revenue.
C. To verify the identity of the payer.
D To serve as proof of payment.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1712, found in the index, describes the collection of audit
stamps at the end of each duty, which are used to confirm the collection of revenue.
