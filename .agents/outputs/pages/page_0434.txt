55. What is the danger of using obsolete and incorrect information?
A. It can lead to a promotion.
B. It can help an officer make sound decisions.
C. It can undermine an officer's maturity and sound judgment.
D. It has no effect on the decision-making process.
Correct Answer:C
Explanation: Obsolete and incorrect information do not help officers to make sound decisions
and can easily undermine factors such as maturity and sound judgment.
56. When a driver detects a minor defect in a government vehicle, what should they do?
A. Wait until it becomes a major problem.
B. Fix it themselves.
C. Report it immediately to the officer-in-charge of transport.
D. Ignore it, as it is a minor defect.
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: Drivers should report immediately to the officer-in-charge of transport whenever
defects (even of a minor nature) are detected.
57. The hours of work in Federal offices are from what time to what time?
A. 9:00 am. to 5:00 p.m.
B. 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.
C. 7:00 am. to 3:00 p.m.
D. 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: The hours of work in Federal offices are from 8.00 a.m. to 4.00 p.m. on Monday to
Friday, except as may be officially varied
58. Who is required to sign the Attendance Register?
A. All staff on GL. 03 - 13.
B. Only Permanent Secretaries.
C. All staff, regardless of grade level.
D. Only senior management.
Correct Answer: A.
Explanation: All staff on GL. 03 — 13 are required to sign the Attendance Register on arrival at
the Office in the morning as well as at the close of work at the end of the day.
