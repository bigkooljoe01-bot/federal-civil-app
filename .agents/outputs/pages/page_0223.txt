5. In the Office of the Accountant-General of the Federation (OAGF), what type of journal
voucher is used for adjustments and transfers made before the "Below-the-line Statement" is
extracted?
A. Principal Journal Voucher (PJV)
B. Supplementary Journal Voucher (SJV)
C. Adjustment Voucher (TF 23)
D. Material Expense Statement (M.E.S.)
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 904(i) states: "...the SN and PJV are used in the Office of the
Accountant General of the Federation (OAGF) for the Preparation of Consolidated accounts,
While SNs are used for all adjustments and transfers that are made before the “Below the-line
Statement” is extracted...”
6. For what primary reason are Principal Journal Vouchers (PJVs) prepared in the OAGF?
A. To adjust differences between ministry transcripts and bank statements.
B. To correct misclassification of accounts detected by the main accounts.
C. To reclassify accounts before the Trial Balance is prepared.
D. To record daily cash transactions.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 904(ii)(a) states that PJVs are prepared “to correct
misclassification of accounts detected by the main accounts.”
7. What information must an adjustment voucher clearly indicate?
A. Only the amount of the adjustment.
B. The reason for the adjustment and full reference to the original debit or credit.
C. The name of the officer who prepared the voucher.
D. The date of the next audit.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 905 states: "Adjustment vouchers must clearly indicate the
reason for the adjustment, and must make full reference to the original debit or credit being
adjusted...”
