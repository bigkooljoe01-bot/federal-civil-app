15. What is one of the duties of the Accountant-General regarding accounting codes and
manuals?
A. To only approve them.
B. To approve and ensure compliance with accounting codes, internal audit guides and stock
verification manuals.
C. To formulate them without external input.
D. To distribute them to the public.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 107(i) states: "approve and ensure compliance with
accounting codes, internal audit guides and stock verification manuals of federal ministries,
extra-ministerial offices and other arms of government.”
16. What type of audit ensures that laid down procedures are being observed in tendering,
contracts, and store-keeping to prevent waste, pilferage, and extravagance?
A. Financial Audit.
B. Appropriation Audit.
C. Financial Control Audit.
D. Value-for-Money (Performance) Audit.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 109(1)(c) states: "Financial Control Audit-to ensure that laid
down procedures are being observed in tendering, contracts and store-keeping with a view to
preventing waste, pilferage and Extravagance.”

other arms of government. or units."
