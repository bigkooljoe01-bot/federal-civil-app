71. What happens if a Sub-Accounting Officer determines that an officer who has come into
possession of public money is likely to do so again?
A. The officer is given a new job
B. The officer is given a promotion
C. The officer is supplied with an official receipt booklet
D. The officer is advised to open a private account for such funds
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: According to Financial Regulation 218, the Sub-Accounting Officer will arrange for
the officer to be supplied with an official receipt booklet.
72. Who is responsible for checking Revenue Collectors’ cash books and cash balances at
irregular intervals?
A. The Head of Internal Audit
B. The Accounting Officer
C. The Head of Finance and Accounts
D. The Accountant-General
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 217 states that the Accounting Officer may consider it
necessary for a revenue collecting office to have its cash books and cash balances checked at
irregular intervals.
73. What must Accounting Officers do upon the establishment of any new revenue collecting
office?
A. Notify the Head of Internal Audit
B. Notify the appropriate Sub Accounting Officer and the Auditor-General
C. Notify the Minister of Finance
D. Notify the Central Bank of Nigeria
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 217 specifies that Accounting Officers will notify the
appropriate Sub Accounting Officer and the Auditor-General of the establishment of any new
revenue-collecting office.
