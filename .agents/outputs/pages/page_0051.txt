169. An officer on Grade Level 05, who is a junior officer, needs to pass which examination to

be confirmed in their appointment?

A.COMPRO |

B. COMPRO Il (Junior)

C. Both COMPRO | and Il

D. A separate departmental exam

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Section 3 is titled “Compulsory Confirmation/Promotion Examinations for Junior

Officers (COMPRO I)," which applies to officers on GL 06 and below.

170. What is the implication of passing the Compulsory Confirmation Examination for Junior

Officers at an accelerated level?

A. The officer is immediately promoted to a senior post.

B. The officer is exempted from future promotion examinations.

C. The officer is granted an accelerated promotion to the next grade level23.

D. The officer receives a cash reward.

Correct Answer: C.

Explanation: Rule 030302 discusses a "Pass at Accelerated Level" for junior officers, which

leads to accelerated promotion.
