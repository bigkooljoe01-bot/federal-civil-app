82. Who is responsible for the overall monitoring and oversight of public procurement under the

Public Procurement Act 2007?

A. The Minister of Finance

B. The President

C. The National Council on Public Procurement and the Bureau of Public Procurement

D. The National Assembly

Correct Answer: C.

Explanation: The National Council on Public Procurement and the Bureau of Public

Procurement are the regulatory authorities responsible for the monitoring and oversight of public

procurement.

83. What is one of the ways the Bureau of Public Procurement helps to prevent fraudulent

procurement?

A. By preventing all foreign companies from bidding

B. By applying the provisions of the Public Procurement Act where necessary

C. By keeping all procurement information secret

D. By allowing only politically connected individuals to bid

Correct Answer: B.

Explanation: A function of the Bureau is to prevent fraudulent and unfair procurement and,

where necessary, apply the provisions of the Act.

84. The Bureau of Public Procurement is tasked with organizing what for procurement

professionals?

A. Social gatherings

B. Training and development programmes

C. Political rallies

D. Intemational conferences

Correct Answer: B.

Explanation: A function of the Bureau is to organize training and development programmes for

procurement professionals.
