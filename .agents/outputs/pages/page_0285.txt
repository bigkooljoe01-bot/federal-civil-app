50. What is the significance of the Subsidiary Account Pension Unit functioning independently?
A. It allows for less oversight.
B. It ensures specialization and clear accountability for pension-related financial procedures.
C. It prevents any external audits.
D. It simplifies the overall accounting process.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The independent functioning, as per FR 1905, suggests a focus on specialized
and accountable management of pension funds.
51. Who issues the monthly personnel cost warrants that trigger the pension accounting
procedures?
A. The Accountant-General.
B. The Director of Finance and Accounts.
C. The Budget Office.
D. The National Pension Commission.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1907 indicates that the Director of Finance and Accounts
receives "monthly personnel cost warrants from the Budget Office.”
52. What type of account is the Retirement Savings Account (RSA)?
A. A general govemment account.
B. An account specific to each employee for pension contributions.
C. A suspense account for unclaimed funds.
D. An investment account managed by ministries.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1904(a) states the NPC will open “Retirement Savings
Accounts (RSA) for each employee.”
53. What aspect of contributions does the National Pension Commission credit to the
Retirement Savings Accounts (RSA)?
A. Only employee contributions.
B. Only employer contributions.
C. Both employer and employee contributions, and interest earned.
D. Only interest eamed.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1904(a) specifies that the NPC will credit "both the
collections and interest earned to the account.”
