91. What must be done with the cancelled receipt or licence, including the original?
A. It should be destroyed immediately.
B. It will be retained in the receipt or licence book.
C. It should be sent to the Accountant-General.
D. It should be given to the payer.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1216 specifies that the "cancelled receipts or licence,
including the original, will be retained in the receipt or licence book."
92. Who is responsible for delivering the original receipt or licence to the payer?
A. The Head of Accounts.
B. The officer who issued the receipt.
C. The Accounting Officer.
D. The cashier.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1217 states that the "original receipt or licence shall be
delivered to the payer by the officer who issued the receipt."
93. What is the disposition of duplicate copies of receipts?
A. They are destroyed.
B. They are used as cash book vouchers and forwarded to the Accountant-General.
C. They are retained by the issuing officer.
D. They are given to the payer.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1218 states that "duplicate copies of receipts shall be used
as cash book vouchers and shall be forwarded to the Accountant-General with the monthly
accounts.”
94. What is the disposition of triplicate copies of receipts?
A. They are destroyed.
B. They are forwarded to the Auditor-General.
C. They are retained in the receipt book.
D. They are returned to the Federal Government Printer.
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1219 states that “triplicate copies of receipts shall be
retained in the receipt book.”
