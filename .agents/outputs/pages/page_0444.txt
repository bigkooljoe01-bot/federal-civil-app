94. How should the deadlines for service delivery be determined?
A. By the whims of the head of department.
B. In an arbitrary manner.
C. In a realistic manner and must be communicated to users27.
D. By the political party in power.
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: Such deadlines shall be determined in a realistic manner, and must be
communicated to users of public services.
95. The Principle of Accountability states that public officers shall be personally accountable for
what?
A. The actions of their colleagues.
B. The decisions of the Minister.
C. Their individual actions or omissions in the performance of their duties.
D. The actions of the govemment as a whole.
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: The Principle of Accountability states that public officers shall be personally
accountable for their individual actions or omissions in the performance of their duties, and shall
respond, where necessary, to their superiors.
96. What is the purpose of setting performance targets for individual public officers?
A. To make the work more difficult.
B. To facilitate the objective and fair evaluation of the quality and quantity of the service
rendered.
C. To reduce their salaries.
D. To increase the workload of the officer.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Clear and verifiable performance targets must be set for individual public officers
50 as to facilitate the objective and fair evaluation of the quality and quantity of the service
rendered.
97. The Principle of Motivation states that public service staff must be motivated to achieve
what?
A. Their personal ambitions.
B. The objectives of the organization.
C. The goals of their friends.
D. The political goals of the government.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: The principle of Motivation states that public service staff must be motivated and
encouraged to achieve the objectives of the organization.
