71. When papers are routed back through official channels, what is the purpose?
A. To get a second opinion from junior staff.
B. To allow all intermediate officers to be acquainted with the decisions reached by the higher
authority.
C. To give more time for junior officers to review the documents.
D. To give an opportunity for the papers to be delayed.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Papers routed upwards through official channels are routed back in the same
way, and all intermediate officers are, therefore, acquainted with decisions reached by the
higher authority.
72. According to the handbook, what does “unprofessional” behavior imply in a Civil Servant?
A. Working hard and being diligent.
B. Treating members of the public with respect.
C. Exhibiting rudeness, discourtesy, insensitivity, and inefficiency.
D. Attending all official meetings.
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: The handbook aims to improve the standards of performance of Civil Servants
and remove the ugly image of unprofessionalism which manifests itself in rudeness, discourtesy,
insensitivity, and inefficiency.
