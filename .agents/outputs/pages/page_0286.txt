55. What fundamental principle of financial management is supported by the detailed
accounting procedures for pensions in Chapter 197
A. Discretionary spending.
B. Centralized control.
C. Transparency and accountability in public finance.
D. Minimizing documentation.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: The detailed regulations, reconciliation requirements, and independent unit
creation all support principles of transparency and accountability in managing pension funds.
Part 4: Financial Regulation: Government Vehicles
56. What is the primary purpose of government vehicles, as stated in Financial Regulation
2001?
A. For personal use by government officials.
B. For official duties of the government.
C. For commercial transportation.
D. For recreational activities.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 2001 states that government vehicles are provided for official
duties of the government.
57. Who is held personally responsible for the efficient and economical use of government
vehicles within their Ministry, Extra-Ministerial Office, or other arm of government?
A. The Head Driver.
B. The Accounting Officer.
C. The Transport Officer.
D. The Mechanical Engineer.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 2002 states that the Accounting Officer is personally
responsible for the efficient and economical use of government vehicles.
