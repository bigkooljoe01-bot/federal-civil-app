43. What is the prescribed method for paying salaries and allowances to officers whose duty
station is in Nigeria?
A) By cash only.
B) Through named banks, and paid locally in Naira.
C) By cheque delivered to their home.
D) Through a foreign bank account.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1503(ii) mandates that salaries and allowances for officers in
Nigeria shall be paid locally in Nigeria and in Naira. Also, FR 1503(i) states that all officers will
have their salaries and allowances paid through named banks upon completion of Treasury
Form 14
44. What is forbidden regarding salaries, allowances, and pensions?
A. Paying them in Naira.
B. Paying them in monthly installments.
C. Paying them in advance.
D. Paying them through a bank.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1503(iii) explicitly states that salaries, allowances, and
pensions must not be paid in advance.
45. How are salaries and allowances based on an annual rate paid?
A. Weekly installments.
B. Bi-monthly installments.
C. Monthly installments of one twelfth of the annual rate.
D. As a lump sum at the end of the year.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1504 specifies that salaries and allowances based on an
annual rate shall be paid by monthly installments of one twelfth of the annual rate.
46. Who is responsible for prescribing the dates in the month on which salaries and allowances
shall be paid?
A. The Auditor-General.
B. The Accounting Officer.
C. The officer controlling expenditure.
D. The Accountant-General.
Correct Answer: D
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1505 states that the Accountant-General shall prescribe the
dates for salary and allowance payments each month.
