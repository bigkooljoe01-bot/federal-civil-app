19. What is one of the key responsibilities of Accounting Officers under the Public Procurement
Act?
A. To personally execute all procurements.
B. To ensure adequate appropriation is available for Procurements in their annual budget.
C. To approve all payments without review.
D. To conduct external audits.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 113(b) states: "ensure that adequate appropriation is
available for Procurements in their annual budget.”
20. What is the role of the Head of Finance and Accounts regarding compliance with Financial
Regulations?
A. To ensure compliance by all staff under his control and supervision.
B. To issue new Financial Regulations.
C. To audit all financial transactions.
D. To approve all variations in accounting procedures.
Correct Answer: A
Explanation: Financial Regulation 114(a) states: “ensuring compliance with Financial
Regulations and the Accounting Code by all staff under his control and supervision."
