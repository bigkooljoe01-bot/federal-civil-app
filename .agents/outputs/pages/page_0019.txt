46. What is required for a person who has previously been employed in public service and
dismissed or called upon to resign?
A. They are eligible for reappointment immediately.
B. They must obtain prior approval from the Head of the Civil Service of the Federation.
C. They must obtain the approval of the Commission's powers, and it should be obtained
irrespective of any delegation of the Commission's powers.
D. They are not eligible for reappointment
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Rule 020207(i)(b) requires that any such applicant must obtain approval from the
Commission's powers, "irrespective of any delegation.”
47. What document is required for appointment to senior posts?
A. Federal Civil Service Commission Application Form (FCSC Form 1)
B. Federal Civil Service Commission Application Form (FCSC Form 2)
C. Public Service Application Form (PS Form 1)
D. Personal Particulars Form (Gen 60)
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Rule 020208(a) specifies that all applicants for senior posts shall submit a
completed "Federal Civil Service Commission Application Form (FCSC Form 2)".
48. What form should an officer newly appointed to a pensionable establishment use to provide
their personal particulars?
A. FCSC Form 2
B. Gen 60
C. PSR Form 1
D. Oath of Secrecy Form
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Rule 020209 states that a newly appointed officer is required to enter his/her
personal particulars on a Staff Record Form (Gen 60).
49. Every newly appointed officer or staff member is required to sign which of the following?
A. Public Service Rules
B. Oath of Secrecy
C. Employment Contract
D. Oath of Allegiance
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Rule 020210 mandates that every newly appointed officer/staff member must sign
an Oath of Secrecy on Security (Form 1).
