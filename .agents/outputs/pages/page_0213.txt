66. How should accrued interests from invested idle funds in missions abroad be classified and
remitted?
A. To a special mission fund.
B. To the personal account of the Head of Mission.
C. To the appropriate revenue head and sub-head and remitted to the Treasury.
D. Reinvested in the same deposit.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 711(ii) states: "Where such idle funds are invested, the
accrued interests received ‘thereof, shall be classified to the appropriate revenue head and sub
head and remitted to the Treasury."
67. What is the implication if an officer pays public money into a private bank account?
A. Its considered a minor irregularity.
B. The officer is deemed to have done so with fraudulent intention.
C. A warming will be issued.
D. The money will be transferred back to the government account.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 713 states: "An officer who pays public money into a private
account is deemed to have done so with fraudulent intention."
68. Where a bank account is kept, what type of payments should generally be made by
cheques?
A. Only large payments.
B. All payments.
C. Only contract payments.
D. Small payments only.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 714 states: "Where a Bank account is kept, only sufficient
cash to meet small payments shall be allowed while all payments should be made by cheques.”

statements with their cash books?
A. Monthly.
B. Quarterly.
C. Weekly and at the close of each month.
D. Annually.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 716 states: "All officers authorised to keep bank accounts
must compare on- weekly basis, and at the close of each month, the entries appearing in the
bank statements with those in their cash books..."
