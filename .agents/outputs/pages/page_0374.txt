12. Who is the topmost career Civil Servant in a Local Government set-up?
A. Chairman
B. Councilor
C. Secretary
D. Mayor
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: The topmost career Civil Servant in the set-up is designated as Secretary
13. What is the duty and responsibility of the Executive Council of the Federation?
A. To elect Local Government Chairmen.
B. To determine Government policies on various matters.
C. To confirm appointments of Ministers and Ambassadors.
D. To manage the daily affairs of the Judiciary.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The Executive Council of the Federation has the duty and responsibility of
determining Government policies on various matters.
14. Who is the Secretary to the Executive Council of the Federation?
A. The Head of the Civil Service of the Federation
B. The National Security Adviser
C. The Secretary to the Government of the Federation
D. The Minister of Finance
Answer: C
Explanation: The Secretary to the Council is the Secretary to the Government of the
Federation.
15. What is the designation of the Chief Executive of a Ministry of Cabinet rank?
A. Chairman
B. Permanent Secretary
C. Minister
D. Director-General
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Each Ministry is headed by a Minister of Cabinet rank who is the Chief Executive.

A. The Minister of State
B. The Chairman of the Extra-Ministerial Department
C. The Permanent Secretary
D. The Head of the Civil Service of the Federation
