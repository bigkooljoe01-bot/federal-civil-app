75. Can cheques received by a Sub-Accounting Officer be endorsed or assigned to a third
party?
A. Yes, with proper authorization.
B. Yes, if the third party is another govemment entity.
C. No, under no circumstances.
D. Only for urgent payments.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 720(i) states: "On no account may cheques received by a
Sub-Accounting Officer be endorsed or assigned to a third party."
76. What is the processing period for cash transfers in respect of AIEs issued by
ministries/extra-ministerial offices in favor of state offices, in view of the electronic banking
system?
A. Not exceed three (3) working days.
B. Not exceed five (5) working days.
C. Not exceed seven (7) working days.
D. Not exceed ten (10) working days.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 721(i)(c) states: "the processing period of cash transfers in
respect of AlE's issued by ministries/extra ministerial offices and other arms of government in
favour of state offices shall not exceed Seven (7) working days.”
77. Who is ultimately responsible for ensuring the recovery of dishonored cheques?
A. The officer who accepted the cheque.
B. The Sub-Accounting Officer.
C. The Accounting Officer concerned.
D. The bank.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 730(i) states: “It shall be the ultimate responsibility of the
Accounting Officer concerned to ensure that these amounts are recovered, instituting legal
action if necessary.”
