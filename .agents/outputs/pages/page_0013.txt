22. What is an "officer" when used without qualification?
A. Any person employed by the Federal Government.
B. Means staff in an established post, either on pensionable or contract term.
C. A person on short-term engagement.
D. A consultant.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Rule 010106 defines "officer as "staff in an established post, either on
pensionable or contract term.”
23. What does "pass mark" mean?
A. The minimum score required to pass any examination.
B. The score below which a candidate is considered to have failed the promotion examination,
which currently is 60 percent.
C. The highest score in an examination.
D. The average score of all candidates.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Rule 010106 defines "Pass Mark" as "the score below which a candidate is
considered to have failed the promotion examination, which currently is 60 percent.”
24. What does "Permanent Secretary” mean?
A. The Head of a Department.
B. Officer in charge of day-to-day administration in the Ministry/Extra-Ministerial Offices.
C. The Chief Executive Officer of a Parastatal.
D. The Head of the Civil Service of the Federation.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Rule 010106 defines "Permanent Secretary" as "Officer in charge of day-to-day
administration in the Ministry/Extra-Ministerial Offices."
25. What does "Public Service of the Federation" mean?
A. The service of the Federal Government in any capacity.
B. The service of the Federation as defined in Part 4 Section 318 of 1999 Constitution (as
amended).
C. The service of state governments.
D. The service of local governments.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Rule 010106 defines "Public Service of the Federation" as "the service of the
Federation as defined in Part 4 Section 318 of 1999 Constitution (as amended).”
