19. How does the Accountant-General provide financial guidelines to federal ministries and
other arms of government?
A. Through direct verbal instructions.
B. By issuing treasury circulars.
C. By conducting training sessions only.
D. By auditing their accounts annually.

Explanation: Financial Regulation 107(k) states the Accountant-General shall “provide financial
guidelines through the issuance of treasury circulars.”

system in federal ministries?
A. The Minister of Finance.
B. The Auditor-General
C. The Accountant-General.
D. The Head of Finance and Accounts.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 107() states the Accountant-General shall “supervise and
control the computerization of the accounting system.”

A. To collect all federal government revenue.
