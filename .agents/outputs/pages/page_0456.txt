44. What is the purpose of a "Memorandum" in the Civil Service?
A. To document a personal conversation.
B. To provide a confidential report.
C. To communicate internally within the same department or Ministry.
D. To communicate with the public.
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: A memorandum is a type of correspondence used to communicate internally
between officers within the same department or Ministry.
45. The handbook advises against using obsolete and incorrect information because it can
undermine what?
A. An officer's salary.
B. An officer's maturity and sound judgment.
C. An officer's personal life.
D. An officer's ability to attend meetings.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Obsolete and incorrect information do not help officers to make sound decisions
and can easily undermine factors such as maturity and sound judgment.
46. What is the purpose of an “official letter” in the Civil Service?
A. To communicate with other Ministries and the public.
B. To communicate internally.
C. To document a personal conversation.
D. To provide a confidential report.
Correct Answer: A.
Explanation: An official letter is used to communicate with other Ministries, Parastatals, and the
general public.
