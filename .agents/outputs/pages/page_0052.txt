173. An officer on probation who fails to pass the Compulsory Confirmation/Promotion
Examination for Junior Officers is liable to what consequence?
A. Afine
B. A deferment of their increment
C. Termination of their appointment
D. A transfer to another department
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Rule 030104, which is a general rule for confirmation examinations, applies to
all officers, including junior officers, stating that failure to pass leads to termination of
appointment
174. What is the required pass mark for each subject in the Compulsory
Confirmation/Promotion Examination for Junior Officers?
A. 40%
B. 50%
C.60%
D. 75%
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The pass mark for each subject in the junior officers’ examination is 50%,
consistent with the senior officers’ examination

175. What is the primary requirement for officers in police and paramilitary services with
regard to confirmation and promotion examinations?
A. They must pass the same examinations as civil servants.
B. They must pass the examinations prescribed by their respective service commissions.
C. They are exempted from all confirmation examinations.
D. They are required to take an oral interview instead of an examination.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Rule 030401 states that officers of police and paramilitary services are required
to pass the examinations prescribed by their respective service commissions.
