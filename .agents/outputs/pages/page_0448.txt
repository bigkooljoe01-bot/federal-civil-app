10. What is the role of a "Fact Book" during the budget defense process?
A. To list all of the staff in a department.
B. To provide comprehensive information to address queries from the National Assembly.
C. To document all a Ministry's expenses for the year.
D. To serve as a personal diary for the Minister.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: The Fact Book is a key document that contains all relevant information to address
any queries from the National Assembly during the budget defence. This question is from a

Pat 2: Records Management in the Civil Service
11. What is the primary purpose of records in the Civil Service?
A. To create a historical archive of government actions.
B. To provide a basis for continuity and reference to the decisions of past governments.
C. To document the personal lives of civil servants.
D. To generate a paper trail for all financial transactions.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Records are kept to provide continuity through reference to the decisions of past
governments. The Civil Service's continued relevance is a function of how well it manages these
records.
12. What is the main danger of an organization's records not being kept properly?
A. Civil servants will not be paid on time.
B. It could lead to the loss of government revenue.
C. It could lead to policy reversals and conflicts
D. The public will lose trust in the government.
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: If records are not kept properly, it could lead to policy reversals, conflicts within
and between agencies, and chaos in the public sector.
