27. What is the purpose of a Value-for-Money (Performance) Audit carried out by the
Auditor-General?
A. To determine if government accounts are faithfully kept.
B. To ensure funds are expended as appropriated.
C. To ascertain the level of economy, efficiency, and effectiveness from government projects.
D. To prevent pilferage and extravagance.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 109(i)(d) defines Value-for-Money (Performance) Audit as
being "to ascertain the level of economy, efficiency, and effectiveness derived from government
projects and programs.”
28. The scope of work of the Auditor-General includes auditing the accounts of which entities?
A. Federal ministries and extra-ministerial offices.
B. Parastatals and goverment statutory corporations.
C. Federal government establishments in states and FCT Area Councils.
D. All of the above.
Correct Answer: D
Explanation: Financial Regulation 109(i) lists all these as within the scope of the
Auditor-General's work.
29. Who has free access to books of accounts, files, safes, security documents, and other
records relating to government accounts?
A. Only the Accountant-General.
B. Only the Auditor-General.
C. The Accountant-General and the Auditor-General or their representatives.
D. Accounting Officers and Heads of Finance.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 110 states that “the Accountant General and the
Auditor-General or their representatives shall, at all reasonable times, have free access to
books of accounts, files, safes, security documents, and other records and information relating
to the accounts."
30. What does the term "Accounting Officer” mean?
A. Any officer involved in financial transactions.
B. The Permanent Secretary of a ministry or head of an extra-ministerial office is responsible for
human, material, and financial resources.
C. The Chief Executive Officer of a parastatal.
D. An officer who keeps a cash book.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 111()) defines "Accounting Officer" as “the Permanent
Secretary of a ministry or the head of extra-ministerial office and other arms of government who
is in full control of, and is responsible for, human, material, and financial resources.”
