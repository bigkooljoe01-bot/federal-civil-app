55. What type of receipt book is used by all Sub-Accounting Officers, unless a specially printed

A. Treasury Book 5

56. What is the process for making duplicate and triplicate copies of a receipt when using
General Receipts (Treasury Book 6)?
A. By making photocopies

D. By using a special duplicating machine
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 206(ii) states that double-sided carbon paper shall be used
to make the duplicate and triplicate copies of the receipt.

Explanation: Financial Regulation 206(ii) states that the original receipt shall be handed or
delivered to the payer by the officer who issued the receipt.
