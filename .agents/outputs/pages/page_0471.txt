6. Why is it important for a chairman to prepare adequately for a meeting?
A. To show off his intelligence to the members.
B. To ensure a smooth and effective control of the meeting and earn the respect of the
members.
C. To waste time before the meeting starts.
D. To give the Secretariat less work to do.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: It is only by preparing adequately that the chairman can assure himself of an
effective control of the meeting and earn the full respect of the members.
7. What is a crucial step a chairman should take to prepare for a meeting?
A. Read the files and other relevant documents to become fully conversant with the
subject-matter.
B. Ignore all previous meeting minutes.
C. Arrive late to assert his authority.
D. Avoid discussing the agenda with the Secretariat.
Correct Answer: A.
Explanation: The chairman should read the files as well as other relevant documents and
materials so as to become fully conversant with the subject-matter
8. What should the chairman do with the draft agenda prepared by the Secretariat?
A. Ignore it and create his own.
B. Vette the draft agenda and direct its circulation to all members in advance21.
C. Hand it out to members at the beginning of the meeting.
D. Keep it secret from the members until the meeting starts.
Correct Answer:B
Explanation: The chairman should get the Secretariat to prepare and submit for his vetting the
Draft Agenda for the meeting. Once he clears this, he should direct its circulation to all members
well in advance of the meeting.
9. If the chairman has follow-up actions to take before a meeting, what must he do?
