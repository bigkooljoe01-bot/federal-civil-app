38. Who is responsible for preparing the Federal Budget?
A. The Legislature
B. The Executive

Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Therefore, the Executive is responsible for preparing the Federal Budget while the
Legislature approves it
39. What does the President's directives to the Minister of Finance and the Budget Office of the
Federation aim to achieve through the budget?
A. To increase government debt
B. To reduce poverty, generate wealth and create jobs
C. To decrease social welfare programs
D. To centralize all economic activities
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The plans indicate what the Government intends to do to reduce poverty,
generate wealth and create jobs
40. What serves as a policy tool for attaining the immediate, medium and long-term
development goals of the Government?
A. The Constitution
B. The Budget
C. The annual report
D. The monetary policy
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: As the preparation of the Federal Budget is done against the background of these
development plans, the budget serves as a policy tool for attaining the immediate, medium and
long-term development goals of Government
