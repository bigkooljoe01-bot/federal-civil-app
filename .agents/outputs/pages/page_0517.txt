99. What is Nigeria's Foreign Policy a reflection of?
A. The country’s military strength.
B. The policies of its colonial past.
C. Its political, economic, social, and cultural circumstances.
D. The prevailing global political ideologies.
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: Nigeria's foreign policy reflects its political, economic, social, and cultural
circumstances.
