25. What Treasury Form (TF) is used for the issue and payments of impress?
A. Treasury Form 15.
B. Treasury Form 23.
C. Treasury Form 9.
D. Treasury Form 34.
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1006 states: "Authorities for issue-and payments of imprests
shall be effected on a Departmental Imprests Warrant (Treasury Form 9)..."
26. Where should all imprests issued and their retirement be recorded?
A. In the main ledger.
B. In a special imprest ledger.
C. In the cash book only.
D. In the vote book.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1006 specifies: "All imprests issued and their retirement shall
be recorded in a special imprest ledger.”
27. How should issues of imprest be classified in the General Ledger Account?
A. To "Expenditure" in the name of the Imprest Holder.
B. To "Revenue" in the name of the Imprest Holder.
C. To "Imprests” in the name and designation of the Imprest Holder.
D. To "Suspense Account”
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1006 states: "Issues shall be classified to "Imprests" General
Ledger Account in the name and designation of the Imprest Holder.”
