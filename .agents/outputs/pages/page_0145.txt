3. The principal financial authorities are primarily dealt with in which part of Chapter 12
A. Part Il - Financial Responsibilities of Public Officers.
B. Part | - Financial Authorities.
C. Appendix 1.
D. The Foreword.
Correct Answer: B

4. What is the primary source of provisions for government financial business in Nigeria?
A. The Annual Appropriation Act.
B. The Finance (Control and Management) Act.
C. The 1999 Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.
D. Treasury Circulars.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 102 states, "The 1999 Constitution of the Federal Republic
of Nigeria makes ample provisions for government financial business."
5. Which of these financial matters are regulated by the relevant sections of the Constitution?
A. Only the payment of revenue into the Federation Account.
B. Authorization of revenue allocation and disbursement from major funds.
C. Audit of public accounts and other financial matters.
D. All of the above.
Correct Answer: D
Explanation: Financial Regulation 102 specifies that the Constitution regulates "The payment
of revenue into the Federation Account and the Consolidated Revenue Fund, the authorization
of allocation of revenue from the federation account, the disbursement of funds from the
Consolidated Revenue Fund, the Development Fund, and the Contingencies Fund, the audit of
public accounts, and other financial matters.”
6. Who has been assigned the responsibility for all financial business of the government of the
federation by the President?
A. The Accountant-General of the Federation.
B. The Auditor-General for the Federation.
C. The Minister of Finance.
D. The Head of the Civil Service.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 103 states, “The President of the Federal Republic of Nigeria
has assigned to the Minister of Finance the responsibility for all financial business of the
government of the federation.”
