95. What happens if a Foreign Service Officer's marriage to a foreigner is deemed not to be in
the interest of the Service?
A. The officer will be suspended without pay.
B. The officer will be demoted to a lower grade level.
C. The officer will be required to leave the Foreign Service or return to the Home Service11.
D. The officer will be fined.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: If the marriage to a foreigner is not in the best interest of the Service, the officer
will be required to either leave the Foreign Service or return to the Home Service.
96. What is the official protocol for Ministerial Departments, Agencies, and other bodies
embarking on foreign trips?
A. They must get permission from the Presidency.
B. They are not expected to embark on foreign trips without informing the Ministry of Foreign
Affairs12.
C. They can go on trips independently as long as they are self-funded.
D. They must inform the Head of the Civil Service.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: MDAs and other government bodies are not supposed to travel abroad without
first informing the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.
97.: Is a Foreign Service Officer allowed to accept gifts or presentations for services rendered?
A. Yes, if they are of a small value.
B. Yes, as long as they declare them to the government.
C. No, they are not allowed to accept gifts or presentations from anyone for services rendered
or to be rendered13.
D. Only from other diplomats as a sign of goodwill
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: Foreign Service Officers are forbidden from accepting any gifts or presentations,
whether money or otherwise, for services they have rendered or will render.
98. What is the primary consideration in the pursuit of Nigeria's foreign policy?
A. Its national interest14.
B. The foreign policies of other African nations.
C. Global peace initiatives.
D. Economic benefits from a single trading partner.
Correct Answer: A.
Explanation: The main factor considered in Nigeria's foreign policy strategies is her national
interest.
