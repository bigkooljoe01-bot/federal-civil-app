83. If defects or discrepancies are found in receipt or licence books upon receipt, to whom must
they be reported immediately?
A. Only the supplying officer.
B. The Accountant-General and the Auditor-General.
C. The supplying officer, with a copy to the Accountant-General and the Auditor-General.
D. The Head of Department.
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1211 requires reporting to the supplying officer, with copies
to the Accountant-General and Auditor-General.
84. Who is held responsible for any errors discovered in receipt/licence books after the duplicate
copy of the Receipt Book Issue Note has been receipted and returned?
A. The supplying officer.
B. The receiving officer.
C. The Federal Government Printer.
D. The Accountant-General.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1211 states that the “receiving officer shall be held
responsible for any errors discovered after the duplicate copy of the Receipt Book Issue Note
has been receipted and retumed to the supplying officer.”
85. Where must all Treasury receipt books issued from main stocks for use be recorded by the
receiving officer?
A. Ina general ledger.
B. In the Receipt Book Register.
C. In a daily log.
D. In the cash book.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1212(i) mandates recording in the Receipt Book Register.
86. Question: What security measures are required for receipt books at the close of business
daily?
A. They can be left on the desk.
B. They must be locked in a safe or strong-room.
C. They should be taken home by the officer.
D. They can be left with a colleague.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1212(i) states that the receiving officer "will be responsible
for ensuring that the books are locked in a safe or strong-room at the close of business.”
