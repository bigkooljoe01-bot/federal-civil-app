46. What should a schedule officer do when an enquiry is addressed to the wrong Ministry?
A. Discard the letter.
B. Reply to the letter and direct the sender to the appropriate Ministry.
C. Forward the letter to the appropriate Ministry and notify the sender.
D. Keep the letter in their file.
Answer: C.
Explanation: Where an enquiry from the public is addressed to the wrong Ministry or
Department, the proper procedure is to forward the original letter to the appropriate Ministry or
Department and to write a brief note to the sender informing him that the letter has been
forwarded to the correct office.
