58. What characteristic of good govemance focuses on institutions and processes serving all
stakeholders within a reasonable timeframe?
A. Security and Order
B. Conflicts
C. Responsiveness
D. Welfare of People
Correct Answer: C

59. What is the role of the Civil Service in relation to good governance, as stated in Chapter 1?
A. To dictate government policies
B. To hinder the implementation of government policies
C. To play a crucial role in ensuring Nigeria is administered consistent with good govemance
attributes
D. To only monitor, not actively participate in governance
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: The civil service has a crucial role to play in ensuring that Nigeria is administered
in a manner consistent with the aforementioned attributes and characteristics of good
governance
60. What is NOT a characteristic of good governance listed in the handbook?
A. Rule of Law
B. Autocracy
C. Transparency
D. Accountability
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Chapter 10 of the handbook lists several characteristics of good governance,
including Rule of Law, Participation, Transparency, Accountability, Effectiveness and Efficiency,
Equity and Inclusiveness, Consensus Orientation, Responsiveness, Security and Order,
Conflicts (capacity to manage), Purposeful Leadership, and Welfare of People. Autocracy is not
listed and is contrary to the principles of good governance.
