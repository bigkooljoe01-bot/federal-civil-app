Part 8: Posting of Officers
80. What is the definition of “posting” according to the Public Service Rules?
A. The temporary movement of an officer.
B. The initial assignment of an officer in a position/job schedule in an MDA.
C. The permanent transfer of an officer to a new location.
D. The movement of an officer on promotion.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Rule 020601 defines posting as “the initial assignment of an officer in a
position/job schedule in an MDA, or reassignment to another position/job schedule within or
outside his current MDA/Department.*
81. Who is responsible for posting and redeploying all senior staff?
A. The Federal Civil Service Commission
B. The Head of the Civil Service of the Federation
C. The Ministry/Extra-Ministerial Department
D. The Permanent Secretary/Head of Extra-Ministerial Office
Correct Answer: D
Explanation: The rules state that all senior staff can be posted and/or redeployed to new posts
in accordance with the approved Employee Mobility Policy.
82. For an officer posted outside their professional cadre, what is the mandatory period they
should serve in such a post?
A. Two years
B. Four years
C. Six years.
D. Eight years
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Rule 020602(i)(b) stipulates that posting of officers to posts outside their
professional cadres is prohibited®. However, rule 020602 (i) (c) says that posting of pool officers
in the service shall be mandatory every four (4) years.
