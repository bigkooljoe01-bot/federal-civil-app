75. What is the proper attitude for a Civil Servant towards the Nigerian tax payers?

A. To be indifferent to their needs.

B. To be rude and unhelpful.

C. To offer quality service without let or hindrance at all times.

D. To charge them for every service provided.

Correct Answer: C.

Explanation: Any other attitude apart from offering quality service without let or hindrance at all

times is unacceptable, unpatriotic and should not be tolerated in the reforming Civil Service.
