10. What is required for the issuance of audit stamps to Internal Audit staff?
A. Verbal permission from the Head of Department.
B. A written request.
C. A form signed by the Accountant-General.
D. A requisition form (Treasury Form 65A) in triplicate.
Correct Answer: D
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1711 details the procedure for issuing audit stamps,
requiring a requisition form (Treasury Form 65A) in triplicate.
11. What must an Internal Auditor do with audit stamps at the end of each duty?
A. Destroy them.
B. Keep them for the next day's work.
C. Account for and return them to the Head of the Internal Audit Unit.
D. Distribute them to other staff.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1712 states that Internal Auditors must account for and
return their audit stamps to the Head of the Internal Audit Unit at the close of each day's duty.
12. What is strictly prohibited regarding audit stamps?
A. Using them on vouchers.
B. Transferring them to another staff member.
C. Collecting them from the Head of the Internal Audit Unit.
D. Keeping them in a safe
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1713 states that "Audit stamps are not transferable” and
must not be used by anyone other than the officer to whom they were issued.
