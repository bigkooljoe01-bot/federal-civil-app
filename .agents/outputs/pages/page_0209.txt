51. Who is authorized to open an official bank account?
A. The Minister of Finance.
B. The Head of the Ministry.
C. The Accountant-General.
D. The Director of Finance and Accounts.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 701(i) states: "No official bank account shall be opened
unless authorised by the Accountant-General.”
52. How many bank accounts is each establishment generally allowed to operate?

Correct Answer: C

operate only three (3) bank accounts as follows: (a) Salary Account; (b) Overhead Cost
Account; and (c) Revenue Account.”
