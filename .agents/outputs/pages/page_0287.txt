58. What document must every government vehicle have attached to its logbook, as per FR
2003?
A. A copy of the driver's license.
B. A copy of the vehicle's insurance policy.
C. A copy of the "Authority to Use Government Vehicle" (Treasury Form 140).
D. A copy of the vehicle purchase invoice.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 2003 states that a copy of the "Authority to Use Government
Vehicle" (Treasury Form 140) must be attached to the logbook.
59. What information must be clearly displayed on the front and rear number plates of all
government vehicles?
A. The vehicle's model.
B. The official registration number and the inscription “Federal Government" or “State
Government.”
C. The driver's name.
D. The ministry's logo.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 2004 requires that all government vehicles must display the
official registration number and the inscription “Federal Government" or "State Government” on
both front and rear number plates.
60. Which document must be carried in every government vehicle, and why?
A. Vehicle purchase invoice for proof of ownership.
B. The logbook (Treasury Form 141) for recording journey details.
C. The vehicle service history for maintenance records.
D. The driver's medical certificate for emergencies.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 2005 mandates that a logbook (Treasury Form 141) must be
carried in every vehicle for recording details of journeys.
61. Who i responsible for ensuring the proper and up-to-date maintenance of the logbook?
A. The Accounting Officer.
B. The driver.
C. The Transport Officer.
D. The Head of Department.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 2005 states that the driver is responsible for the proper and
up-to-date maintenance of the logbook.
