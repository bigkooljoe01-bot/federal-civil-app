99. What is the role of the Sub-Accounting Officer regarding the classification of a voucher?
A. They can change it arbitrarily.
B. They cannot change it arbitrarily; only the officer controlling expenditure can.
C. They must approve all changes.
D. They are not involved in classification
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 421 states: “The classification of a voucher shall not be
changed arbitrarily by a checking officer or Sub-Accounting Officer.”
100. Question: What is the primary objective of “Expenditure Control* as discussed in Chapter
4?
A. To ensure all allocated funds are spent.
B. To prevent fraud and waste in public spending.
C. To accelerate payments to contractors.
D. To simplify accounting procedures.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The entire chapter emphasizes mechanisms (vote books, warrants,
responsibilities) to ensure proper and authorized use of funds, directly aiming to prevent fraud,
waste, and unauthorized expenditure.
