26. What are "Public Service Rules"?
A. Guidelines for private sector employment.
B. Consists of policies and guidelines which define the employment relationship between Public
Servants and the Government.
C. Laws enacted by the National Assembly.
D. Regulations for international diplomacy.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Rule 010106 defines "Public Service Rules” as “consisting of policies and
guidelines that define the employment relationship between public servants and the
government; they also spell out the conditions of service and human resources management
procedures.”
27. What does "Schemes of Service" refer to?
A. Training programs for new recruits.
B. A reference document in the public service that specifies the duties of each post, method of
entry, including qualification requirements and the prerequisites for advancement within and
beyond cadres.
C. Retirement benefit plans.
D. Disciplinary procedures.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Rule 010106 defines "Schemes of Service" as "a reference document in the
public service that specifies the duties of each post, method of entry, including qualification
requirements, and the prerequisites for advancement within and beyond cadres.”
