13. What is the process for deposits held in foreign currency?
A. They must be converted to local currency immediately.
B. They should be treated in the same manner as deposits in local currency.
C. They are not subject to these regulations.
D. They are the responsibility of the Central Bank of Nigeria
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: While not explicitly stated, the general principle of the financial regulations is that
all government funds are subject to proper accounting procedures, regardless of the currency.
The regulations for deposits apply universally.
14. What must be included in the Deposit Register for each transaction?
A. Only the amount of the deposit.
B. The name of the depositor, particulars of the deposit, and withdrawal.
C. The date of the transaction and the signature of the officer.
D. The reason for the deposit and the expected withdrawal date.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1304 requires the register to contain the name of the
depositor and particulars of all deposits and withdrawals made.
15. If a deposit is transferred to revenue, can the original depositor claim the money later?
A. No, the claim is permanently extinguished.
B. Yes, but the claim must be made within one year.
C. The claim will be treated as a claim against public funds.
D. The money can be re-transferred from revenue back to the deposit account.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1311 states that if the deposit is transferred to revenue and a
valid claim is later made, it will be treated as a claim against public funds.
