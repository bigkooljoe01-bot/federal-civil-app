9. Who may exclude an item of expenditure from the Annual General Warrant if special control
is desired?
A. The Accountant-General.
B. The Auditor-General.
C. The Minister of Finance.
D. The National Assembly.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 304 states: “The Minister of Finance may exclude from the
Annual General Warrant any item of expenditure over which it is desired to exercise special
control."
10. A Supplementary General Warrant authorizes funds for additional services approved in
which document?
A. Treasury Circulars.
B. Supplementary Estimates.
C. Annual Appropriation Act.
D. Public Service Rules.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 305 states: "A Supplementary General Warrant authorises
the Accountant-General to issue funds to pay for the additional personal emoluments and other
services which must have been approved in Supplementary Estimates..."
11. Under what circumstances may a Supplementary (Contingencies) Warrant be issued?
A. For routine, planned expenditures.
B. In very exceptional cases where virement is not possible and urgency is high.
C. To cover budget deficits.
D. For capital projects only.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 306 specifies "very exceptional cases, where virement is not
possible and where an application for additional provision reveals such a degree of urgency..."
