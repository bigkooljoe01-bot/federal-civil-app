34. Who determines the seniority of an officer?
A. The date of appointment
B. The date of assumption of duty
C. The date of appointment or the date of assumption of duty, whichever is earlier and duly
certified in the appropriate register.
D. The date of confirmation
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Rule 020105 states that seniority is determined by the date of assumption of duty
certified by an authorized officer and/or date of present appointment as reflected in the
appropriate register and career progression.
35. What is the rule number that prohibits the employment of unpaid staff in the Federal Public
Service?
A. 020105
B. 020106
C. 020107
D. 020108
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Rule 020106 explicitly states, "The employment of unpaid staff is prohibited.”
36. Who is responsible for keeping the up-to-date personal record of every officer?
A. The Federal Civil Service Commission
B. The Office of the Head of the Civil Service of the Federation
C. The respective Ministry/Extra-Ministerial Office
D. The Accountant-General of the Federation
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Rule 020107 mandates that Permanent Secretaries/Heads of Extra-Ministerial
Offices shall keep an up-to-date record of every officer under their control.
