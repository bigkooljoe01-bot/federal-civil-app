73. What should a Civil Servant do with a file that has been on their desk for a long time without
any action?
A. Keep it in their desk.
B. Report it to their superior officer and return it to the registry.
C. Throw it away.
D. Pass it to a colleague.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Should a file be left on a desk for a long time without any action, it should be
reported to the superior officer and retumed to the registry.
74. What is the purpose of an "open registry"?
A. To keep all classified documents.
B. To keep all personal files.
C. To keep all non-classified documents and correspondences.
D. To keep a record of all secret meetings.
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: The open registry is where non-classified documents and correspondences are
kept and processed.
75. What is the purpose of a "secret registry"?
A. To keep all non-classified documents.
B. To keep all personal files.
C. To keep all classified documents and correspondences.
D. To keep a record of all open meetings
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: The secret registry is where classified documents are handled.
76. What is the purpose of an “official stamp"?
A. To indicate who created the document.
B. To indicate that a document is authentic and has been received by an office.
C. To make a document look nice.
D. To indicate the date the document was created.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: An official stamp is used to indicate that a document is authentic and has been
received by an office.
77. What is the purpose of a "circular"?
A. To communicate with the public.
B. To communicate an official announcement, directive, or information to all staff in a Ministry or
the Service at large.
