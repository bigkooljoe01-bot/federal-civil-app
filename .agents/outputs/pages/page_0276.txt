14. What is the primary purpose of the Internal Audit Unit?
A. To perform administrative tasks for the department.
B. To provide an independent appraisal of accounting, financial, and other controls.
C. To sign off on all procurement contracts.
D. To manage the department's human resources.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The policy of internal audit and its duties, as described in Chapter 17, are
centered on providing an independent appraisal of financial and other controls to the Accounting
Officer.
15. To whom does the Internal Auditor submit periodic audit reports?
A. The Accountant-General.
B. The Head of Department.
C. The Accounting Officer.
D. The Minister of Finance.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1706 explicitly states that periodic audit reports are
submitted to the Accounting Officer.
16. What is the relationship between the Internal Auditor and the staff whose accounts they are
auditing?
A. The Internal Auditor is superior to the staff.
B. The Internal Auditor is subordinate to the staff.
C. The Internal Auditor should be independent of the staff.
D. The Internal Auditor works under the supervision of the staff.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: The entire purpose of internal audit is to provide an independent appraisal, which
implies independence from the staff whose work is being audited.
