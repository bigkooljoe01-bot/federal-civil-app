26. The handbook mentions that all files should be classified into what?
A. Secret and Open.
B. Temporary and Permanent.
C. Subject and Personal.
D. Urgent and Normal.
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: All files should be classified into subject files and personal files.
27. What is typically contained in a "Subject File"?
A. Documents relating to a staff member's personal life.
B. Correspondence and documents relating to a particular subject matter.
C. Financial records of the government.
D. The personal assets of a public officer.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: A subject file is a collection of official correspondence and other documents on a
particular subject matter.
28. What is typically contained in a "Personal File*?
A. Documents on a particular subject matter.
B. Correspondence and documents relating to a particular staff member.
C. Financial records of a Ministry.
D. The official history of a Ministry.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Personal files are files that contain all correspondence and other documents
relating to a particular staff member.
29. How should all documents and correspondence in a file be arranged?
A. In no particular order.
B. In reverse chronological order, with the most recent at the front.
C. In chronological order, with the most recent at the back.
D. In alphabetical order.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: All documents and correspondence in a file must be arranged in reverse
chronological order, with the most recent on top.
