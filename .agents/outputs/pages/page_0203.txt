26. What is strictly prohibited when preparing payment vouchers?
A. Using ball pens.
B. Writing in pencil.
C. Writing totals in figures.
D. Using typewriters.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 604(a) states: "On no account shall vouchers be written in
pencil."
27. What should be drawn immediately before and after the Naira (N) figure when it appears in
words on a voucher?
A. A single thin horizontal line.
B. A double thick horizontal line.
C. A single thick horizontal line.
D. No line is required.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 604(c) states: "A single thick horizontal line shall be drawn
immediately before, and immediately after the Naira (N) figure where it appears in word."
28. Who is responsible for maintaining a payment voucher register and confirming the accuracy
of entries before signing a voucher?
A. The Sub-Accounting Officer.
B. The Head of Finance and Accounts.
C. The Officer controlling the vote.
D. The Internal Auditor.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 605 states: "Officer controlling vote shall maintain a payment
voucher register... Under no circumstances shall he sign a voucher until it has been entered in
the register and has accordingly confirmed the accuracy of the entries therein."

A. All copies.

C. Only the originals.
D. Only the copy was sent to the Auditor-General.
