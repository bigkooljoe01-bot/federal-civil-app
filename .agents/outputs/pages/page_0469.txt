99. Which of the following is a key characteristic of a good minute?
A. It should be verbose and include all personal thoughts.
B. It should be written in a conversational tone.
C. It should be brief, factual, and free from grammatical errors.
D. It should only be written by senior officers.
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: A good minute should be brief, factual, well-reasoned, and free from grammatical
errors, spelling mistakes, and ambiguity.
100. When writing a minute, what should an officer reference to support their position?
A. Personal experiences.
B. Previous government decisions and rules.
C. The opinions of colleagues.
D. Unofficial conversations.
Correct Answer: B..
Explanation: When writing a minute, an officer should support their position by referring to
previous government decisions, rules, or regulations on the matter.
