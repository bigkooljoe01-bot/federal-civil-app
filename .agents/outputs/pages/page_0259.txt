47. On cessation of appointment, up to what day is an officer's salary payable?
A) The last day of the month.
B) The day the resignation letter was submitted.
C) The day after the cessation of appointment.
D) Up to and including the day of cessation.
Correct Answer: D
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1510 states that on cessation of appointment, the salary of
the officer shall be payable up to and including the day of cessation.
48. What is the consequence for an officer who receives information about a colleague's
resignation but fails to promptly inform the Accounts Department?
A. They will be transferred to another department.
B. They will be liable to surcharge and discipline for negligence.
C. They will receive a formal warning.
D. They will have their own salary delayed.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: According to Financial Regulation 1508, an officer who fails to promptly pass on
information to the Director, Finance and Accounts Department is liable to surcharge and
discipline for negligence and dereliction of duty.
49. What should be done with any balance of salary or other moneys due to an officer who has
been dismissed for misappropriation of government funds?
A. It should be paid immediately to the officer's family.
B. It should be held indefinitely by the government.
C. It shall not be paid without the authority of the Accounting Officer.
D. It must be used to pay off their debts.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1511 states that any balance of salary or other money due to
an officer who has been convicted or dismissed for misappropriation or theft shall not be paid
without the Accounting Officer's authority.
50. How long does the Sub-Accounting Officer have to pay unclaimed salaries, allowances, and
pensions to the Treasury?
A. Within 30 days.
B. Within seven days.
C. At the end of the financial year.
D. As soon as the cash is withdrawn.
Correct Answer: B
