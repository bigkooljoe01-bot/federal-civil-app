14. What does “cut-off mark" mean in the context of the PSR?
A. The minimum score for passing any exam.
B. The score set by the Federal Civil Service Commission to determine eligible candidates for
promotion among those who pass the examination as determined by declared vacancies.
C. The maximum score achievable in an examination.
D. The age limit for promotion.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Rule 010105 defines "cut-off mark" as "the score set by the Federal Civil Service
Commission to determine eligible candidates for promotion among those who pass the
examination as determined by declared vacancies.”
15. What is an "Established Post"?
A. A temporary position in the public service.
B. A post provided for under the Personnel Emoluments sub-head of the Estimates.
C. A position for contract staff only.
D. A new position created without approval.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Rule 010105 defines "Established Post" as "a post provided for under the
Personnel Emoluments sub-head of the Estimates.”
16. What does "Expatriate Officer” mean?
A. A Nigerian officer working abroad.
B. A staff member who ranks as such for the purpose of pension in accordance with the Pension
Reform Act 2004 as amended.
C. A foreign national working in Nigeria.
D. An Officer on secondment from another country.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Rule 010105 defines "Expatriate Officer" as "a staff member who ranks as such
for the purpose of pension in accordance with the Pension Reform Act 2004 as amended."
