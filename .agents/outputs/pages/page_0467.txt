91. What is the purpose of an “official letter"?
A. To communicate with other Ministries and the public.
B. To communicate internally.
C. To document a personal conversation.
D. To provide a confidential report.
Correct Answer: A.
Explanation: An official letter is used to communicate with other Ministries, Parastatals, and the
general public.
92. How should all documents and correspondence in a file be arranged?
A. In no particular order.
B. In reverse chronological order, with the most recent at the front.
C. In chronological order, with the most recent at the back.
D. In alphabetical order.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: All documents and correspondence in a file must be arranged in reverse
chronological order, with the most recent on top.
93. The handbook advises against removing documents from a file. What is the exception to this
rule?
A. When an officer is in a hurry.
B. When the document is no longer needed.
C. When it is done to correct a grave error and is approved by a senior officer.
D. When the document is personal.
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: No document shall be removed from a file unless it is to correct a grave error and
is approved by the Head of the Department or the Accounting Officer.

A. To create a historical archive of government actions.

C. To document the personal lives of civil servants.
D. To generate a paper trail for all financial transactions.
