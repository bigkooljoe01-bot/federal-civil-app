67. What is the role of the Accounting Officer concerning personal emolument records?
A. To ignore them.
B.To ensure they are maintained to prevent over-payments and ensure correct deductions are
made.
C. To personally calculate every salary.
D. To only maintain them for senior staff.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1506(ii) states that the Accounting Officer shall ensure
personal emolument records are maintained to ensure payments are made when due, that
over-payments are not made, and that all required deductions are made
68. Who should be promptly informed of any variation or contingency likely to affect an officer's
salary, such as death or dismissal?
A) The next of kin.
B) The Auditor-General
C) The officer controlling expenditure.
D) The local police.

69. What is the purpose of a summary payment voucher in payroll preparation?
A) To list all the officers in a department.
B) To capture the total basic salaries, allowances, deductions, and net amount payable for all
officers in a pay-point.
C) To record an officer's performance.
D) To request a loan for an officer.
Correct Answer: B

raised to capture the total basic salaries and allowances, total deductions, and the net amount
payable for all officers in a pay-Point.
