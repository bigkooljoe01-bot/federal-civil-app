87. What type of control is emphasized for the collection of all revenues under the responsibility
of the Accounting Officer?
A. External control.
B. Internal control.
C. Centralized control.
D. Informal control.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1603(g) specifically highlights an "adequate system of
Internal control".
88. What is the general rule regarding the disbursement of public funds in a self-accounting
unit?
A. Funds can be disbursed at the discretion of the Head of Department.
B. Disbursements must strictly follow existing financial rules and regulations.
C. Disbursements only require verbal approval.
D. Funds can be transferred to private accounts.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1603(a) states the need for strict observance of existing
financial rules and regulations governing the disbursement of public funds.
89. What should detailed accounting instructions issued by the Accounting Officer embrace?
A. Only the Accounting Officer's personal preferences.
B. The general provisions of Financial Regulations and specific directives from higher
authorities.
C. Only intemal policies.
D. Guidelines from external consultants.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1603(h) specifies that detailed accounting instructions
should embrace general provisions of Financial Regulations and directives from the Minister of
Finance or the Accountant-General.
