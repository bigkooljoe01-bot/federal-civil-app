53. What is a primary function of the Civil Service in relation to the Executive Council?
A. To keep the Council's decisions secret.
B. To prepare and present papers and memoranda on behalf of the Minister to the Council.
C. To write the laws for the Council.
D. To manage the Council's budget.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: The Civil Service provides the instruments to administer government at the
highest level, which includes preparing and presenting papers and memoranda on behalf of the
Minister to the Council.
54. What should an officer ensure about papers presented to the Executive Council?
A. That they are based on personal opinion.
B. That they are based on verified facts, figures, and data.
C. That they are as short as possible.
D. That they are as long as possible.
Correct Answer: B..
Explanation: The officer must therefore be very careful with the facts, figures, and data
provided in such papers.
55. What is the purpose of keeping a "Reference" in the top right-hand corner of a letter?
A. To show the personal details of the sender.
B. To show the date of the letter.
C. To uniquely identify the letter for record-keeping.
D. To show the recipient's address.
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: Every letter should bear a reference number, which serves as a unique identifier
for record-keeping purposes.
