79. What type of balance must the Accounting Officer ensure is included in the monthly
accounts (FR 1603(vii))?
A. Daily cash receipts.
B. Opening balance.
C. Closing cash/bank balance.
D. Outstanding commitments.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1603(vii) requires the Accounting Officer to ensure the
closing cash/bank balance is included.
80. What is the role of the Accountant-General in relation to the accounting instructions issued
by the Accounting Officer, according to FR 1603(h)?
A. To ignore them.
B. To issue them independently.
C. To provide specific directives to embrace the general provisions of Financial Regulations.
D. To audit them retroactively.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1603(h) mentions that specific directives from the Minister of
Finance or the Accountant-General should be embraced in the detailed accounting instructions.
81. What is the fundamental principle regarding public funds in a self-accounting unit, as implied
by Chapter 16?
A. They can be used for personal expenses.
B. They must be managed with strict adherence to financial regulations.
C. They are exempt from auditing.
D. They are to be invested without oversight.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The entire chapter emphasizes the Accounting Officer's responsibility to ensure
strict observance of financial rules and regulations governing public funds.
