Section D
Part 1: Financial Regulations: Adjustments
1. Question: What is the primary purpose of adjustments in financial accounting, as described in
Chapter 9?
A. To increase or decrease cash on hand.
B. To make transfers between accounts without actual cash receipt or payment.
C. To record daily petty cash transactions.
D. To prepare annual financial statements for audit.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 901 states: "Adjustments are effected by means of Journal
entries which enable transfers to be made from one account to another without actual receipt or
payment of cash.”
2. Which of the following is an example of a transaction that would typically be handled through
an adjustment?
A. Payment for a new office supply order.
B. Correction of accounting errors arising from misclassifications.
C. Receipt of monthly revenue from a government agency.
D. Disbursement of salaries to staff.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 901 lists "correction of accounting errors arising from
misclassifications” as an example of an adjustment.
3. Which Treasury Form (TF) is primarily used for inter-ministerial transactions?
A. TF 15
B.TF72
C.TF23
D. TF 34
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 903(i) states: "The Adjustment Voucher (TF 23) which is
designed to adjust inter-ministerial transactions is mostly used in - ministries, extra-ministerial
offices and other arms of government.”
4. How many copies of the Adjustment Voucher (TF 23) are typically submitted?
A. One (original)
B. Two (original and duplicate)
C. Three (original and two copies)
D. Four (quadruplicate)
Correct Answer: D.
