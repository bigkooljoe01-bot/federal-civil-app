91. An acting appointment is NOT a form of trial for what purpose?

A. Confirmation of appointment

B. Promotion

C. Probation

D. Transfer

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Rule 020703 explicitly states, "Acting appointment is not intended as a means of

testing the suitability of officers for promotion.”

92. What is the maximum duration of an acting appointment?

A. One month

B. Six months

C. One year

D. Two years

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Rule 020705 states that an acting appointment shall not exceed one year, but in

exceptional circumstances, it can be extended for another one year.

93. What is the effective date of an acting appointment?

A. The date of the gazette notice of the appointment.

B. The date on which the officer takes over substantively the duties and responsibilities of the

post.

C. The date the FCSC is notified.

D. The first day of the month.

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Rule 020705 states that the date on which an acting appointment commences

“will be indicated in the gazette notice authorizing the appointment” and takes effect when the

officer takes over the duties and responsibilities®*.
