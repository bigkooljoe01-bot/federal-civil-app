22. Who is responsible for convening the Annual Board of Survey for a Ministry, Extra-Ministerial
Office, or other arm of government?
A. The Accountant-General.
B. The Head of the Internal Audit Unit.
C. The Accounting Officer.
D. The Auditor-General.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1802 specifies that the Accounting Officer is responsible for
convening the Annual Board of Survey.
23. What is the minimum number of members on a Board of Survey, according to FR 1802?
A. One.
B. Two.
C. Three.
D. Four.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1802 states that the Board shall consist of at least three
members.
24. What must the members of the Board of Survey be, as a requirement for their appointment?
A. They must be from the same department as the officer being surveyed.
B. They must be from different departments or units.
C. They must be senior accounting officers.
D. They must be lawyers.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1802 requires that the members shall be from different
departments or units other than the one whose accounts are being surveyed.

documents?
A. To confiscate them.
B. To sell them at a public auction.
C. To physically count and verify them against the relevant accounting records and registers.
D. To destroy them if they are found to be old.
Correct Answer: C
