18. What is "interdiction"?
A. A permanent dismissal from service.
B. The temporary removal of an officer from performing his normal duties when disciplinary
proceedings for his dismissal are being undertaken.
C. A temporary promotion.
D. A form of leave without pay.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Rule 010105 defines “interdiction” as “the temporary removal of an officer from
performing his normal duties when disciplinary proceedings for his dismissal are being
undertaken or is about to be undertaken, and he shall be placed on half salary pending the
determination of the case.”
19. What is a "junior officer?
A. A new entrant to the service.
B. A pensionable officer on GL. 06 and below and their equivalent.
C. An officer on probation.
D. An officer undergoing training.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Rule 010105 defines "Junior Officer” as "a pensionable officer on GL. 06 and
below and their equivalent.”
20. What are "Junior Posts"?
A. Posts in ministries/extra-ministerial offices attracting emoluments on grade level 06 and
below and their equivalent.
B. Posts for contract staff.
C. Posts for interns.
D. Posts for temporary staff.
Correct Answer: A
Explanation: Rule 010105 defines "Junior Posts" as “posts in Ministries/Extra-Ministerial
Offices attracting emoluments on Grade Level 06 and below and their equivalent.”
21. What does "monthly-rated” employment mean?
A. Employment paid on a daily basis.
B. Employment on monthly rates of pay.
C. Employment paid annually.
D. Employment with performance-based pay.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Rule 010106 defines "monthly-rated" as "employment on monthly rates of pay."
