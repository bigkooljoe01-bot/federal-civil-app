60. Who should be required to explain why they are late to work?
A. All staff who signed below the red line.
B. Only junior staff.
C. Only senior staff.
D. Only staff on Grade Level
Correct Answer: A.
Explanation: Late-comers should be required to explain why they are late. This applies to all
staff who signed below the red line.
61. Why is it important for a Civil Servant to be helpful to others?
A. To gain personal favors.
B. To discourage the unfortunate tendency of people seeking help only through personal
connections.
C. To get a promotion faster.
D. To create a good personal image.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: The unfortunate tendency of people only getting help from those they know
should be discouraged by all Civil Servants.
62. What is a key component of being a "public servant" in terms of kindness?
A. To give people what they are not entitled to.
B. To respect the privileges and rights of officers, employees, and members of the public.
C. To be strict with everyone to show authority.
D. To only be kind to those you know personally.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Kindness involves respecting the privileges and rights of officers, employees and
members of the public, irrespective of the fact that those concerned are unknown to you. A Civil
Servant is a public servant, and should therefore serve impartially.
