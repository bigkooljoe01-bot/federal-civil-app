79. Can a dishonored cheque be held as cash?
A. Yes, temporarily.
B. Yes, with the Accounting Officer's approval.
C. No, under no circumstances.
D. Only if the amount is small.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 732(ii) states: "Under no circumstances may a dishonoured
cheque be held as cash."
80. What is the primary purpose of maintaining a departmental dishonored cheques register?
A. To track bank charges.
B. To ensure clearances are pursued and recoveries affected.
C. To record all incoming and outgoing cheques.
D. To provide a summary of daily bank transactions
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 733(ii) states: "The register shall be checked monthly by the
Head of accounts to ensure that clearances are pursued and recoveries effected.”
