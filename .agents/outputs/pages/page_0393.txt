90. What is the implication of the Due Process policy on projects funded by the government?
A. They must prioritize speed over procedure.
B. They must follow laid-down rules and regulations.
C. They are not subject to any form of oversight.
D. They are awarded based on the discretion of a single individual.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Due Process ensures that government business is carried out on the basis of
relevant and prescribed laws, rules, regulations, and practices.
91. What is the ultimate purpose of the Due Process policy's objective to guarantee the
elimination of waste?
A. To increase government spending
B. To ensure value for money
C. To prolong the duration of projects
D. To increase government bureaucracy
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: The objective to eliminate waste is directly linked to the aim of achieving value for
money.
92. Who is the regulatory authority responsible for setting standards and developing the legal
framework for public procurement in Nigeria?
A. The Head of the Civil Service of the Federation
B. The Bureau of Public Procurement
C. The Accountant-General of the Federation
D. The Auditor-General for the Federation
Correct Answer B
Explanation: The Bureau of Public Procurement is one of the regulatory authorities responsible
for monitoring and oversight, and for setting standards and developing the legal framework for
public procurement.
93. Which of the following is an objective of the Due Process policy related to project
conceptualization?
A. To ensure projects are conceptualized on an ad-hoc basis
B. To ensure projects are disconnected from the annual financial appropriations
C. To ensure that project conceptualization and packaging match the defined priorities and
targets as contained in the annual financial appropriations
D. To allow project conceptualization to be done by external consultants only
Correct Answer: C
