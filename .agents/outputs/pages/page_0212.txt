62. Under what circumstances should payment be made for services not yet performed or

goods not yet supplied?

A. In urgent cases with special approval.

B. If a mobilization fee is required.

C. Under no circumstances.

D. Only if a contract is in place.

services not yet performed or for goods not yet supplied."

64. What happens if a government bank account is overdrawn?

A. The bank will automatically cover the overdraft.

B. The officer responsible shall be made to refund any bank charges incurred thereon.

C. Areport is sent to the Minister of Finance.

D. The account is immediately closed.

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Financial Regulation 710 states: “In the event of an account being overdrawn, the

officer responsible shall be made to refund any bank charges incurred thereon.”
