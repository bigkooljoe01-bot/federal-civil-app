48. Who is personally and peculiarly responsible for the due performance of the financial duties
of their ministries and for any inaccuracies in accounts rendered under their authority?
A. Only the Accountant-General.
B. Only the Auditor-General.
C. All officers.
D. Only the Head of Finance and Accounts.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 124 states, "All officers are personally and peculiarly
responsible for the due performance of the financial duties of their ministries/extra-ministerial
offices and other arms of government.”
49. If an officer delegates or entrusts the performance of duties to a subordinate officer, is the
delegating officer relieved of responsibility?
A. Yes, completely.
B. Yes, partially.
C. No, never.
D. Only if the delegation is in writing.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 125 states, "No officer shall be relieved of his responsibility
should he delegate or entrust the performance of duties or any part thereof to his subordinate
officer.”
50. Who is responsible for causing surprise inspections to be carried out on the accounts of
officers with financial responsibilities?
A. Only Accounting Officers.
B. Only the Auditor-General.
C. The Accountant-General.
D. The Minister of Finance.
Correct Answer: C
