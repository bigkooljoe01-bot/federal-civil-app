67. Where must ministries/extra-ministerial offices that earn revenue in foreign currency pay this
revenue?
A. To the Central Bank of Nigeria
B. To a commercial bank
C. To the foreign treasury
D. To a private foreign account
Correct Answer: A
Explanation: Financial Regulation 213(iii) requires that such revenue must be paid to the
Central Bank of Nigeria without exemption.
68. What is the purpose of the Revenue Collector's Chart?
A. To track the daily activities of all staff
B. To record the number of receipts and licences in use
C. To give a continuous account of the value of receipts and licences
D. To serve as a monthly report of all expenditures
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: According to Financial Regulation 214(i), the chart is to give a continuous account
of the value of receipts, licenses, and other revenue books issued for collections.
69. What should an officer who is neither a Revenue Collector nor a Sub-Accounting Officer do
if they come into possession of public money?
A. Keep it safe until the end of the month
B. Lodge it without delay with a Sub-Accounting Officer
C. Hand it over to their Head of Department
D. Deposit it directly into the ministry's bank account
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 218 states that such an officer will lodge the money without
delay with a Sub-Accounting Officer who will issue an official receipt.
