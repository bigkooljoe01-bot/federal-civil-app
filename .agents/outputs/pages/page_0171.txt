5. When is a Provisional General Warrant issued?
A. At the end of the financial year.
B. When the Appropriation Act has not come into operation at the commencement of the
financial year.
C. For additional, unforeseen expenditure.
D. To transfer funds between sub-heads.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 303 states: "A provisional General Warrant shall be issued
where the Appropriation Act has not come into operation at the commencement of the financial
year."
6. For how long can a Provisional General Warrant authorize the withdrawal of money from the
fund?
A. Not exceeding three months.
B. Not exceeding six months.
C. Not exceeding nine months.
D. For the entire financial year.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 303 specifies "a period not exceeding six months.”
7. To whom is the original copy of a Provisional General Warrant addressed?
A. The Auditor-General.
B. The Minister of Finance.
C. The Accountant-General.
D. The Head of Civil Service.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 303 states: "The original copy of a Provisional General
Warrant is addressed to the Accountant-General with a signed copy forwarded to the
Auditor-General."
8. What does the Annual General Warrant authorize the Accountant-General to do?
A. Only to pay for personal emoluments.
B. To issue funds to pay for personal emoluments and other services provided in the Annual
Estimates.
C. To transfer funds from the Contingencies Fund.
D. To approve all government contracts.
Correct Answer: B
