95. What information should a voucher substitute contain?

A. Only the amount paid.

B. Month and year of account, payment voucher number, classification, amount paid, and

sufficient details to permit identification of the transaction.

C. The name of the payee.

D. The reason for the missing voucher.

Correct Answer: B.

Explanation: Financial Regulation 810 states: "Voucher substitutes will show month and year of

account and will also contain payment voucher number, classification, amount paid and

sufficient details to permit identification of the transaction.”

96. What register must a Sub-Accounting Officer maintain for outstanding vouchers?

A. A general ledger.

B. A register of outstanding vouchers (Treasury Form 73).

C. A payment log.

D. A daily transaction record.

Correct Answer: B.

Explanation: Financial Regulation 811(i) states: "Sub-Accounting Officer shall maintain a

register of outstanding vouchers (Treasury Form 73) which shall be marked off 11S they are

received.”

97. Who is personally responsible for obtaining acquittal for payments out of public funds until

the relevant payment vouchers are obtained?

A. The Accounting Officer.

B. The Auditor-General.

C. The Sub-Accounting Officer.

D. The Head of Accounts.

Correct Answer: C.

Explanation: Financial Regulation 811(ii) states: “It is the personal responsibility of the

Sub-Accounting Officer to obtain acquittal for the payments out of public funds and this

responsibility exists until he has obtained the relevant payment vouchers (See Regulation 621)."
