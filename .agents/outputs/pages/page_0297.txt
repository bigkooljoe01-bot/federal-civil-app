99. What is the process for issuing stores?
A. Verbal requests are sufficient for issuing stores.
B. Stores should only be issued against a properly completed and authorized Store Issue
Voucher (SIV) or other authorized stores requisition forms.
C. The Storekeeper can issue stores at their discretion.
D. Stores are issued only on an annual basis.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 2132 states that no stores shall be issued except on the
authority of a properly completed and authorized Store Issue Voucher (SIV) or other authorized
stores requisition forms.
100. What is a "Store Receipt Voucher" used for?
A. To authorize payment for a purchase.
B. To acknowledge the receipt of goods into a store.
C. To request a new item from a supplier.
D. To record the transfer of a store to another department.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: A Store Receipt Voucher (SRV) is used to acknowledge the receipt of goods into
a store.
