11. Who is the Head of the Federal Government Accounting Services and the Treasury?
A. The Minister of Finance.
B. The Auditor-General for the Federation.
C. The Accountant-General of the Federation.
D. The Head of Finance and Accounts of a ministry.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 106 states, "The Accountant-General of the Federation is the
Head of the Federal Government Accounting Services and the Treasury.”
12. What is a key responsibility of the Accountant-General of the Federation regarding
accounting systems and controls?
A. To personally manage all financial transactions.
B. To provide adequate accounting systems and controls in all government arms.
C. To audit all public accounts annually.
D. To formulate national economic policy.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 106 states the Accountant-General "has the responsibility for
providing adequate accounting systems and controls in the ministries, extra-ministerial offices,
and other arms of Government.”
13. Which of the following is a duty of the Accountant-General of the Federation?
A. To serve as the Chief Accounting Officer for receipts and payments of the government.
B. To supervise accounts of federal ministries.
C. To collate, prepare, and publish statutory financial statements.
D. All of the above.
Correct Answer: D
Explanation: Financial Regulation 107 lists all these as duties of the Accountant-General.

A. Consolidated Revenue Fund, Development Fund, and Contingencies Fund.
B. Only the Federation Account.
C. Only the Consolidated Revenue Fund.
D. Private bank accounts of public officers.
