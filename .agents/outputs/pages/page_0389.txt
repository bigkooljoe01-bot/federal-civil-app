74. What does the Bureau do to ensure compliance with the principles of due process?
A. It holds regular meetings with the private sector.
B. It reviews and certifies Federal Government contracts.
C. It lobbies the National Assembly for more funding.
D. It prosecutes all cases of contract inflation.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: The Bureau reviews and certifies Federal Government contracts according to
established thresholds to ensure compliance with due process principles.
75. According to the fundamental principles for procurements, no procurement proceedings
shall be formalized until the procuring entity has done what?
A. Paid all contractors in full
B. Ensured that funds are available to meet the obligations
C. Secured approval from the Minister of Finance
D. Received a No Objection’ certificate from the President
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: No procurement proceedings shall be formalized until the procuring entity has
ensured that funds are available to meet the obligations.
76. What must a procuring entity obtain from the Bureau before a contract can be awarded,
subject to the regulations?
A. Aletter of intent
B. A "No Objection’ to Contract Award certificate
C. A fiscal strategy paper
D. A signed agreement from all bidders
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: The procuring entity must obtain a certificate of ‘No Objection’ to Contract Award
from the Bureau.
77. The Public Procurement Act requires all public procurement to be conducted using what
bidding method?
A. Selective bidding
B. Restricted bidding
C. Open competitive bidding
D. Direct procurement
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: All public procurement should be conducted by open competitive bidding.
