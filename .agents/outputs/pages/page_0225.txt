13. What types of transfers are effected by Principal Journal Vouchers (PJVs) at the end of the
month?
A. Transfers from "Below-the-line Accounts” to "Above-the-line Accounts.”
B. Transfers from "Above-the-line Accounts” to "Below-the-line Accounts.”
C. Transfers between different ministries.
D. Transfers related to revenue collection only.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 904(ii)(c) states that PJVs are prepared to "effect month-end
transfer of accounts from Above-the-line Accounts to Below-the-line Accounts.”
14. What is the purpose of Supplementary Journal Vouchers (SJVs) in the OAGF?
A. To adjust differences between ministry transcripts and bank transfer statements, and to
re-classify accounts before the Trial Balance.
B. To correct misclassifications and effect annual transfers.
C. To record all incoming and outgoing cash.
D. To manage unallocated stores
Correct Answer: A.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 904(iii) states SJVs are prepared "to adjust differences
between ministry transcript's figures and those contained in the Bank Transfer Statements
prepared by the Sub- Treasurer of the Federation; and to re-classify accounts before the Trial
Balance is prepared.”
15. When an adjustment voucher is accepted by a ministry, extra-ministerial office, or other arm
of government, what is returned as evidence of acceptance or rejection?
A. A copy of the original memo.
B. One of the fully signed adjustment vouchers.
C. A verbal confirmation.
D. A new Treasury Form 23.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 903(iii) states: “The ministry, extra-ministerial office or other
ams of government which is receiving the adjustment voucher shall stamp and return one of
the fully signed vouchers as evidence of acceptance or rejection.”
