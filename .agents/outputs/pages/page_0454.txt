35. What is the purpose of classifying documents?
A. To make them difficult to access.
B. To sensitize officers about the degree of care required for each document.
C. To hide their contents from other Ministries.
D. To prevent the public from ever seeing them.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Classified documents are duly classified so as to sensitize officers about the
degree of care required for each.
36. What is the lowest level of classification for an official document?
A. RESTRICTED.
B. CONFIDENTIAL.
C. SECRET.
D. TOP SECRET.
Correct Answer: A.
Explanation: The various classification ratings in descending order of security are TOP
SECRET, SECRET, CONFIDENTIAL, and RESTRICTED.
37. What is required for an officer to handle "TOP SECRET" documents?
A. They must be a senior officer.
B. They must have a special security clearance.
C. They must be a member of the armed forces.
D. They must be a new hire.
Correct Answer:B.
Explanation: Only officers with the requisite security clearance, who have been instructed to
handle Top Secret documents, may do so.
38. What is the best way to destroy classified documents that are no longer needed?
A. Tear them up and throw them in the waste bin.
B. Shred them or burn them.
C. Leave them on a desk for others to see.
D. Put them in the Open Registry.
Correct Answer: B.
