36. What is the purpose of the Executive Council of the Federation?
A. To make all the laws.
B. To determine Government policies on various matters.
C. To manage all the finances
D. To hire all government staff.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: The Executive Council of the Federation... has the duty and responsibility of
determining Government policies on various matters.
37. What is the role of a Civil Servant regarding Executive Council Papers?
A. To make a policy pronouncement without approval.
B. To provide the political leadership with instruments to administer government at the highest
level.
C. To keep them confidential from their superiors.
D. To discard them.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: A key purpose of the Civil Service is to provide the political leadership with the
instruments with which to administer Government at the highest level, i.e. the Executive Council
of the Federation.
38. What should officers do regarding Executive Council papers?
A. Treat them as unimportant.
B. Be very careful with the facts, figures, and data provided.
C. Ignore them unless they are directly addressed.
D. Not be concerned with them at all.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: The officer must therefore be very careful with the facts, figures, and data
provided in such papers because the political leadership would be using them to make policies.
