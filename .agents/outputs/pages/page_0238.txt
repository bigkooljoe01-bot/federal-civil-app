64. What is required for the safe custody of all bonds, agreements, and other valuable
documents?
A. They can be kept in a regular office drawer.
B. They must be kept in a strong-room or safe, with a register maintained.
C. They can be stored off-site.
D. They only need to be scanned and stored digitally.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1123 mandates that "All bonds, agreements and other
valuable documents shall be kept in a strong-room or safe, and a register shall be maintained
for all such documents deposited.”
65. When an officer is transferred, what must happen with the keys and contents of safes or
strong-rooms under their charge?
A. The keys can be left with a junior officer.
B. The keys and contents must be formally handed over to the relieving officer, and a certificate
of handing-over obtained.
C. The keys can be mailed to the new officer.
D. The contents can be disposed of.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1124 requires that "When an officer is transferred, the keys
of any safe or strong-room under his charge, and the contents thereof, must be formally handed
over to the relieving officer, and a certificate of handing-over obtained.”
66. What is the responsibility of the relieving officer during a handover of keys and contents?
A. To accept without verification.
B. To verify the contents and sign the certificate of handing-over.
C. To report any discrepancies later.
D. To discard old records.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1125 states, "The relieving officer must satisfy himself that
the contents are correct, and will sign the certificate of handing-over.”
