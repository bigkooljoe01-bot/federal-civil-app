7. To whom do the provisions of the Public Service Rules (PSR) apply?
A. Only to the Office of the Head of the Civil Service of the Federation.
B. Strictly to all ministries, extra-ministerial offices, and agencies.
C. Only to officers on grade level 17 and above.
D. To all employees in the private sector.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Rule 010101 states, "The provisions of the Public Service Rules (PSR) shall be
strictly enforced by all Ministries, Extra-Ministerial Offices, and Agencies.”
8. The Public Service Rules apply to all officers in the Public Service except where they conflict
with specific terms approved by the Federal Government and written into the contract of
employment or letters of appointment. This indicates that:
A. The PSR is always supreme.
B. Specific contract terms can override the PSR.
C. The PSR is a suggestion, not a rule.
D. Only the Head of the Civil Service can grant exceptions.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Rule 010101 states that the rules apply “except where they conflict with specific
terms approved by the Federal Government and written into the contract of employment or
letters of appointment.”
9. What is the duty of every officer regarding the Public Service Rules and other extant
regulations and circulars?
A. To ignore them if inconvenient.
B. To acquaint himself with them.
C. To delegate the responsibility to subordinates.
D. To challenge them if they disagree.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Rule 010103 states, "It shall be the duty of every officer to acquaint himself with
the Public Service Rules and other extant regulations and circulars.”
10. How are the chapters, sections, and rules numbered in the PSR?
A. Randomly.
B. Each rule has a six-digit number, with the first two indicating the chapter, the next two the
section, and the last two the rule number.
C. Sequentially from 1 to 100.
D. Based on the year of amendment.
Correct Answer: B
