5. A Permanent Secretary, as an Accounting Officer, is expected to respond to audit queries

within what timeframe?

A. One week

B. 30 days

C. One month

D. 21 days

Correct Answer: D.

Explanation: The accounting officer has a period of 21 days to respond to an audit query.

6. Which document is the primary basis for the scrutiny of government accounts by the Public

Accounts Committees?

A. The Appropriation Act

B. The Finance (Control and Management) Act

C. The Annual Report of the Auditor-General for the Federation

D. The Civil Service Handbook

Correct Answer: C.

Explanation: The committees rely on the annual report of the Auditor-General for the

Federation to scrutinize the accounts of MDAs.

7. What is the consequence for an Accounting Officer who fails to appear before the Public

Accounts Committee when summoned?

A. A simple warming

B. Suspension from duty

C. A penalty of two years' imprisonment

D. The committee can recommend sanctions, including dismissal

Correct Answer: D.

Explanation: Failure of an accounting officer to appear before the committee when summoned

is a serious offense, and the committee can recommend sanctions, including dismissal.
