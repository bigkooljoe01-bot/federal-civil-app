Part 5: Financial Regulations: Stores Accounting and Custody
91. What is the primary purpose of Chapter 21 of the Financial Regulations book?
A. To outline procedures for bank reconciliation.
B. To provide guidelines for stores accounting and custody.
C. To define the roles of internal auditors.
D. To manage pension schemes.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Chapter 21 is titled "Stores Accounting and Custody,” indicating its primary
purpose is to provide guidelines on this subject.
92. According to Financial Regulation 2101, what must Accounting Officers do to ensure proper
control over government stores?
A. Keep all stores in their personal custody.
B. Employ a system of stores accounting and control that provides an efficient check on all
receipts, issues, and balances.
C/ Delegate all store management to a single junior officer.
D. Sell all excess stores.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 2101 mandates that Accounting Officers employ a system of
stores accounting and control to provide an efficient check on all transactions and balances.
93. What is the key document used to record the receipt of stores?
A. A payment voucher.
B. A Store Receipt Voucher (SRV) (Treasury Form 14).
C. A bank statement.
D. A staff payroll record.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The Store Receipt Voucher (SRV) (Treasury Form 14) is the key document used
to record the receipt of stores.
