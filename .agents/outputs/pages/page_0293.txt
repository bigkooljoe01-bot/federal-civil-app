83. What should be done with government vehicles when not in use?
A. Parked anywhere convenient.
B. Parked in designated government garages or secure premises.
C. Used for personal errands.
D. Left unlocked.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: While not explicitly stated in a single regulation in this chapter, the emphasis on
proper control and accountability (FR 2002) and security of government assets (related general
regulations) implies secure parking.
84. What is the main objective of strict control over government vehicles?
A. To limit their use.
B. To prevent abuse, ensure efficiency, and safeguard public resources.
C. To generate revenue.
D. To make them available for private hire.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The regulations consistently emphasize efficient and economical use, prevention
of abuse, and proper accounting, all aimed at safeguarding public resources.
85. What is the immediate step a driver must take if involved in an accident causing injury or
death?
A. Drive away from the scene.
B. Report it to the nearest police station immediately.
C. Call their family.
D. Attempt to settle with the injured party.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 2014 mentions obtaining police reports for all accidents,
implying immediate reporting, especially in severe cases.
