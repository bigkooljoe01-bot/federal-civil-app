75. What is the Accounting Officer responsible for regarding the preparation and defense of
annual budget proposals?
A. Delegation to junior staff.
B. Personal compilation and defense under their supervision.
C. Approval only.
D. Ignoring the process.
Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1603(i) states that the Accounting Officer is responsible for
compiling and defending the annual budget proposals under his supervision.
