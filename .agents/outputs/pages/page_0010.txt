Part 2: Definitions
11. What does "Accredited Representative of the Federal Republic of Nigeria” mean?
A. Any Nigerian citizen living abroad.
B. The Diplomatic or Consular Representative of the Federal Republic of Nigeria within the
area.
C. A foreign diplomat in Nigeria.
D. A trade envoy.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Rule 010105 defines "Accredited Representative of the Federal Republic of
Nigeria" as "the Diplomatic or Consular Representative of the Federal Republic of Nigeria within
the area.”
12. What does "Civil Service of the Federation" mean?
A. Service in any government agency.
B. Service of the Federation in the civil capacity as a staff of the Office of the President, Vice
President, a Ministry, and Extra Ministerial Offices of the Government of the Federation.
C. Service in the military.
D. Service in the private sector.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Rule 010105 defines "Civil Service of the Federation" as "Service of the
Federation in the civil capacity as a staff of the Office of the President, Vice President, a
Ministry, and Extra Ministerial Offices of the Government of the Federation assigned with the
responsibility for any business of the Government of the Federation.”
13. "Classified Correspondence” means correspondence that has been graded:
A. Public, Open, or General.
B. Restricted, Confidential, Secret, or Top Secret.
C. Internal, External, or Private.
D. Unrestricted, Accessible, or Common.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Rule 010105 defines "Classified Correspondence” as “correspondence that has
been graded Restricted, Confidential, Secret, or Top Secret.”
