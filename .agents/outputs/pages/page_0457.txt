stored?
A. Only the Permanent Secretary.
B. Only the Head of the Civil Service.

50. What is the purpose of a *bring-up® pad?
A. To store files that are not needed.
B. To keep a list of all meetings.
C. To store a file that requires action at a future date.
D. To keep a list of all staff.
Correct Answer: C.
Explanation: When an officer has finished with a file, he should ensure that it is returned to the
registry. If a file is required at a later date, it should be put on a "bring-up” pad.
