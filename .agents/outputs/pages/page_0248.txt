Section E
Part 1: Financial Regulations: Deposits
1. According to Financial Regulation 1301, under what classification shall deposits be
accounted for?
A. Above-the-line classification
B. Below-the-line classification
C. Consolidated Revenue Fund
D. Development Fund
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1301 specifies that deposits shall be accounted for under the
“"Below-the-line classification" list issued by the Accountant-General.
2. Which of the following may NOT be placed on deposit, according to Financial Regulation
1302?
A. Unclaimed salaries
B. Security for a contract
C. A court-ordered payment
D. A refundable payment for a service
Correct Answer: A
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1302 explicitly states that "unclaimed salaries wages,
unspent balances of allocation, sum due to Merchants or Contractors or any form of revenue
shall not be placed on deposit."
3. Who is responsible for maintaining a Deposit register?
A. The Accountant-General
B. The Accounting Officer
C. The Sub-Accountant authorized to record deposit transactions
D. The Head of Finance and Accounts
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1304 mandates that a Deposit register shall be maintained
by the Sub-Accountant authorized to record all transactions in respect of the deposits.

Regulation 13052
A. The name of the person on whose behalf the deposit is made and the date
B. The name of the depositor, the nature of the transaction, and the necessity for the deposit
C. The amount of the deposit and the signature of the cashier
D. The account number of the depositor and the Head of Accounts’ approval
