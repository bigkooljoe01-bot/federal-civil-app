Financial Regulations: Receipt and Licence Books
71. Who is responsible for the general control of all Treasury and other receipt and licence
books?
A. The Auditor-General.
B. The Accountant-General.
C. The Minister of Finance.
D. The Head of Civil Service.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1201 states that the Accountant-General is responsible for
the general control of all Treasury and other receipt and licence books.
72. Who is responsible for the printing of all Treasury and other receipt and licence books?
A. The Federal Government Printer.
B. The Ministry of Finance.
C. The Accountant-General.
D. The Central Bank of Nigeria.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1202 specifies that the Federal Government Printer is
responsible for printing these books.
73. What information must be printed on all receipt and licence books?
A. Only the title of the book.
B. The title of the book, the serial number of each receiptlicence, and the number of the book.
C. The date of printing.
D. The name of the issuing officer.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1203 requires that the title of the book, the serial number of
each receiptlicence, and the number of the book must be printed on all receipt and licence
books.
74. What is the procedure for dealing with any defects found in receipt or licence books upon
receipt from the Federal Government Printer?
A. The books should be used as is.
B. The books should be immediately returned to the printer.
C. The defects must be reported immediately to the Accountant-General and the
Auditor-General.
D. The defects should be noted for future reference.
Correct Answer: C.
