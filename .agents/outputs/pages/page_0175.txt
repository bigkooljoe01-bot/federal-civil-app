20. What is the limit of authority conveyed by Recurrent Expenditure Warrants to officers
controlling votes?
A. Unlimited, as long as funds are available.
B. Limited to the amounts provided under each sub-head in the Approved Estimates and
Supplementary Estimates.
C. Subject to the discretion of the Accounting Officer.
D. Only for personal emoluments
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 313 states: “The authority conveyed... is limited to the
amounts provided under each sub-head in the Approved Estimates and Supplementary
Estimates.”
21. Can an unspent balance on a recurrent expenditure vote be revoted for the next financial
year?
A. Yes, if approved by the Minister of Finance.
B. Yes, if approved by the National Assembly.
C. No, it cannot be revoted.
D. Only for capital projects.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 314 states: "An unspent balance on a recurrent. expenditure
vote cannot be revoted.”
22. To whom should applications for supplementary provision be submitted?
A. Only to the Accountant-General.
B. To the Minister of Finance.
C. To the Auditor-General.
D. To the Head of Civil Service.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 315 states: “applications for supplementary provision should
be submitted to the Minister of Finance...”
23. When must applications for virement relating to grading and establishment be submitted and
approved?
A. Not later than the end of the financial year.
B. Not later than the first quarter of the Financial Year.
C. Not later than the tenth month of the budget year.
D. At any time during the financial year.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 316(i) states: "Such application shall be made and approval
obtained not later than first quarter of the Financial Year."
