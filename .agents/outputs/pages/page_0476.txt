26. A good minute should be what?
A. As verbose as possible.
B. A faithful and accurate summary of what transpired at the meeting.
C. A reflection of the secretary's personal opinions.
D. A list of all the jokes told at the meeting.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: A good minute should be a faithful and accurate summary of what transpired at
the meeting.
27. The minutes must be written and circulated to all members as soon as possible after the
meeting. What is the reason for this?
A. To prevent the members from forgetting what was discussed.
B. To ensure that the decisions are fresh in the minds of the members.
C. To show that the secretary is a fast writer.
D. To make the meeting look more important.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: The minutes should be circulated as soon as possible after the meeting to ensure
that the decisions are fresh in the minds of the members.
28. What is the purpose of having a "Matter Arising” section in the minutes of a meeting?
A. To discuss new business.
B. To review the decisions and actions taken from the previous meeting82.
C. To discuss personal issues.
D. To debate old decisions.
Correct Answer: B.
Explanation: The "Matters Arising" section is used to review the decisions and actions taken
from the previous meeting.
