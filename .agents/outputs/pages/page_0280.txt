30. Who receives the three copies of the Board of Survey report?
A. The Accounting Officer, the Accountant-General, and the Auditor-General.
B. The Accounting Officer, the Head of the Internal Audit Unit, and the Minister of Finance.
C. The Accounting Officer, the Head of Department, and the officer-in-charge.
D. The Auditor-General, the Head of Internal Audit, and the Head of Department.
Correct Answer: A
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1807 directs that one copy of the report goes to the
Accounting Officer, one to the Accountant-General, and one to the Auditor-General.
31. What is the purpose of a "surprise" Board of Survey?
A. To give notice to the officer-in-charge.
8 To check for discrepancies without prior warning.
C. To replace the annual survey.
D. To only be conducted when there is a known shortage.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1804 allows for the convening of "surprise" Boards of Survey
to check cash, stamps, and other security documents without prior warming.
32. What action should be taken if an Accounting Officer in a field headquarters office is absent
when a surprise Board of Survey is convened?
A. The survey must be postponed until the officer returns.
B. The survey can proceed without them.
C. The next most senior officer shall act in his place.
D. The Board must be dissolved.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Financial Regulation 1804 states that if the Accounting Officer is absent, the next
most senior officer shall be required to act in his place for the purposes of the survey.
33. What is the responsibility of the members of the Board of Survey regarding the report?
A. They are not responsible for the contents of the report.
B. They are jointly responsible for the accuracy of the report.
C. Only the Chairman is responsible for the report's accuracy.
D. They are only responsible for the physical counting.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The fact that the report must be signed by all members implies that they are jointly
responsible for its accuracy.
