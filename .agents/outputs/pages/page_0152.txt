31. Which of the following is a function of the Accounting Officer?
A. Ensuring proper budgetary and accounting systems are established.
B. Ensuring the safety and proper maintenance of all government assets.
C. Ensuring accurate collection and accounting for public moneys.
D. All of the above.
Correct Answer: D
Explanation: Financial Regulation 112(1) lists all these as functions of the Accounting Officer.
32. An Accounting Officer is responsible for ensuring that any revenue collected is:
A. Spent immediately on ministry needs.
B. Remitted to the appropriate authorities promptly and not spent.
C. Used to cover immediate operational expenses.
D. Invested in short-term deposits.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 112(I)(1) states the Accounting Officer is responsible for
“ensuring that any revenue collected is not spent but remitted to the appropriate authorities
promptly.”
33. In compliance with the Public Procurement Act, Accounting Officers are charged with the
responsibility of:
A. Presiding over the activities of their Tenders Boards.
B. Ensuring adequate appropriation for procurements in their annual budget.
C. Integrating their entity's procurement expenditure into its yearly budget.
D. All of the above.
Correct Answer: D
Explanation: Financial Regulation 113 lists all these as special responsibilities of Accounting
Officers under the Public Procurement Act.
34. If an Accounting Officer fails to comply with the provisions of the Public Procurement Act,
who shall be held personally liable for any breach or contravention?
A. Only the subordinates who caused the breach.
B. The Accounting Officer, whether or not caused by them in person or their subordinates.
C. The Minister of Finance.
D. The Bureau of Public Procurement.
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Financial Regulation 113(i) states the Accounting Officer "shall be personally
liable for any breach or contravention thereof, whether or not such breach or contravention was
caused by them in person, their subordinates, or any person to whom they may have delegated
their responsibilities.”
35. What is one of the duties of the Head of Finance and Accounts of a ministry?
A. To approve all government contracts.
